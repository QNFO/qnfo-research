## Phase 1 — The claim
1. **What is the claim about?** The cognitive-epistemic basis of representation choice in
   mature technical fields, and the design consequence (two-layer structure; meta-design).
2. **What does it claim precisely?** Convergence + outsourcing + the design principle, as
   stated. It does NOT claim diagrams are mathematically deeper, nor that machines cannot
   explain.
3. **What is at stake?** Whether legibility is a design constraint or an accident; whether the
   QNFO programs' diagrammatic investments are ergonomically grounded.
