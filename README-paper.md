## Overview

A finite-entropy world is a finite-distinction world: uncountable precision is
unphysical — a single real coordinate requires infinitely many yes/no distinctions —
while computable depth and p-adic valuation remain physically real. The state-space
geometry of finite distinctions is combinatorial and ultrametric; read as
thermodynamics, quantum mechanics is a stochastic thermodynamics of finite
alternatives, in which unitary evolution and superposition emerge as the
large-distinction limit of an entropy-Hessian gradient flow — supported at finite N
within the per-distinction model (the physical status of the per-distinction rate
structure is open). The Hilbert-space formulation over the complex numbers is that
limit — a map, not the territory. All quantitative claims are verified in code
(6/6 checks, seed 20260821, deposited source); the emergence claims remain
conjecture-grade inside the locked record.
