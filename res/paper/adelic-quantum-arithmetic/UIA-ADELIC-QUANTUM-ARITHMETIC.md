Q2 (What is already known?): Ostrowski's classification of places [established]; p-adic and adelic quantum mechanics as a research field (Dragovich; Zúñiga-Galindo) [established]; the Bose–Einstein/Fermi–Dirac multiplicity-rule identifications [proved, RES.027].

## Phase 2 — The structure of the not-known

Q4 (What is explicitly unknown?): Whether any p-adic place couples to measurement beyond the published nulls; whether any practitioner-measurable quantity discriminates adelic from Archimedean QM at accessible scales.
