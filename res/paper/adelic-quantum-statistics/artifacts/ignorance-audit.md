# Universal Ignorance Audit — QNFO.RES.027 (ZENODO-INQUIRY-1)

- **Target:** the locked core claim T1–T3 of `adelic-quantum-statistics` (2026-08-27): squarefree → Fermi–Dirac, unrestricted → Bose–Einstein (T1); γ = 1/N from bath degeneracy (T2); symplectic emergence as the large-N Fermi limit (T3).
- **Protocol:** 5 phases / 15 questions (10.5281/zenodo.21901984, 10.5281/zenodo.21901983). Answers written down; NOT resolved during Phases 1–4; silence after Q14; Q15 seeds the next pass.

---

**Q1 — Hidden assumptions and scaffolds.** (a) The "distinction reservoir" itself is carried over from RES.021 as a scaffold; (b) "the product formula CONSTRAINS multiplicities" — the word *constrains* does unexamined work: the formula normalizes, and whether normalization can dictate occupation structure is exactly the claim; (c) identifying squarefree with exclusion assumes the integer lattice is the right carrier of occupation, not merely a convenient one; (d) the four-paper-sequence framing ("final missing tier") predisposes the derivation to complete an arc rather than test a hypothesis.

**Q2 — Map mistaken for territory.** The integers-as-bath is a map; the claim would substitute map for territory if it asserted physical particles ARE prime factors. The bridge from arithmetic to statistics is the named mapping hypothesis, not the territory.

**Q3 — The wobble.** Bose–Einstein condensation has no obvious squarefree shadow; and the N-fold degeneracy cancellation assumes indistinguishable alternatives, while the finite-distinction reservoir's alternatives are by construction distinct — where does indistinguishability enter? That seam is the tension.

**Q4 — Inversion.** What if the orientation is reversed — unrestricted gives Fermi, squarefree gives Bose? The assignment's direction must be derived, not stipulated, or T1 is a convention.

**Q5 — A world where this is false.** A world where statistics derive from spin-statistics/braid topology alone and the adelic derivation is a redundant re-encoding: the paper becomes a dictionary, not a derivation. For fermions that world is plausibly this one — spin-statistics already explains them. The honest status may be explanatory re-encoding, not new constraint.

**Q6 — Invariant under frame change.** The dichotomy (exclusive vs. unrestricted occupation) is the invariant; the carrier (squarefree integers) may be one realization among many. Keep the dichotomy, test the carrier.

**Q7 — A radically different observer.** A number theorist sees a partition identity; a physicist sees statistics; a QEC engineer sees erasure costs. The paper's value depends on the bridge surviving all three readings — the crosswalk must be exact, not evocative.

**Q8 — Who benefits; who is silenced.** The framing benefits the sequence (completes the arc) and the JPCUB mission (derives the assumed rate). Silenced: the spin-statistics literature (RES.009/RES.010), whose causal story is displaced if the adelic derivation claims primacy. F4 must arbitrate, not the arc.

**Q9 — The most dangerous question.** *Is this a derivation or a dictionary?* If the mapping is stipulated, T1–T3 are definitions dressed as theorems — the exact failure mode flagged in the July poster review ("rigor masking absence of empirical content"). F1–F3 exist because of this question.

**Q10 — Where the ignorance lives in the body.** The pull to declare the arc complete; impatience with the ugly open problem of parastatistical intermediates. The feeling signals the temptation to publish the closure rather than the test.

**Q11 — Known but pretending not to know.** The primon gas already made the squarefree/unrestricted identification in the 1990s. The novel delta is narrow (product-formula normalization + γ derivation + symplectic link). Pretending otherwise is F4 bait.

**Q12 — What the unknown wants.** A falsifiable prediction beyond reproduction — the parastatistical-intermediate open problem (partial exclusions → intermediate statistics) is the only thing that makes the mapping earn its keep.

**Q13 — Actionable now, without resolving.** Run F1–F3 verification scripts immediately (reproduction first, prediction after); complete the corpus sweep and the Riemann-gas literature check before drafting a single section.

**Q14 — The gift in the not-knowing.** The clarity that statistics may be a property of multiplicity constraints rather than particle type — a perspective worth keeping even if every theorem fails.
