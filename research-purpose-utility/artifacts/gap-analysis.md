Phase 1 Due Diligence · 2026-08-16 · Evidence: artifacts/external-search/evidence-2026-08-16.md

## 1. What exists already (corpus + external)

**Direct corpus predecessor:** `so-what-of-knowledge` (QNFO Research, 2025-11, no DOI) argues the knowledge paradox: if the universe is self-sufficient, human knowledge must serve purpose beyond comprehension; knowledge should enable action, not just description; math is a model not reality (Wigner); Gödelian incompleteness makes "theory of everything" a pedantic academic exercise without a target objective. **This is the philosophical seed of the user's mandate — RES.012 must cite it as nearest prior art and NOT duplicate it.**

**Supporting corpus critiques:**
- `two-faced-scientific-methodology` (2025-01): theoretical attractor states resist falsification via systemic incentives (funding, peer review, publication) — supports premise L1 (institutional silos produce low-utility research).
- `meta-pattern-of-reification-in-physics` (10.5281/zenodo.19605445): mistaking formalisms for reality → "misallocation of intellectual resources" — supports the abstraction-for-its-own-sake anti-pattern.
- `paper-institutional-reform` (10.5281/zenodo.21299211): prestige cascade, VC-mismatch, proposes five reforms incl. time-bound falsification registries — an operational precedent for G3-style gates.
- `quantifying-structural-unification-and-epistemic-scaffolding` (10.5281/zenodo.17229528): epistemic scaffolding — supports premise-depth (L2) operationalization.
- `joules-per-solution-metric` (10.5281/zenodo.21637028) + JPCUB landscape: a POSITIVE corpus example of utility-grounded research (metric → procurement → benchmark) — the grounding-path ideal case.
- `consilience-framework` / `ultrametric-consilience-atlas` / `consilient-gap-synthesis`: cross-domain synthesis precedents with silo-cost tables (RES.009: Laws-of-Form 1969 → NEVER-connected SILO-FAILURE >50yr).

**External anchors (science of science):**

- AI pipelines amplify silo-fodder: Chen arXiv:2505.12039 (AI-driven SoS); Hajkowicz arXiv:2306.09145 (AI across 333 fields).

## 2. The GAP (what none of these supply)

1. **No operational criterion.** so-what-of-knowledge argues WHY knowledge needs purpose; nobody in the swept set proposes a TESTABLE program-level criterion ("live, non-vacuous grounding path + premise-depth disclosure + no-silo-legitimacy") with a falsification design (F1-F3) and an audit procedure.
2. **No premise-depth disclosure standard.** The corpus has scaffolding language (17229528) but no operational L0..Ln declaration standard binding at publication time (SO-WHAT-GATE-1 / research v2.114 gestures at it; no paper operationalizes it as a requirement).
3. **No anti-pattern taxonomy for research programs AND AI pipelines** (silo-lock, abstraction-for-its-own-sake, utility-deferral-without-path, premise-concealment) with detection criteria.
4. **No application of the criterion to AI-assisted research pipelines** (the QNFO pipeline itself) — the epistemic-legibility angle (IAPS 10.5281/zenodo.21901983) is adjacent but does not give the pipeline a utility-gate.
5. **Falsifiability of the "silo causes low utility" claim is unexplored** in the corpus; external SoS gives measurement machinery but no criterion-level falsification design.

## 3. Premise-chain depth (per G2 + SO-WHAT-GATE-1)

L0 value primitive (utility = legitimate first-order criterion) → L1 empirical premise (silos causally produce low-traceable-utility research; falsifiable via traceable-impact audit) → L2 epistemic premise (premise-depth disclosure increases testability; falsifiable + meta-test F3) → L3 derived (criterion, procedure, taxonomy) → L4 bounded (program-level, exploratory OK with open path). Depth claim: as deep as L0-L2, nothing more. Canonical depth-map discipline from RES.011 (configuration-space-topology).

## 4. Novelty claim (bounded by premise depth)

**NEW:** operational grounding criterion + falsification design + anti-pattern taxonomy + pipeline implementation procedure. **NOT claimed:** proof that utility maximization is derivable from first principles; that all abstraction is harmful; that per-result utility is required. Direct continuity from so-what-of-knowledge (cite, extend, operationalize — not duplicate).
