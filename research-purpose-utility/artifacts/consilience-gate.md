## 3. Synthesis

The six silo-cost rows cohere into the paper's core empirical claim (L1): **siloed, abstraction-only research programs systematically under-produce traceable utility, and the institutional metrics used inside silos (citations, prestige) are precisely the proxies that fail to detect the loss.** The criterion (G1-G3) is the convergent synthesis across the five lexicon domains: a research program's advancement claim must exhibit a live grounding path (economics/SoS operationalization), declare premise depth (philosophy/methodology operationalization), and refuse silo legitimacy (governance/policy operationalization) — while the AI-governance column supplies the pipeline-amplification warning (Chen 2505.12039; Hajkowicz 2306.09145) that motivates the implementation procedure.
