# Core Claim (Locked — QNFO.RES.012 §1.2, P6 Gate)

**Status:** LOCKED 2026-08-16 (Phase 0). Any reformulation requires a new P6 gate.

> **A research program's claim to advancement should be evaluated by a grounding criterion: (G1) it must exhibit a live, non-vacuous path from its questions to real-world utility (practical application, decision-relevant knowledge, or a concrete artifact), with the path traceable and the utility asserted in falsifiable form; (G2) it must declare where its premises end (derived claims vs. unanalyzable primitives or named imported inputs); and (G3) it must not derive legitimacy solely from membership in an academic discipline, abstraction level, or knowledge silo. Exploratory research remains valuable iff it maintains an open grounding path (a stated candidate route to utility). The criterion is program-level and directional, not per-result.**

## Premise chain (depth-of-premises disclosure, per G2 of the mandate)

- **L0 — value primitive (unanalyzable):** real-world utility is a legitimate first-order criterion for allocating research effort.
- **L1 — empirical premise (named input):** discipline-membership, abstraction level, and knowledge silos causally produce low-traceable-utility research at scale. Falsifiable (F1).
- **L2 — epistemic premise (named input):** premise-depth disclosure increases testability, accountability, and novelty-claim credibility. Falsifiable (F2 + meta-test F3).
- **L3 — derived:** the grounding criterion, its application procedure, the anti-pattern taxonomy.
- **L4 — derived, bounded:** program-level scope; exploratory research with an OPEN grounding path remains valuable.

**Claimed depth:** as deep as L0-L2. Explicitly NOT claiming: utility-maximization from first principles; all abstraction is bad; per-result utility is required.

## Falsification conditions (standalone)

- **F1:** traceable-impact audit shows siloed/abstraction-only programs produce utility at rates statistically indistinguishable from grounded programs → L1 fails.
- **F2:** requiring premise-depth disclosure measurably reduces output quality/utility → L2 fails.
- **F3 (self-siloing meta-test):** applying the criterion to itself reduces total utility (checklist ritual crowding out exploration) → criterion is self-refuting, must be revised/withdrawn.
