# The Purpose Principle: Grounding Research in Reality, Utility, and Premise-Depth

**QNFO.RES.012** · Rowan Brad Quni-Gudzinas · 2026-08-16 · Zenodo preprint (draft)

## Why a reader should care

Most research effort is allocated by disciplinary prestige and publication counts — neither measures whether the work has any traceable connection to a human problem. This paper proposes the *grounding criterion*: a research program's claim to advancement should be evaluated by whether it (G1) exhibits a live, non-vacuous path to real-world utility, (G2) declares where its premises end, and (G3) does not derive legitimacy solely from a discipline, abstraction level, or knowledge silo. Exploratory research remains valuable iff it maintains an open grounding path. Falsification conditions F1–F3 are named; the criterion is as deep as its three premises (L0–L2), no deeper.
