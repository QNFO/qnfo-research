## Primary claim [TERRITORY — established]

In topologically ordered (gapped-bulk) phases — Z₂ topological insulators and quantum-Hall systems — fermionic transport is **confined to the material boundary** (surface/edge states) via the bulk–boundary correspondence. The **Meissner effect** and **AC skin effect** expel the *field/current density*, **not** the particles: in a superconductor the electrons (the "workers") flow **through the bulk** as a dissipationless supercurrent, and the London depth is the *field's* decay length, not an electron-localization length.

## Bridge claim [MAP — to be defended]

The Signal-Worker ontology's boundary-confinement reading — *"the signal orders where the worker may act; the worker is excluded from the bulk"* — is **overgeneralized**:

1. It holds for topological insulators and quantum-Hall systems (gapped bulk → boundary transport).
2. It **fails** for the Meissner effect and AC skin effect (field/current-density expulsion, not mode confinement).
3. It **fails** for the corpus's own flagship material: *Quantum Abacus* (18543167) works on **TaAs, a Weyl semimetal** — gapless, **conducting bulk** with Fermi-arc surface states.

A corrected framing must:
- (a) distinguish **field-expulsion** (Meissner, skin) from **mode-confinement** (TI, QH);
- (b) account for **composite bosons** (Cooper pairs — two fermions → one boson) that break the boson=signal/fermion=worker mapping;
- (c) engage the **spin-statistics theorem** explicitly (the real boson/fermion distinction the ontology re-describes);
- (d) be explicitly labeled an **unconfirmed internal proposal** (ambient superconductivity has never been achieved).

**Falsifiability condition:** disconfirmed if the corrected framing yields no new observable consequence beyond the standard bulk–boundary correspondence and spin-statistics taxonomy — i.e., if it is pure relabeling (the criterion the Prime Valuation Depth follow-on, QNFO.RES.006, applied to itself).
