### 2. Number Theory → ODR (RQ2)
**Isomorphism:** "Place-democracy (Ostrowski)" ⟺ "The real continuum ℝ is ONE completion, not the 'real' number system."

### 3. Holography → ODR (RQ3)
**Isomorphism:** "BT tree as tensor network geometry (p-adic AdS/CFT)" ⟺ "BT tree as coordinate system for particle identity (ODR)."

- This is the strongest external corroboration of the entire thesis.

### 4. Information Theory → ODR (RQ5)
**Isomorphism:** "Radix trie (1960s CS)" ⟺ "BT tree for particle classification."
- Five disciplines discovered the same (p+1)-valent combinatorial tree with cross-ratios independently.
- The radix trie stores keys by their base-p representation; the BT tree classifies particles by their prime factorizations of N_C.

### 5. Quantum Foundations → ODR

- The trembling motion at 2mc²/ħ was dismissed as a QFT artifact. ODR says: it was the right frequency, wrong interpretation — it's the particle's clock rate, not a spatial wobble.
