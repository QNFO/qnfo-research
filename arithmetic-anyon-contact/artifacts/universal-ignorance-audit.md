# Universal Ignorance Audit — QNFO.RES.028 (Phase 0, 2026-08-27)

Applied to the RES.028 core claim: *"The bounded-occupation family ζ(s)/ζ((m+1)s) is Gentile intermediate statistics and cannot carry braid-group exchange phases; the phase-carrying arithmetic objects are multiplicative characters at roots of unity; the prime-gap density of states of the Riemann gas supplies a distinguishing observable."* Administered before Phase 1; not resolved here (resolution is Phase 4's job).

1. **Scaffolds.** (a) Riemann-gas isomorphism primes↔energy levels (ε = ln p) is a chosen labeling, not derived. (b) Max-entropy as a selection postulate. (c) The m-family is imposed as an occupation truncation by hand; the adelic product formula does not itself imply it. (d) The RES.027 §6 "must contact" claim rests on the unproven assumption that braid phases reduce to occupation constraints. (e) C2 assumes the in-corpus roots-of-unity records describe the same braid data as physical anyon models — to be checked, not inherited.
2. **Map–territory.** RES.027 is an explicit structural register ("no particle ontology"). C1/C2 keep that register: the claims are about which mathematical object carries a phase, not about what anyons are. The conflation risk is promoting the m-family dictionary into a physical claim in practitioner prose.
3. **The wobble.** Occupation truncation changes counting (real generating function, no phase); braiding changes phase. A real-valued Dirichlet series cannot encode a complex exchange phase — this is the structural reason C1 should hold, and it must be checked rather than assumed (that check is C1's computation).
4. **Inversion.** The squarefree↔Fermi assignment is rigid (canonical derivative asymmetry, RES.027 check S1); inversion is not the failure mode. The live inversion is the other direction: if the character model (C2) fails D2, the arithmetic program has no phase carrier at all.
5. **Falsifiability.** D1–D3 are computable. C1 is expected to be *confirmed* (m-family fails as phase carrier); the publishable content is the adjudication and the characterization, not the surprise.
6. **Invariants.** Two-domain dichotomy, exponential-family max-entropy, γ = 1/N all survive base change (RES.027). The exchange *phase* is the fragile layer — it lives in the braid group, not the lattice.
7. **Perspectival shift.** Number theorist: ζ(s)/ζ((m+1)s) is a classical m-th-power-free identity; condensed-matter physicist: Gentile statistics is 1940s prior art and HES↔braid was settled in the 1990s–2015; topologist: braids are trajectories, caps are not. Each perspective must appear in the paper's related work.
8. **Power.** The silenced incumbent is the spin-statistics theorem (Lorentz, microcausality) — audited symmetrically, not dismissed; the framework does not derive it and must not claim to.
9. **Most dangerous question.** "Any countable set bijects to the primes — is this numerology with verification scripts?" The honest answer: the lattice statistics are a translation; they become physics only through the predictive leg (H2) and the practitioner adjudication (C1/C2). This project exists to close exactly that gap.
10. **Somatic.** The anyon section is where the unease lives: the m-family is too smooth (no topology, no phase) to be the anyon bridge; the body knows the correspondence is underdetermined.
11. **Willful ignorance.** ζ(s) diverges at s = 1; the thermodynamic limit of the primon gas has a Hagedorn-type singularity at the Bost–Connes point, which the golden-number derivation at fixed β ignores. H2's specific-heat computation must respect the singularity.
12. **What the unknown wants.** Stop forcing m to be a braid phase; ask what generates e^{iθ}. The corpus already answers with roots of unity (quantum groups at q a root of unity). C2 tests that answer.
13. **Actionable now.** The m-family vs Laughlin/Haldane/Fibonacci comparison and the character braid matrices are computable in-session (Phase 4).
14. **The gift.** The open gap m = 1 … ∞ remains as the playground for character-theoretic phase objects; closing C1/C2 converts the gap into a map without losing the arithmetic ladder itself.
15. **Meta-question.** *What minimal observable distinguishes the arithmetic origin of the two statistics from the standard derivation — and is it computable inside the Riemann-gas model or only in a physical 3D system?* Carried into Phase 1 as H2's formulation constraint.
