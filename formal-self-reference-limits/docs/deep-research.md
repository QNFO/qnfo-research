## §3 Gap Analysis

**What is already covered (internally):**
1. Anthropocentric origin of base-10 — Ten-Fingered Trap (deep coverage of pentadactyly contingency).
2. Computational self-reference management — Universal Computational Topos (trinity, fixed-point spectra, strange loops as essential).
3. Observer inside/outside — s10-observer (+ 29-schisms, Bootstrap Theorem).
4. Map/territory — map-is-not-the-universe, MAP-TERRITORY-1 discipline.

**What is NOT covered (the gap this paper fills):**
1. **The objectification thesis** — the *diagnosis* that formal systems' discomfort with self-reference is not an accident of anthropocentric origins but the structural price of third-person objectification (the map excludes the mapmaker BY DESIGN). Ten-Fingered Trap documents the convention; Universal Computational Topos manages the loop; neither diagnoses *why* systems are "uncomfortable" and neither connects the two halves.
2. **The convention-layer vs formal-core distinction applied to the seed question** — anthropocentric units (base-10, heartbeat-Hz, meter) are convention-layer residues that never enter the formal core; the seed question's premise that "the systems are inherently anthropocentric" is *partially wrong*: the units are anthropocentric, the formal core is not. Self-reference limits live in the formal core; unit conventions live at the interface.
3. **The partial-self-knowledge corollary** — "we see ourselves truly but never totally"; a total self-model requires an outside; meta-levels (Gödel sentences, Tarski hierarchy, mirrors) give truth-without-totality. s10-observer's independent conclusion (residual external perspective relocates rather than eliminates the outside) is a *supporting isomorphism* — strong consilience anchor.

**Novelty verdict:** the components are internal; the *synthesis* (objectification thesis + convention-layer distinction + partial-self-knowledge corollary, applied to the seed question) is the novel, publishable contribution. This is a philosophical/mathematical-epistemology synthesis (RES domain), not a new formal theorem. Falsifiability of P6 remains as locked (a consistent recursively-axiomatizable system defining its own truth predicate would disconfirm; base-dependence of a formal theorem would disconfirm the convention-layer claim).
