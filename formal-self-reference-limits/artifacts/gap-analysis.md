## 2. What the corpus does NOT cover (the gap)

1. **The objectification thesis (P6 core claim):** a unified *diagnosis* that formal systems' discomfort with self-reference is the structural price of third-person objectification — the map excludes the mapmaker BY DESIGN — not a defect of anthropocentric origin. No internal record states this diagnosis.
2. **Convention-layer vs formal-core distinction applied to the seed question:** anthropocentric units (base-10, heartbeat-second, meter) are convention-layer residues that never enter the formal core; the premise "the systems are inherently anthropocentric" is partially wrong. Ten-Fingered Trap documents the origin; no record makes the formal-core claim.
3. **Partial-self-knowledge corollary:** "we see ourselves truly but never totally; a total self-model requires an outside." The seed note's final answer ("the mapmaker is in the map's blind spot") is not stated as a corollary in any internal record.
4. **The seed question answered explicitly** ("Can we not truly see ourselves?" → yes, truly but partially, through meta-levels).
