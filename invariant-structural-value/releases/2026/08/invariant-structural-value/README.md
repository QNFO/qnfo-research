# Invariant Structural Value

**WBS:** QNFO.RES.007 · **Repo:** QNFO/qnfo-research · **Branch:** res/paper/invariant-structural-value

## Overview

A structuralist reading of physical law: fundamental constants and formulas encode
**invariant relations** — dimensionless ratios, symmetry-group data, topological
indices, fixed-point values — not unit-dependent magnitudes. Measurable physics is the
invariant quotient of a larger non-measurable mathematical structure modulo redundancy
groups. The constants e and π are fixed points of self-reference (self-application and
self-closure) on a primitive mark/distinction; Euler identity is their joint fixed point.

## Core claims

- **C1** Constants/formulas encode invariant relations, not magnitudes.
- **C2** Measurable physics = invariants of a larger mathematical structure / redundancy groups.
- **C3** e (self-application) and π (self-closure) are fixed points of mark-and-distinction self-reference.
