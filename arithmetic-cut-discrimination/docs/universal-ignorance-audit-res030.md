- **Subject:** Core claim D1–D3, PROJECT-PLAN.md §1.2

- **Method note:** administered per the instrument's administration protocol; answers feed Phase 1 due-diligence formulations.

## Phase 1 — Surface the Structure

**Q1 (Scaffold detection).** Load-bearing assumptions that have become invisible:
(a) equilibrium statistical mechanics at finite P_max is the correct arena —
Boltzmann weights, ergodicity, no dynamics, no interactions; (b) "same mean
level density" is the *right* equivalence class for the mimicry objection — the
objection's force is entirely hostage to this choice, and the published
observable was never tested against it; (c) staircase unfolding is a
representation-faithful operation at finite N — UMP.014's own limitations
section concedes the null Monte Carlo ran at n=2000 against real spectra at
n≈200,000; (d) a real inverse temperature β∈R is the only temperature — this is
the documented premise boundary (RES.029, UMP.014), but it is a premise, not a
theorem. All four are named here so none operates silently.

**Q2 (Map–territory hygiene).** The specific-heat deviation "up to roughly
three quarters at low temperature" (RES.028) is a *map*: a computed curve for
the primon-gas idealization. The *territory* would be the measured C_V of an
engineered system. The discrimination thresholds this project computes are
map-level claims about map-level objects until an experiment exists; the paper
must not let "2σ separation in the computation" read as "2σ discovery in
nature." The "2σ" itself is a statistical-map convention, not a physical
quantity.

**Q3 (Wobble probe).** The wobbles, named: (a) the published abstract
foregrounds the low-temperature deviation, while the program's own red-team
block shows all three statistics collapse to β²(ln2)²2^{−β} at low T — low T is
not where statistics discrimination lives; (b) the Bost–Connes transition is a
smooth maximum at finite P_max with width ~1/ln P_max — the "transition" claim
is only honest under a stated rounding convention; (c) the single-realization
spectral form factor is non-self-averaging (K/N fluctuates O(1)) — thresholds
built on K(τ) need ensemble or τ-window averaging, not single-realization
quotes.

## Phase 2 — Stress-Test the Frame

**Q4 (Inversion).** What if D1 is false — the cut is *not* separable from a
matched-density null at any feasible P_max? Then the RES.027–029 specific-heat
observable is level-density content; the identification stays mathematically
exact and becomes physically inert in that channel, and the program's
statistical signature reduces to the two-point function. That is branch N1 of
the disconfirmation matrix and it is publishable. What if D2 is false — C_V
alone separates? Then the two-point machinery is unnecessary for the
discrimination claim and the paper must say so plainly.

**Q5 (Falsifiability test).** A world where D1 is false looks like: ROC curves
against all three matched-density nulls never cross 2σ at any P_max the
computation can reach, at any noise level. A world where D2 is false: at least
one matched-density null is C_V-indistinguishable from the cut while its
two-point statistics also fail to separate. Both worlds are concrete, checkable
outputs of the deposited scripts.

**Q6 (Invariant extraction).** What survives a change of frame: the Euler
products (multiplicative structure of the integer lattice) — exact, independent
of realization; the high/low-temperature limits — exact; the existence of
prime-gap lumpiness in the level density — structural; the p-adic realization
is one of several equivalent realizations (UMP.014 L3). What does not survive:
the specific separation thresholds — they are statistics-dependent and
observable-dependent by construction.

## Phase 3 — Multiply Perspectives

**Q7 (Radical perspectival shift).** A spectral-analysis statistician would
demand ROC/false-positive curves, not single-threshold 2σ quotes. A
condensed-matter experimentalist would say noise models (phase noise,
temperature stability) dominate feasibility, and P_max alone is not the
constraint. A number theorist would ask whether any threshold inherits
unproven error terms (PNT remainder; RH-adjacent corrections) — the twin-gap
hard core is proven (minimum prime gap 2), but fine threshold structure near
the boundary could not be. All three observers are invited into Phase 1
formulations.

**Q8 (Externalized ignorance).** Who knows what I do not: the program's own red
team already raised the matched-density null — that objection is this project's
origin, not its discovery. Spectroscopists know molecular spectra are
quasi-regular ("NaH is its own class," UMP.014) — the null ensembles must be at
least as adversarial as real molecular spectra, or the separation is trivially
meaningless. UMP.014's H2O window was Poisson-like; therefore a
Poisson-on-log-scale null with the *exact same mean spacing* is mandatory —
otherwise a spacing artifact is mistaken for arithmetic content.

## Phase 4 — Uncover the Hidden Forces

**Q9 (Power analysis).** Who benefits from a positive result: the program
(publishable observable gains weight) and practitioners (a pre-registered
go/no-go before spending beam time). Who is harmed by an unqualified claim:
experimentalists who engineer platforms for a signature that was level-density
content all along. The corrective institution exists: the null-ledger culture
(UMP.014 published its first negative real-data result the same way).

**Q10 (Protected ignorance probe).** The most dangerous question: "Is the
specific-heat observable as published — 'up to roughly three quarters' — already
vacuous as a *discriminator*, promoted without a matched-density control?" This
project's answer is to make that question a pre-registered hypothesis (D1/D2)
rather than a taboo. The audit must not be flattered into confirming the claim
it tests.

**Q11 (Somatic/tacit).** The felt state is an asymmetry: aesthetic satisfaction
at the exactness of the Euler products, against background unease at the
unresolved status of the physical signature. This project converts that unease
into thresholds — its affective content is legitimate design input, named here
rather than suppressed.

## Phase 5 — Act

**Q12 (Willful ignorance).** What is already known but easily pretended away:
"deviation from a smooth ideal gas" is *not* "arithmetic content." RES.029's
abstract already separates proved / conjectured / disconfirmed, and the
provenance chats state the mimicry objection in writing. Promoting the
specific-heat observable as discriminative without the matched-density control
would be willful ignorance; this project refuses the pretense by construction.

**Q13 (Actionable ignorance).** Without resolving whether any experiment will
be built: the discriminator script is usable *today* on any candidate level
sequence (as UMP.014 already did for NaH/H2O); the ROC/threshold tables are the
pre-registration for whatever platform comes later. Action does not wait for
resolution.

**Q14 (Relational ignorance).** What the unknown asks: honesty about the
modality of the result. The computation can show map-level separability, not
territory-level existence of arithmetic structure in physics. The paper will
say exactly which modality it demonstrates.

**Q15 (Recursive meta-question).** The question not yet asked: *is the
comparison between cut and null being run under the same unfolding, the same
spectral window, and the same estimator — or is one side allowed a private
advantage?* Sub-questions generated: (a) must the nulls use the identical Li
unfolding as the cut (fairness condition); (b) is the right comparison fixed
P_max or fixed spectral range (the two scale differently); (c) should the
Boltzmann third case — present in the chats but never published — be carried
into the paper at all, or is it an artifact of the provenance material? These
become explicit P2 design constraints.
