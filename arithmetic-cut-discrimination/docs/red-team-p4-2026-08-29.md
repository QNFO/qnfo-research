## 2. Adversary 2 — Methodology Skeptic ("the estimator or the reference is wrong")

asymptotic by ~40% at L=10–20. **Adjudicated by the exact integral:** the
Dyson formula mis-fits by 20–33% at L ≤ 50 (it converges from below); the
estimator matches the EXACT two-point reduction within 8–13% at n = 800–2000.

(uniform 0.0, Poisson 10.63 vs 10.0 confirm the estimator).

**Consequence for D-1 (sharpened):** the published UMP.014 comparison
"1.044 vs 0.525" is doubly invalid — (a) it mixes windows (Dyson(3400) =

theory at L = 20 (0.6495). The zeros' measured values exceed the EXACT
theory by 1.2–2.8× (growing with L) — the low-height S(t) unfolding residual,
now isolated from the baseline error. Integration doc §3 amended with the
