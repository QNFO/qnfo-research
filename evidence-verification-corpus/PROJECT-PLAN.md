# PROJECT-PLAN — Evidence-Verification Corpus for QNFO/QWAV Research Programs

**WBS:** QNFO.RES.014 · **Slug:** `evidence-verification-corpus` · **Repo:** QNFO/qnfo-research
**Branch:** `res/paper/evidence-verification-corpus` · **Level:** project (parent QNFO.RES)

## Purpose

The user's standing concern: the QNFO/QWAV corpus *feels* uniformly untested and speculative — but that impression is a **map failure**, not a statement about the territory. Verification evidence exists and is substantial, but it is scattered across ~1,600 records, per-paper artifacts, calibration registers, and external confluence papers. This project consolidates that evidence into a single ledger indexed per research program (UMP / SLB / INM / CFE / RES / PLT / DEM), classifying every major claim as **VERIFIED / DERIVED / OPEN** with its evidence chain, so the corpus's actual evidential strength is visible at a glance.

## Core claim (locked, P6)

**C₀:** The QNFO/QWAV research programs contain a substantial verified core — code-executed simulations, pre-registered predictions with confirmed outcomes, and externally corroborated structural results — that is currently under-credited because the verification evidence is distributed across the corpus rather than consolidated. A program-indexed evidence ledger, cross-linked to external verification, will show that the corpus is not uniformly speculative: a large fraction of central claims are computationally executed, empirically confirmed, or externally corroborated, and the genuinely open claims are explicitly bounded.

Falsifiable core: the ledger's classification of each claim is checkable — every VERIFIED claim must point to either (a) a code-executed simulation artifact with output, (b) a pre-registration with a confirmed/disconfirmed outcome, or (c) an external paper that independently corroborates the structure; claims without such evidence are labeled OPEN, not VERIFIED.

## Premise-depth disclosure (SO-WHAT-GATE-1)

- **Unanalyzable primitives (imported):** "locale"/"interface" (UMP.011); the mark/act-of-distinction vocabulary (SLB); the ultrametric/p-adic valuation framework (UMP).
- **Named imported inputs:** structural realism (Ladyman–Ross) as interpretive gloss; external theorems (Maltesson et al. arXiv:2510.08546; Brenner–Dias–Koenig arXiv:2509.18854; Mekonnen–Galley–Müller arXiv:2502.17576; Deaconu–Gargava–Kalra–Mosca–Yard arXiv:2510.11526 — Bruhat–Tits buildings in Clifford+R exact synthesis) are imported external results, not derived here.
- **Derived in this project:** the evidence ledger itself — the verified/derived/open classification per claim, per program, with evidence chains.
- **Where the premises END:** the ledger does not prove the physics; it classifies what the corpus itself already evidences, and flags what it does not. A VERIFIED label means "the corpus contains executable/confirmatory/external-corroborating evidence for this claim" — not "the claim is settled physics." External confluence is labeled as confluence (independent convergence), never as derivation.
- **Why a reader should care:** one document tells a researcher, funder, or collaborator exactly which QNFO/QWAV claims are backed by executed code, confirmed predictions, or external theorems — and which are honestly open. It corrects the systematic under-credit of the verified core.
