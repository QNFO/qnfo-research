# Universal Ignorance Audit — QNFO.RES.014 (ZENODO-INQUIRY-1, Phase 0)

**Target X:** C₀ — the claim that a program-indexed evidence ledger will show the QNFO/QWAV corpus is not uniformly speculative, with a large verified core (executed/confirmed/external).
**Method:** Universal Ignorance Audit, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21901984 (v0.3; companion 10.5281/zenodo.21901983). All 15 questions answered in writing; no resolution attempted during Phases 1–4; Q15 seeds the next pass.

## P1 — Surface

1. **Scaffolds:** (a) "a claim is untested unless a lab experiment confirmed it" — false; executable simulations, pre-registrations, and external confluence are evidence classes; (b) "the corpus must be uniformly self-contained" — it is not; external confluence is load-bearing; (c) "a ledger is neutral" — it is a curated map with classification power; (d) "every record should carry its own evidence" — evidence is often in sibling records/artifacts; the ledger's job is to make the cross-links explicit.
2. **Map-territory:** the map = the ledger's VERIFIED/DERIVED/OPEN classification. The territory = what the corpus actually evidences. The failure mode being corrected: the *absence of a map* made readers mistake "not centrally indexed" for "not tested."
3. **Wobble:** the tension between the user's felt sense ("everything I do is untested") and the verifiable record (executed simulations, confirmed predictions, external confluences). The wobble is real and public; the ledger is the correction.

## P2 — Stress

4. **Inversion:** if C₀ were false, the corpus would be genuinely unverifiable — no artifact re-runnable, no register with dated outcomes, no external corroboration. Test: Q-PNA, p-adic wavelet, BQNN baseline, ZBW correlator, Mahler discriminant are all executable artifacts with recorded outputs; CAL-BQNN-05 was confirmed one year early against a dated register; Bruhat–Tits buildings appear inside Clifford+R exact synthesis (external). The inversion fails → C₀ holds.
5. **Falsifiability:** C₀ is falsified if a majority of centrally claimed results lack (a) a re-runnable artifact, (b) a dated register outcome, or (c) external corroboration. The ledger's F1–F4 register makes this checkable per entry.
6. **Invariant extraction:** strip the QNFO branding — what survives: executable computational evidence, dated pre-registration outcomes, independent external corroboration. These are the territory; the ledger is the map.

## P3 — Multiply

7. **Radical observers:** a funder reads "executed code + confirmed predictions + external theorems" differently from "speculative framework"; a collaborator needs to know which seams are open; a skeptic will attack misclassified entries. The ledger must satisfy all three readers.
8. **Externalized ignorance:** what does the external record know that the corpus's own presentation hides? The Bruhat–Tits result (Buildings for Clifford+R), the energy exchange-rate theorems, the parastatistics no-go, the RQT indistinguishability — all external, all corroborating or bounding the corpus's structural claims. The ledger imports them explicitly.

## P4 — Hidden

9. **Power:** who benefits from the "everything is speculative" impression? Critics and competitors do. Who loses? The researcher (under-credit), funders (misinformed), and honest collaborators (misled about the verified core). The ledger redistributes the evidence asymmetry.
10. **Protected/dangerous question (held):** "Is the corpus's *verified core* itself large enough, or is the honest number closer to a minority of central claims?" Held — the ledger's classification will answer this empirically; the audit does not pre-judge the ratio.
11. **Somatic:** the felt sense is frustration at systematic under-credit — the body signal is "evidence exists, it is not being seen." The ledger is the visibility instrument.

## P5 — Act

12. **Willful ignorance:** pretending the verified core is too small to consolidate. The corpus record (executed simulations across UMP/DEM/PLT, confirmed registers, external confluences) says otherwise.
13. **Actionable:** (a) construct the per-program ledger tables (P4); (b) verify each VERIFIED entry's artifact/register/paper link live; (c) publish with SO-WHAT + premise-depth; (d) maintain the ledger as a living table as new confluences land (e.g., QPL 2026).
14. **Relational:** the unknown asks for honest classification — VERIFIED where evidence exists, OPEN where it does not, with no inflation of either column.
15. **Meta (recursive, seeds next pass):** *"Is the ledger itself a map that will fail at its own seams — and does classifying a claim as VERIFIED-EXTERNAL overstate the strength of 'independent convergence' versus 'derivation'?"* — the seed for the next audit pass, applied to the ledger after P4 construction.
