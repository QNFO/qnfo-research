## Falsifiability register

- F1: a claim labeled VERIFIED-EXECUTED whose simulation artifact cannot be re-run or whose output is absent → the ledger entry is false (misclassification).
- F2: a claim labeled VERIFIED-PREREG whose register entry lacks a dated outcome → the entry is false.
- F3: a claim labeled VERIFIED-EXTERNAL whose cited external paper does not exist or does not corroborate the stated structure → the entry is false.
- F4: the ledger itself is subject to C₃-style scrutiny: it is a map of the corpus's evidence — it must not be mistaken for the evidence itself (each entry must resolve to its artifact/register/paper).
