There are several motivations in physics and mathematics to
investigate ultrametric and p-adic mathematical models.
Breakdown of the Archimedean axiom at the Planck scale.
The number field invariance principle: Fundamental physical
laws should be invariant under the change of the number
fields.
Application of p-adic numbers to cryptography: they serve as
a primitive in the selection of the secure elliptic curves
allowing to solve the problem of counting the number of
solutions in finite fields of the bivariate polynomial equations
P(X,Y) = 0.
Ultrametric spaces are adequate mathematical instrument for
investigation of hierarchical structures appearing in some
phenomena of the complex systems, where p-adics can be
used as a toy model.
