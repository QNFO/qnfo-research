## 2. Method

The $\text{PGL}(2,\mathbb{Z})$ action on $\mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{R})$ is generated by Möbius transformations with integer coefficients and determinant $\pm 1$. Three specific transformations mapping $[0,1]$ to $[0,1]$ were tested:

- $g_1(x) = \frac{x}{x+1}$ (matrix: $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$, determinant 1)
- $g_2(x) = \frac{1}{2-x}$ (matrix: $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ -1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$, determinant 1)
- $g_3(x) = \frac{x+1}{x+2}$ (matrix: $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$, determinant 1)

For each generator, 50 random $x \in (0,1)$ were tested. $?(x)$ was computed via recursive Stern-Brocot splitting (depth 20).
