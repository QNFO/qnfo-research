### The Core Premise Under Attack: The Nature of "Quantum"

The paper's entire edifice rests on an unspoken, standard premise: that the mathematical structure of quantum mechanics is fundamentally about **superposition in a complex Hilbert space**, governed by a norm with the standard Archimedean triangle inequality. All the key elements flow from this:
