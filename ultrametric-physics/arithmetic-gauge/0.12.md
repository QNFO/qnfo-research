# Comprehensive Bibliography: Kapustin–Witten Duality and Related Topics

**Organized by subject. Seminal papers marked with ★.**

## Primary Sources

- ★ **A. Kapustin and E. Witten**, *Electric-Magnetic Duality and the Geometric Langlands Program*, Commun. Number Theory Phys. **1** (2007), 1–236. arXiv:hep-th/0604151. — The founding paper.
- **E. Witten**, *Gauge Theory and the Geometric Langlands Program*, Lecture notes, IAS (2007).
- **E. Witten**, *Fivebranes and Knots*, Quantum Topology **3** (2012), 1–137. arXiv:1101.3216. — Connections between knot invariants and the Langlands program via M-theory.
- **E. Witten**, *Knots, Categories, and the Geometric Langlands Program*, Lecture at the IAS (2011).

## Physics Background

### S-Duality and Electric-Magnetic Duality

- ★ **C. Montonen and D. Olive**, *Magnetic Monopoles as Gauge Particles?*, Phys. Lett. B **72** (1977), 117–120. — The original electric-magnetic duality conjecture.
- **P. Goddard, J. Nuyts, and D. Olive**, *Gauge Theories and Magnetic Charge*, Nucl. Phys. B **125** (1977), 1–28. — The GNO (Goddard–Nuyts–Olive) dual group.
- **A. Sen**, *Dyon-Monopole Bound States, Self-Dual Harmonic Forms on the Multi-Monopole Moduli Space, and SL(2, $\mathbb{Z}$) Invariance in String Theory*, Phys. Lett. B **329** (1994), 217–221. arXiv:hep-th/9402032.
- **N. Seiberg and E. Witten**, *Electric-Magnetic Duality, Monopole Condensation, and Confinement in N = 2 Supersymmetric Yang–Mills Theory*, Nucl. Phys. B **426** (1994), 19–52. arXiv:hep-th/9407087.

### N = 4 SYM and Topological Twisting

- **L. Brink, J. Schwarz, and J. Scherk**, *Supersymmetric Yang–Mills Theories*, Nucl. Phys. B **121** (1977), 77–92.
- ★ **E. Witten**, *Topological Quantum Field Theory*, Commun. Math. Phys. **117** (1988), 353–386. — The Donaldson twist.
- **C. Vafa and E. Witten**, *A Strong Coupling Test of S-Duality*, Nucl. Phys. B **431** (1994), 3–77. arXiv:hep-th/9408074.
- **N. Drukker, D. Morrison, and T. Okuda**, *Loop Operators and S-Duality in N = 4 Gauge Theories*, JHEP **09** (2009), 031. arXiv:0906.2319.

### Branes and Boundary Conditions

- **D. Gaiotto and E. Witten**, *Supersymmetric Boundary Conditions in N = 4 Super Yang–Mills Theory*, J. Stat. Phys. **135** (2009), 789–855. arXiv:0804.2902.
- **D. Gaiotto and E. Witten**, *S-Duality of Boundary Conditions in N = 4 Super Yang–Mills Theory*, Adv. Theor. Math. Phys. **13** (2009), 721–896. arXiv:0807.3720.

- **R. Donagi and T. Pantev**, *Langlands Duality for Hitchin Systems*, Invent. Math. **189** (2012), 653–735. arXiv:math/0604617.
