# Recent Developments

**Companion note to the Kapustin–Witten Duality paper**

---

## 1. Overview

Since Kapustin and Witten's breakthrough in 2007, the field has developed rapidly along several fronts. This note surveys the most significant developments.

---

## 2. Proof of the Geometric Langlands Conjecture (2024)

### 2.1 The Result

After decades of work, Dennis Gaitsgory, Sam Raskin, Dima Arinkin, Nick Rozenblyum, and a large team of collaborators completed the proof of the (derived) geometric Langlands conjecture. The first paper in the series appeared on the arXiv in April 2024 [1].

**Statement (proved):** For any reductive group G over $\mathbb{C}$ and any smooth, complete curve C, there is an equivalence of DG categories:

```
IndCoh_Nilp_{glob}(Loc_{^LG}(C))  ≅  D-mod(Bun_G(C))
```

Here:
- Loc_{^LG}(C) is the moduli stack of de Rham ^LG-local systems on C.
- IndCoh_Nilp_{glob} is the category of ind-coherent sheaves with singular support in the global nilpotent cone.
- D-mod(Bun_G) is the DG category of D-modules on the moduli stack of G-bundles.

### 2.2 Proof Strategy

The proof does **not** rely on the Kapustin–Witten physical argument (which remains a heuristic motivation, not a rigorous proof). Instead, it uses:

1. **Local-to-global principle**: The Hecke category acts on D-mod(Bun_G), and the spectral decomposition of this action gives the correspondence.
2. **Crystals and singular support**: Sheaves on Loc_{^LG} are characterized by their singular support (Arinkin–Gaitsgory theory).
3. **Rigidity theorems**: The functor in one direction is essentially unique, reducing the problem to constructing it and checking it is an equivalence on specific objects.
4. **Categorical trace methods**: The trace of the Frobenius endofunctor on sheaves on Bun_G (for curves over F_q) provides a bridge from the function-field case (proved by Lafforgue) to the $\mathbb{C}$ case.

### 2.3 Significance

- The proof confirms the correctness of the Kapustin–Witten physical picture.
- It validates decades of work by Drinfeld, Laumon, Beilinson, Frenkel, Gaitsgory, and many others.
- It establishes derived algebraic geometry (DG categories, ind-coherent sheaves, crystals) as essential tools for the Langlands program.

## 3. The AGT Correspondence (2009)

### 3.1 The Duality

In 2009, Alday, Gaiotto, and Tachikawa [2] proposed a remarkable duality:

```
4D N = 2 gauge theory (class S)   ↔   2D conformal field theory
     (on ℝ⁴ or S⁴)                         (on Riemann surface C)
```

Specifically, for G = SU(2):

- **Left side**: Nekrasov instanton partition function of 4D N = 2 SU(2) gauge theory with N_f = 4 flavors (the superconformal case), with $\Omega$-background parameters $\varepsilon _1 \varepsilon _2$.
- **Right side**: Conformal blocks of Liouville theory on a 4-punctured sphere, with central charge c = 1 + 6Q$^2$, where Q = b + 1/b and b$^2$ = $\varepsilon _1 \varepsilon _2$.

### 3.2 Generalization to Higher Rank

For general G (or, more precisely, for a Lie algebra 𝔤 of type ADE), the AGT correspondence relates:

- N = 2 class S theory of type 𝔤 on a punctured Riemann surface C, to
- **Toda field theory** of type 𝔤 on C (a 2D CFT with W(𝔤)-algebra symmetry).

The instanton partition function for general 𝔤 was computed using the Nekrasov–Shatashvili formalism and matched with Toda conformal blocks.

### 3.3 Connection to Kapustin–Witten

The AGT correspondence and the Kapustin–Witten duality are closely related:

- Both involve a 4D gauge theory associated to a Riemann surface C.
- The Hitchin moduli space M_H(G, C) appears in both: as the **Coulomb branch** of the 3D theory obtained by compactifying the class S theory on S$^1$, and as the **target** of the 2D sigma model in the Kapustin–Witten construction.
- S-duality in N = 2 class S theories (Gaiotto duality) is a generalization of the S-duality in N = 4 SYM, and it interchanges different degeneration limits (pants decompositions) of C.

## 4. Quantum Geometric Langlands

### 4.1 The Quantum Parameter

The **quantum geometric Langlands** correspondence introduces a deformation parameter $\Psi$ (or q), which in the Kapustin–Witten construction corresponds to the finite size of $\Sigma$ (rather than the strict $\Sigma \to$ 0 limit).

The correspondence becomes:

```
D-mod_Ψ(Bun_G(C))  ≅  D-mod_{-1/Ψ}(Bun_{^LG}(C))
```

where D-mod_$\Psi$ denotes the DG category of twisted D-modules with twisting parameter $\Psi$ ∈ $\mathbb{C}$ ∪ {$\infty$}.

### 4.2 Physical Origin

From the perspective of the 4D gauge theory:
- $\Psi$ = 0 corresponds to $\Sigma \to$ 0 (classical geometric Langlands).
- $\Psi \to \infty$ corresponds to the S-dual limit.
- Finite $\Psi$ corresponds to $\Sigma$ of finite area, and the theory on $\Sigma$ is a **gauged WZW model** (or, dually, a sigma model with a B-field).

At the self-dual point $\Psi$ = i, there is a conjectural **self-duality** for G = ^LG (when G is self-dual, e.g., G = SO(2n), E$_8$).

### 4.3 Mathematical Status

The quantum geometric Langlands correspondence has been formulated mathematically (Stoyanovsky, Frenkel, Gaitsgory) and partially proved in some cases. It is closely related to:
- The representation theory of affine Kac–Moody algebras at the critical level.
- The **Feigin–Frenkel** isomorphism between the center of the critical-level affine algebra and the classical W-algebra.

## 5. 3D-3D Correspondence

### 5.1 The Duality

Witten's work on Chern–Simons theory and the Jones polynomial (1989) established a connection between 3D topology and conformal field theory. The **3D-3D correspondence** extends this:

```
3D N = 2 gauge theory on M₃   ↔   Complex Chern–Simons theory on M₃
```

Equivalently: the partition function of 3D N = 2 theory on a 3-manifold M$_3$ with boundary C computes a wavefunction on the phase space M_flat(G_$\mathbb{C}$, C), which is the moduli space of flat G_$\mathbb{C}$-connections on C — i.e., the Hitchin moduli space in complex structure J.

### 5.2 Connecting to Kapustin–Witten

The Kapustin–Witten construction on $\Sigma \times$ C can be viewed as a **4D lift** of the 3D-3D correspondence: compactifying on $\Sigma$ (or taking $\Sigma$ of finite size) reduces the 4D theory to a 3D theory on $\Sigma \times$ C $\to$ C (where $\Sigma$ is now a circle plus a line interval). The resulting 3D theory on C $\times \mathbb{R}$ is related to the complex Chern–Simons theory on C, whose quantization is the geometric Langlands program.

### 5.3 Open Questions

- Can the arithmetic Langlands program be realized via a 3D or 4D gauge theory on a suitable "arithmetic 3-manifold"?
- What is the physical interpretation of the local Langlands correspondence (for a p-adic field)?
- Is there a unified "topological Langlands program" that encompasses all variants?

## 6. Categorical Langlands

### 6.1 From Sheaves to Categories

The original geometric Langlands conjecture is an equivalence of categories of sheaves. The **categorical geometric Langlands conjecture** (Arinkin–Gaitsgory, Gaitsgory) upgrades this to an equivalence of **2-categories** of module categories:

```
(Shv_cat on Bun_G)  ≅  (QCoh_cat on Loc_{^LG})
```

where the objects are categories themselves (module categories for the Hecke category and the spectral category, respectively).

### 6.2 Physical Interpretation

In the physical picture, the categorical upgrade corresponds to including **boundary conditions and interfaces** in the gauge theory. The 2-category structure reflects the composition of defects (interfaces between different gauge theories), and the equivalence of 2-categories is the statement that S-duality extends to an equivalence of the full defect TQFT.

### 6.3 Status

Partial results toward the categorical conjecture have been obtained, and the 2024 proof of the derived geometric Langlands conjecture is a major step toward this more refined goal.

## 7. Physical Approaches to the Riemann Hypothesis

### 7.1 The Hilbert–Pólya Idea

The classical **Hilbert–Pólya conjecture** suggests that the non-trivial zeros of the Riemann zeta function are eigenvalues of a self-adjoint operator (a "Hamiltonian"). This would imply the Riemann Hypothesis (RH) via the reality of the spectrum.

### 7.2 Speculative Connections

Several speculative approaches connect the Langlands program to RH:

- The **Bost–Connes system** (1995): a C*-dynamical system whose KMS states correspond to the Riemann zeta zeros. This system has been related to the class field theory of $\mathbb{Q}$.
- The **Fesenko program**: attempts to construct a "non-archimedean quantum field theory" whose partition function is the Riemann zeta function.
- **p-adic and adelic physics**: The Langlands program over the adeles suggests that a unified physical theory over all completions of $\mathbb{Q}$ (Archimedean and all p-adic) might yield a Hamiltonian whose spectrum encodes the zeros of L-functions.

**Caveat:** At present, none of these approaches has produced a rigorous derivation of RH or even a physically testable prediction. They remain at the level of structural analogy and programmatic speculation.
