## 1. The Starting Point: \(\alpha\) as a Length Ratio

  It is a purely classical interaction scale, not a physical hard surface.

- **Reduced Compton wavelength** \(\bar{\lambda}_C\): The distance below which the single‑particle description of the electron breaks down due to pair creation and quantum vacuum fluctuations:  
  \[
  \bar{\lambda}_C = \frac{\hbar}{m_e c} \approx 3.862 \times 10^{-13}\,\text{m}.

\[
\alpha = \frac{r_e}{\bar{\lambda}_C}

\]

This reframing reveals \(\alpha\) as a **geometric invariant**—the comparison of two lengths of the same system. All dimensionful constants cancel by construction, regardless of the unit system. The dimensionless nature is no longer a happy accident but a necessary consequence of comparing two intrinsic lengths.
