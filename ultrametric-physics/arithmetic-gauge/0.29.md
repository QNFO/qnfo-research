## 1. Methodological Soundness Review

**Score: 8/10**

### 1.1 Strengths

1. **Data-driven foundation.** The vocabulary audit of 47 terms across 17 source documents provides an objective basis for distinguishing genuine overlaps from imposed framing. Every convergence claim is backed by specific term counts, not rhetorical assertion. This addresses the central failure of 0.1.md.

2. **Honest about limitations.** The document explicitly acknowledges: (a) the degenerate cross-ratio question (§5.2), (b) that "building" may include non-Bruhat-Tits uses (Appendix), (c) that some convergence signals are hypotheses, not findings (§5.1 status table), and (d) that "conformal blocks are cross-ratio functions" needs investigation, not assertion.

3. **Appropriate scope reduction.** The salvage trades the grand consilience narrative for five specific convergence signals, each grounded in vocabulary data. This is the right move — smaller, sharper, more honest.

4. **Falsifiability.** Three testable predictions are proposed with explicit falsification conditions. Even if none are experimentally accessible today, the presence of falsification criteria distinguishes this from the unfalsifiable rhetoric of 0.1.md.

5. **Clear error acknowledgment.** Section 3 ("What Was Imposed") and Section 7 ("Abandoned Claims") directly confront the mistakes of 0.1.md without defensiveness. This builds credibility.

### 1.2 Weaknesses

1. **Vocabulary audit term selection is author-driven.** The 47 terms were chosen by the salvage author. While the selection appears reasonable (spanning projective geometry, group theory, Langlands, trees, p-adic, physics, computation), there is no independent check against selection bias. A skeptic could ask: "Were terms chosen that would favor the salvage narrative?" This is partially mitigated by the inclusion of both "genuine overlap" and "imposed" categories, but the selection process should be documented more transparently.

2. **Some convergence signals rest on thin evidence.** Per the audit data:
   - "projective" in Ultrametric Synthesis: only 2 occurrences (compared to B:83, KW:7). Is 2 occurrences a genuine convergence signal?
   - "p-adic" in Undecidability: only 1 occurrence
   - "non-Archimedean" in Undecidability: only 1 occurrence
   - "dual/duality" in Braids: only 3 occurrences
   
   The document claims these as "3 project" convergence signals, but for some terms, one project barely mentions the concept. This isn't disqualifying — a single occurrence can still indicate genuine engagement — but the document should acknowledge the uneven distribution more explicitly rather than treating all "present" counts equally.

3. **"Building" in Braids may include non-Bruhat-Tits uses.** Context verification was claimed (Appendix) but not shown. A spot-check of 0.2.md and 0.6.md (the only Braids files with "building" matches) was inconclusive due to a regex bug in the verification script. If any of the 10 "building" occurrences in Braids are non-mathematical, the three-project convergence claim for "building" weakens. This should be verified and, if necessary, corrected.

4. **"Conformal blocks are cross-ratio functions" claim.** The document states this in §5.1 for Kapustin-Witten. The K-W main paper (0.7.md) contains exactly 1 occurrence of "conformal block" with zero "cross-ratio" occurrences. The claim is mathematically correct (conformal blocks in CFT are functions of cross-ratios of insertion points) but the K-W source documents provide negligible textual evidence for it. This is an instance where the salvage document imports mathematical knowledge that exceeds what the source documents actually state — a milder version of the retroactive framing problem.

## 2. Logical Coherence Review

**Score: 8/10**

### 2.1 Argument Structure

The argument flows cleanly:

1. **Audit data** → identifies what genuinely overlaps
2. **Genuine overlaps** → basis for first-principles framework
3. **Framework** → yields cross-ratio hypothesis
4. **Hypothesis** → yields testable predictions
5. **Abandoned claims** → prevents backsliding into over-claiming

Each step follows from the previous. There are no logical leaps of the "therefore, consciousness is a projection artifact" variety that plagued 0.1.md.

### 2.2 Potential Logical Gaps

1. **From "term appears in source document" to "project converges on concept."** The vocabulary audit counts regex matches. A term appearing 2 times in a project does not necessarily mean the project is structured around that concept. For example, "projective" in Ultrametric Synthesis (2 occurrences) might be used in passing, not as a central framework element. The document implicitly equates term frequency with conceptual centrality — this is a reasonable proxy but should be acknowledged as such.

2. **From "shared vocabulary" to "shared mathematical structure."** The document argues that because three projects use the word "Archimedean," they share a genuine mathematical concern. This is mostly correct (the Archimedean/non-Archimedean distinction is a specific mathematical concept), but the inference is vulnerable to false positives: two projects could use the same term in different senses. The manual context verification claimed in the Appendix addresses this partially but should be more systematic.

### 2.3 Circularity Check

The salvage avoids the circularity that afflicted 0.1.md. In 0.1.md, the framework was the lens and the discovery — you found cross-ratios everywhere because you looked through a cross-ratio lens. In 0.28.md, the framework emerges from vocabulary that genuinely appears across source documents — you find projective geometry because the source documents discuss projective geometry. The direction of inference is correct.

## 3. Evidence-Calibration Review

**Score: 7/10**

### 3.1 Strongest Evidence

- **Archimedean/non-Archimedean tension:** KW:22/9, U:22/8, UD:3/1. Three projects with substantial engagement. This is the strongest convergence signal.
- **Tree structures:** KW:41, U:8, UD:264 (plus Stern-Brocot:58). Strong signal across three projects.
- **p-adic mathematics:** KW:67, U:2, KW paper: 14. Strong in K-W, present in Ultrametric.
- **Cross-ratio as Braids-specific:** B:478, CAP:88, all others: 0. The data clearly shows cross-ratio is a Braids concept, not a universal convergence.

### 3.2 Weaker Evidence

- **Projective geometry as three-project convergence:** B:83 (strong), KW:7 (moderate), U:2 (very thin). The "3 projects" label is technically correct but obscures the uneven distribution.
- **"Building" as three-project convergence:** B:10, KW:27, U:28. The Braids count needs context verification — if "building" in Braids means literal construction rather than Bruhat-Tits building, this signal drops to 2 projects.
- **Duality as three-project convergence:** B:3 (very thin), KW:109 (strong), U:16 (moderate). Braids barely mentions duality.

### 3.3 Recommendation

The document should introduce a signal-strength tier system:
- **Tier 1 (Strong):** $\geq$ 10 occurrences in $\geq$ 2 projects — Archimedean, tree, p-adic, duality (K-W+U)
- **Tier 2 (Moderate):** 5-9 occurrences in $\geq$ 2 projects — projective, building, Möbius
- **Tier 3 (Weak):** <5 occurrences but present in $\geq$ 2 projects — some of the thinner signals

This would make the evidence calibration more transparent and preempt the "thin evidence" criticism.

## 4. Testable Predictions Review

**Score: 7/10**

### 4.1 p-Adic Analog of α (Prediction 6.1)

**Assessment:** Theoretically interesting but practically challenging.
- **Strength:** Specifies a concrete mathematical object (cross-ratio on Bruhat-Tits boundary)
- **Weakness:** Falsification condition is "if no such quantity can be defined" — constructive non-existence is hard to prove. A better falsification condition would be: "if the p-adic cross-ratio $\times$ (some natural time/length scale) yields a value with no physical interpretation."

### 4.2 Cross-Ratio Under Monna Map (Prediction 6.2)

**Assessment:** The strongest of the three predictions.
- **Strength:** Concrete, computable (pick 4 points on Stern-Brocot boundary, compute their cross-ratio, see what happens under M)
- **Weakness:** Requires specifying which 4 points are "natural" — the prediction needs more specificity. What points on the Stern-Brocot boundary correspond to physical observables?
- **Testability:** This could be investigated computationally today with a Python script.

### 4.3 Topological Origin of α (Prediction 6.3)

**Assessment:** Intellectually appealing but experimentally inaccessible.
- **Strength:** Makes a clear, falsifiable claim ($\alpha \to$ rational at Planck scale)
- **Weakness:** Planck-scale experiments ($10^{19}$ GeV) are far beyond current technology. The prediction is falsifiable in principle but not in practice.
- **Risk:** Functions more as a "statement of faith" in the research program than a near-term test.

### 4.4 Overall

Three predictions are appropriate for a theoretical research program. The Monna map prediction (6.2) is the most actionable and should be prioritized for investigation.

## 6. Remaining Concerns

### 6.1 The "Building" Verification Gap

The document claims that "building" in Braids (10 occurrences) refers to Bruhat-Tits buildings. A spot-check was attempted but inconclusive. **Recommendation:** Verify all 10 Braids "building" occurrences and, if any are non-mathematical, adjust the convergence claim or note the qualification.

### 6.2 Conformal Block Claim Thinness

The statement that "conformal blocks are cross-ratio functions" in the K-W context rests on 1 occurrence of "conformal block" in the K-W main paper, with zero "cross-ratio" occurrences. The mathematical fact is correct, but the source-document support is negligible. **Recommendation:** Either (a) provide the mathematical derivation showing that K-W conformal blocks ARE cross-ratio functions, independent of the source documents' vocabulary, or (b) downgrade this claim to "hypothesis to be investigated."

### 6.3 Potential "Steel-Manning" of the Salvage

The document presents the genuine convergence signals as a coherent alternative framework. There is a risk that this new framework, while more honest, is itself a retroactive reframing — taking the vocabulary that happens to overlap and organizing it into a narrative that feels more coherent than the source documents would naturally support. The difference from 0.1.md is that 0.28.md is explicit about its methodology and limitations. But the risk of "seeing a pattern because you're looking for one" applies to any synthesis.

**Mitigation:** The document should note that the five convergence signals are proposed as *candidate* patterns, not demonstrated unities, and that future investigation may reveal some signals to be coincidental rather than structural.
