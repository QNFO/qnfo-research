information theory, statistics, thermodynamics, and quantum non-demolition (QND)

(QNFO.ADL.001, 10.5281/zenodo.22024240) with three new computationally verified results:

1. **R1 — p-adic maximum-entropy ≡ Bose–Einstein.** The p-adic max-entropy distribution

   fugacity z = 1/p, i.e. at inverse temperature **β_p = ln p** at the p-adic place; its
   mean 1/(p−1) = H_p^{max}.

   Fermi–Dirac occupation number at the same β_p = ln p (verified over 1.2M squarefree
   integers; max deviation 8.5×10⁻⁶; exact identity (p−1)/(p²−1) = 1/(p+1)).

   measurement of a p-adic-valued observable preserves H_p exactly: the
