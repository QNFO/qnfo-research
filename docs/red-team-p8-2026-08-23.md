## THE THESIS AND BROADER THEME (what the title/abstract must lead with)

The QNFO program's thesis, per the locked core claim: **the seven program
domains are seven vocabularies for one structural object — a nested,
hierarchical (ultrametric) partition logic whose local geometry is the
p-adic/Bruhat-Tits tree, whose global synthesis is the adelic product
formula, and whose minimal act is Spencer-Brown's distinction.** The
scientific content is carried by three falsifiable hypotheses: H1 (the
ultrametric compression prior), H2 (Archimedean physics as the
thermodynamic/ergodic average over the hierarchy), H3 (non-Archimedean
noise signatures in quantum-coherent systems), with a 2028 strong-form
decision point. The broader theme: **the program is the scientific study of
the resolution hierarchy of observation** (UIA Q7) — and it serves the
QNFO/QWAV mission, **the energy-efficiency benchmark for quantum computing
(JPCUB: what does a correct quantum answer cost in energy?)**, to which the
bound family (Landauer/Bremermann/Margolus-Levitin, already analyzed in
§4.2) is the direct link. The keyword-taxonomy audit is evidence for this
program — not the headline.
