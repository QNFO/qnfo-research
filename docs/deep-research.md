- **Gisin–Del Santo program (external):** arXiv:1803.06824 ("Are Real Numbers Really
  Real?"), 1909.03697 (finite-information quantities), 2011.02348, 2205.11547,
  2305.02429, 2409.10601. "Physical quantities contain only finite information; real
  numbers are physically unreal — de facto hidden variables of classical mechanics;

- **Corpus convergence:** `finite-precision-oc-convergence` (10.5281/zenodo.21647362)
  already converged the Gisin–Del Santo program with Autaxys Ontological Closure — 7
  convergent theses, certainty 3–5/5, with the **empirical-equivalence problem
  explicitly unresolved**. `valuation-independent-foundations` (10.5281/zenodo.21803677)
  gives the category-theoretic version ("Valuation Without R").
- **Corpus refinement that COMPLICATES the seed claim:** `continuum-trilogy-03`
  (10.5281/zenodo.21672990) decomposes the continuum into Depth (Archimedean
  completeness — **physically real, required for dynamics/causality**), Breadth
  (uncountability — **physically vacuous**), Valuation (p-adic — real but not
  geometric). The seed claim "the continuum is unphysical" is **too strong against the
  corpus's own refined position**: only uncountable breadth is vacuous; computable
  depth is physical.
- `measurable-vs-imaginable` (10.5281/zenodo.21645350): the computable-real boundary
  ("where physics ends and cognitive fiction begins") — same boundary, already drawn.

**Gap verdict on claim 1:** NOT novel as a claim; novel only if RES.021 delivers the

valuation-real) plus **finite-N predictions that break the empirical equivalence**.

## 2. External constraints on the novel core (the ℂ gap and the ψ-epistemic wall)

- **PBR 2012** (arXiv:1111.3328, Nature Phys. 8:475): ψ-epistemic models with
  independent preparations contradict QM. H-BORN ("superposition emergent from finite
  alternatives") must position itself against PBR/BCLM (1609.01558), not ignore it.
- **Hardy 2001** (quant-ph/0101012): Axiom 5 — *continuity* of reversible
  transformations — is the quantum axiom; it "explains the need for complex numbers."
  H-UNIT's emergence story must explain where continuity AND the complex structure come
  from — a real gradient flow cannot conjure ℂ without new input (UIA Q3 ℂ-wobble has
  formal teeth).
- **Aaronson 2004** (quant-ph/0401062): no nontrivial p-norm theories; real amplitudes
  fail. The large-distinction limit must land on the 2-norm, not on any other.
- **Semiorders (Luce 1956):** indistinguishability is not transitive; the equivalence-
  relation premise of the ultrametric state space is an idealization that must be
  argued, not assumed.
- **Ultrametricity literature (RTV 1986 + Parisi RSB):** ultrametricity is a property of
  hierarchical order in disordered systems; the generality claim is constrained (UIA Q8).
- **Underdetermination (corpus UMP.010/UMP.011):** rendered-ultrametric perception is
  indistinguishable from Archimedean; the ontology claim needs finite-N predictions or
  it is observationally empty (UIA Q4).
