## 1. ACCURACY AUDITOR
### H1 [HARD] Two contradictory metric definitions
- §2.1: J_S(T,ε) = E_comp+E_mem+E_io+E_cool+E_conv+E_mfg (6-component total incl.
  cooling, conversion, amortized manufacturing)
- §2.3: J_S(T,ε) = (P_task − P_idle)·t_exec / p_succ (wall-plug INCREMENTAL only)
- The §2.3 canonical measurement CANNOT produce the §2.1 value: incremental
  wall-plug power excludes E_mfg entirely and cannot attribute facility cooling
  or conversion losses as specified in §2.2. Two definitions = benchmark not
  reproducible as written. FIX: make §2.3 the definition and §2.2 the optional
  component breakdown, OR define additive corrections with measurement rules.

### H2 [HARD] T-CHEM classical baseline is wrong by orders of magnitude
- §4.1 T-CHEM: classical equivalent "FCI/CASCI, minutes on CPU" for H2O 6-31G,
  ε=1.6 mHa. H2O 6-31G: 10 electrons in 24 spatial orbitals → FCI space
  C(13,5)² = 1,656,369 (13 spatial orbitals: 9 O + 2+2 H; 10 electrons) — a 1.66M-dimension FCI matrix, Davidson iterative diagonalization = hours-days on a cluster, NOT minutes on CPU.
- A wrong classical baseline poisons the quantum-vs-classical framing the
  benchmark exists to measure. FIX: correct to CASCI(active space) with a
  stated orbital count, or DMRG/FCI with explicit node-hours estimate.

### H3 [HARD] 100-shot floor is inconsistent with ε=1.6 mHa chemistry
- §2.3/§6.2 require p_succ over ≥100 shots; T-CHEM demands 1.6 mHa chemical
  accuracy. Resolving energy to mHa precision on noisy hardware needs orders
  of magnitude more shots (or QPE-depth circuits) — 100 shots cannot support
  the claimed ε. FIX: per-task shot-count formula tied to the ε requirement
  (e.g., statistical bound n ∝ σ²/ε²), not a flat 100.

### S1 [SOFT] T-ML FLOP claim
- "CNN inference <10^6 FLOPs" for MNIST: a real CNN forward pass is ~10^7–10^8.
  Understates the classical equivalent. FIX: cite a specific architecture.

### S2 [SOFT] QWAV design target "<10^-3 J/sol" vs IBM Eagle 0.89
- Three orders of magnitude below the incumbent with no model derivation shown
  in this doc. It is labeled "design target" (Tier 0), so not a claim — but
  the gap invites the Qubit-Delusion critique it exists to counter. FIX:
  point to the model-derived derivation source.

## 2. COMPLETENESS AUDITOR
### H4 [HARD] No QEC-aware extension — benchmark is NISQ-only by construction
- All four taxonomy tasks (N=15, H2O, 20-node MAX-CUT, MNIST) are NISQ-scale;
  none requires fault tolerance. The metric as specified cannot measure
  fault-tolerant machines (QEC energy overhead dominates at scale) — the
  benchmark would "prove" NISQ advantage forever by never including a
  QEC-requiring task. FIX: add a Tier-2 taxonomy track "T-FT" (e.g., logical
  qubit synthesis at 10^-4 logical error rate) or explicitly scope the
  standard to NISQ with a dated review clause.

### S3 [SOFT] Shot-count vs success definition for estimation tasks
- p_succ for T-CHEM/T-ML (estimation tasks) is ill-defined vs T-FACT (decision
  task). FIX: define per-task success criteria (energy within ε, accuracy ≥
  0.95 on held-out set) with the ε already in the taxonomy — currently the
  taxonomy lists ε but §2.3's p_succ formula treats all tasks alike.
