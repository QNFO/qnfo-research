## Phase 1 — Surface the Structure

**Q1 (Scaffold detection).** Hidden assumptions holding the claim up:

1. *The "same statistical language" scaffold.* The charter's own preamble and the chat Dictionary's preamble ("the physical universe and the mathematical universe are two dialects of the same statistical language") load-bear the project's appeal. C4 partially dismantles it, but the project title itself — "Quantum Thermodynamics" — re-erects it. The scaffold is the unstated slide from "formally identical" to "saying something about physics."
2. *The β = s identification.* The whole dictionary treats the formal inverse-temperature parameter β of the primon gas as the complex variable s of ζ. That identification is a choice (a real-section restriction), not a theorem, and "temperature" at β is formal — no system has been shown to occupy it.
3. *The primon gas is physics.* A Hamiltonian on a formal Hilbert space of integers is a mathematical model. The charter assumes the "quantum statistical mechanics" vocabulary is the right one; an equally valid frame is "combinatorics of multiplicative generating functions."
4. *L0 is a genuine primitive.* The ladder declares distinction "the unanalyzable primitive" — declared, not demonstrated; the pre-arithmetic audit note (_26241061955) likewise takes this as a starting-point choice and flags that calling it ontic is an extra commitment.
5. *The correction ledger is complete.* Eleven rows are listed; completeness is asserted by audit, not by proof. A twelfth error would falsify the "audited" completeness claim — that risk is itself a scaffold.

**Q2 (Map–territory hygiene).** Where the map is mistaken for the territory:

- "ζ(β) is the partition function of a physical gas" — the map is the Euler product; the territory would be a laboratory system whose states are indexed by integers with energies ln n. Nothing in the claim establishes the territory exists. The β = 1.06 crossover discussion in the lineage is a map property (finite cutoff of a product); the Bost–Connes pole is an infinite-mode limit property.
- The explicit-formula statement ("zeros enter as subleading oscillatory corrections") is a property of the *map* (the analytic structure of ζ). Whether any physical spectrum's fluctuations track those corrections is exactly the L3 territory, and the claim does not assert it — but prose must not let the map's precision stand in for the territory's silence.
- The five-level ladder is itself a map of other people's claims; it classifies claim-types, and it can misclassify if a claim spans levels.

**Q3 (Wobble probe).** Where the model does not balance:

- *The Dyson/Bost–Connes wobble.* The published lineage shipped a Dyson number-variance pair mixing window lengths and an asymptotic formula used outside its range (adjudicated in RES.030 as D-1), and a Bost–Connes specific heat at β = 1.06 read as critical behavior. The wobble is not only that these shipped — it is that the same chat lineage produced a Dictionary that misattributes the 34σ result to the zeros. The record exists because of this wobble; the wobble is the subject, not the backdrop.
- *The T2/D2 tension.* The lineage's T2 claimed the specific heat separates "at every sampled temperature, up to ~3/4 at low temperature"; RES.030 then disconfirmed D2 (specific heat alone cannot separate the arithmetic cut from a matched-density null — the deviation is level-density content). The treatise must not resurrect T2's overreading through the Dictionary's thermodynamics section.
- *The elegance pull.* The exactness of ζ = Z is aesthetically load-bearing; the wobble is the felt gap between that elegance and the thinness of the empirical spine (geometric channels null; one real-data result negative; D1 confirmed but D2 disconfirmed as pre-registered).

## Phase 2 — Stress-Test the Frame

**Q4 (Inversion).** What if the opposite were true?

- *Opposite of C1-as-significance:* the correspondence is structurally generic, not special — any set of independent modes with multiplicities yields an Euler product; the "arithmetic" content reduces to choosing ε_p = ln p on the multiplicative lattice. The pre-arithmetic audit already concedes the ultrametric hierarchy is prime-free; by extension, the partition-function dictionary is *multiplicative-structure* content dressed in prime clothing. If so, the record's honest title would be "multiplicative generating functions meet occupation statistics," and primes are a convenient realization. This inversion is not refuted by anything in the claim; it is absorbed by C2's L1/L2 framing and must be stated as such in print.
- *Opposite of C3-as-correction:* the draft Dictionary could be defended in places — e.g., the "chemical potential ↔ twist" row could be salvaged as a formal analogy (fugacity z and character χ are both multiplicative weights). Our C3 rows must therefore be stated as *code-verifiable* errors (derivative factors, signs, attributions), not as style judgments; rows that are only "misleading" must be labeled as such.

**Q5 (Falsifiability).** A world in which this is false:

- C1 is theorem-level about a constructed object: a world where the identities fail is a world without unique factorization. Its falsifiability is *internal* (a computational error in any identity) — the verification suite is the falsification instrument, not an experiment.
- The genuinely falsifiable content is: (i) the *completeness* of C3 (a reader finding a twelfth uncorrected error), (ii) the *adequacy* of C2 (an exhibited admissible L2→L4 inference would break the ladder's central rule), and (iii) the *prose discipline* of C4 (the negative list surviving the published text). Each is pre-registered in the disconfirmation matrix; a world where the framing reimports the L2→L4 slide is the world where C4 fails — and it fails silently if no one checks the prose against it.
- The empirical content (L3) has already been adjudicated: RES.030 D1 CONFIRMED, D2 disconfirmed as pre-registered. This record adds nothing empirical; its falsifiability is audit-level, and that must be the published framing, not a claim to new physics.

**Q6 (Invariant extraction).** What survives a change of frame?

- Re-frame from "arithmetic ↔ quantum thermodynamics" to "combinatorics ↔ generating functions": every identity in C1 survives unchanged (they are statements about multiplicative generating functions); the Gentile family survives; the explicit formula survives. What vanishes is the word "physical." The invariants are: unique factorization → Euler products; exponent rules → statistics filters; analytic continuation of ζ → subleading corrections; the L2-≠-L4 rule → the claim-type discipline. The record should be written so that these invariants are the visible core and the physics vocabulary is flagged as borrowed.

**Q9 (Power analysis).** Who benefits; who is silenced?
