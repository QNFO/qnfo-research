# Universal Ignorance Audit — Post-Positional Numeracy (QNFO.RES.024)

**Target (stated explicitly):** Core claim C1 — "Positional notation is not a structural optimum of numerical representation; Ostrowski's theorem shows the valuation structure of Q consists of the Archimedean place together with all p-adic places; the adele ring A_Q is the rigorous foundation for a post-positional numeracy replacing the tyranny of uniqueness with local-global simultaneity."

**Instruments:** 10.5281/zenodo.21901984 (UIA v0.3) and 10.5281/zenodo.21901983 (IAPS — AI-assisted-pipeline principles). Administered 2026-08-26. Phases 1–4 answered without resolution; Q14 held; Q15 seeds the next pass.

## Phase 1 — Surface the Structure

**Q1. Scaffolds (hidden load-bearing assumptions):**
- S-A: "Numeracy = faithful finite-symbol representation of numbers." The claim inherits this scaffold rather than questioning it; a post-positional numeracy still represents numbers by finite symbols.
- S-B: That the "perfection" discourse is a mathematical claim worth collapsing — it may be pure rhetoric, making Ostrowski a hammer against a rhetorical target.
- S-C: That Ostrowski + adeles are the right lens (vs. e.g. the counter-position that positional notation is optimal for the Archimedean place and that is all it ever promised).
- S-D: That the thesis is a new contribution rather than known Hensel-code/adelic-arithmetic folklore (tested by H-PPN-4).

**Q2. Map–territory:**
- The intervention is itself map-territory hygiene ("perfection" is a claim about the map, notation, not the territory, numbers). Its own risk is the reverse error: treating the adele ring as the true territory when it too is a construction. "Local-global simultaneity as liberation" is a map-emotion projected onto territory.

**Q3. Wobble:**
- The source note's cultural indictment (religion/patriarchy analogies, "centralize epistemic authority") sits uneasily on a mathematical scaffold — the thesis does not balance there.
- Deeper wobble: p-adic digits ARE positional notation (base-p expansion). Every local component of an adele is again positional. "Post-positional" therefore means multi-place, not non-positional — and the thesis currently trades on the stronger word.

## Phase 2 — Stress-Test the Frame

**Q4. Inversion:** The opposite — positional notation IS a structural optimum for its declared task (human Archimedean sequential computation), and "perfection" was always task-scoped; under this reading C1's content shrinks to "notation should be task-scoped" and the adele ring is overkill. Also: what computation requires all places simultaneously that place-by-place computation cannot do?

**Q5. Falsifiability (false-world test):** A world where someone exhibits a non-positional numbering system that dominates positional notation on NUMERATA's axes for its target task AND has no adelic interpretation — there the adele ring is one example among many, not "the" foundation. A world where H-PPN-3 (finite-adele injectivity) has collisions — C1's constructive leg fails. A world where the perfection claim was never mathematical — Ostrowski collapses a straw man.

**Q6. Invariant extraction:** Changing base leaves the place structure of Q invariant (Ostrowski's classification is base-independent). Changing the field Q → K generalizes the product formula but changes the story. The invariant core: *any finite-symbol numeration of a global field is place-relative; the global object carries all places; exact reconstruction needs the global constraint.* This survives frame changes — it is the real claim.

## Phase 3 — Multiply Perspectives

**Q7. Radical perspectival shift:** A p-adic observer (e.g., one computing 3-adically) finds the decimal system parochial and the thesis obvious. A base-2 machine finds the adele ring beautiful but asks "what can I run on it?" (the practitioner-relevance gate). A future exact-quantum-arithmetic observer sees places as natural resource bounds.

**Q8. Externalized ignorance:** Number theorists in adelic point counting / local-global methods would say the blind spot is triviality risk — "adelic numeracy" may be Hensel codes re-dressed, and product-formula-constrained reconstruction is textbook (Dixon-style p-adic exact linear algebra). Historians (e.g., Plofker's *Mathematics in India*) would say the "perfection" target is a pop-history straw man. Numeracy researchers would say "numeracy" already means adult quantitative literacy — the title borrows a loaded term.

## Phase 4 — Uncover the Hidden Forces

**Q9. Power analysis:** The "lie that centralizes epistemic authority" framing benefits the critique's rhetorical reach and positions the adelic program as liberatory; the political vocabulary (tyranny/liberation) imports affect into mathematics. Silenced: the pragmatic adequacy of positional notation for nearly all computation, and the existing exact-arithmetic workers whose techniques the thesis annexes unless cited.

**Q10. Protected ignorance (most dangerous questions):** (a) Is this a mathematical contribution or an essay with theorems attached? (b) Is it Silent Radix v2 with Ostrowski's name added — i.e., does the QNFO corpus already contain the claim (the Silent Radix paper already reads positional notation as an ultrametric tree)? (c) Would the historical premise survive the number-systems literature (Knuth; Akiyama–Pethő; Thuswaldner)?

**Q11. Somatic/tacit:** The source note carries relish at catching a civilization in a lie — a bias vector toward overclaiming. The p-adic appeal is exactness without approximation. The delight must be held at arm's length from the mathematics.

## Phase 5 — Act

**Q12. Willful ignorance:** I know p-adic digits are positional (base-p) — the honest formulation is multi-place (adelic), not non-positional. I know the perfection discourse is rhetoric no one defends formally.

**Q13. Actionable ignorance:** (1) H-PPN-4 novelty sweep (Hensel codes, Dixon lifting, adelic arithmetic) is immediately runnable; claim scope follows evidence. (2) The Q3 wobble is actionable now: reframe to "single-place notation vs. global object"; quarantine the cultural-indictment layer to a clearly labeled essayistic section or drop it (Phase 4 drafting decision).

**Q14. Relational ignorance (held, not resolved):** The thesis wants the adele ring to be more than machinery — a way of seeing numbers. *(Silence held.)*

**Q15. Meta-question (seed of next pass):** *What does exactness cost?* Is post-positional numeracy's real contribution an energy/complexity accounting for exact arithmetic — what is the cost of multi-place exact reconstruction vs. single-place approximate arithmetic — rather than an epistemological one? This connects PPN to the JPCUB mission (energy cost of correct answers) and gives the paper a falsifiable computational axis it currently lacks. **Next audit pass targets this question.**
