# WBS: QNFO.INM.001 — The Informational Universe

## Charter

**Core Claim (locked at Phase 0):** Information — understood as the structure
of distinctions, not as the state of a physical substrate — is the primary
substrate of physics. Physical law is the constraint structure that governs how
distinctions can be drawn, related, and communicated by observers.

**Research Question:** What is the minimal set of information-theoretic
principles from which the observable structure of physics (quantum mechanics,
thermodynamics, measurement, adelic number systems) follows?

## Core Claim (pre-registration)

The claim above is locked at Phase 0 with this timestamped commit. Per the
research-integrity mandate: this paper's cross-domain correspondences must pass
the KIF-60 Bayesian evidential-weight gate — a framework that "explains"
everything by design carries zero evidential weight. The falsifiability
condition for the core claim:

> **Disconfirmed if:** a complete, testable physical law is found that cannot
> be derived from (or shown consistent with) an information-as-primary
> formulation, with the derivation performed pre-registration and the physical
> law's structure genuinely constraining the information-theoretic axioms
> (not merely being compatible with them).
