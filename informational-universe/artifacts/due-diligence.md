## 3. Gap Analysis

The claim "information is the primary substrate" has a long philosophical lineage
(it-from-bit) and a strong *reconstruction* program (Hardy, Chiribella, Masanes–Müller)
showing QM follows from information axioms. **What the QNFO formulation adds:**

1. **Distinction-structure primacy** — the reconstruction programs start from
   *probabilistic theories* (states + measurements); this project starts from
   *distinctions* (the act of cutting) as more primitive, deriving the observer's
   perspective as a structure, not an input.
2. **Adelic number-system connection** — the Continuum Trilogy's Ostrowski
   place-democracy (number system as substrate) is the arithmetic face of the
   information-as-substrate claim. No external work connects QBism/reconstruction
   to adelic completions.
3. **Measurement stratigraphy** — the QEC-Darwinism line (UMP.004) gives an
   operational, quantitative anchor: the observer's "distinctions" are exactly
   the environment fragments that carry redundant classical records.

**Novelty risk:** HIGH philosophical overlap with it-from-bit / QBism. The
differentiator must be operational: the paper must derive a *specific, testable*
consequence (e.g., the adelic structure of measurement, or a distinction-based
derivation that the reconstruction programs do not produce). Per KIF-60: the
correspondence must carry Δlog-odds > 0 via pre-registered prediction, not
post-hoc "explains everything."
