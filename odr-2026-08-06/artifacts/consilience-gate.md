## Synthesis Consilience

**Meta-Principle:** The combinatorial tree encoding the completions of $\mathbb{Q}$ at every prime place — the Bruhat-Tits building — is the universal information geometry. Every physical system that processes information does so by distinguishing states in a hierarchically organized tree. The depth of the tree is set by the energy scale; the branching factor is set by the prime structure of the underlying field. Tensor networks, quantum readout, Casimir forces, Landauer erasure, and biological phototransduction are all manifestations of the same counting: how many distinguishable states exist at a given depth, and what energy is required to discriminate them.

**Frontier Question:** Does the BT tree's prime-dependent branching — different $p$ produce different trees — imply that physical computation has a prime-indexed spectrum of "hardness classes"? That is, do computations instantiated on the $p=2$ tree (binary branching) have a different resource cost than those on $p=3$ (ternary) or $p=5$?
