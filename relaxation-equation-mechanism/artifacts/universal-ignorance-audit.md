## Phase 1 — Surface the Structure

**Q1. Scaffold detection — hidden assumptions holding this up?**
- S1: "Reddiger's formalism can host a concrete relaxation equation" — the project's load-bearing scaffold; Reddiger 2017/2026 must actually specify local random variables tightly enough to attach a dynamics.
- S2: "ε=1e-2 with 1e5 shots is a fair test" — statistical power: 1e5 shots gives σ ≈ 3e-3 per branch, so ε=1e-2 is ~3σ — adequate but not generous; pre-registered so no post-hoc widening.
- S3: "Born statistics is the correct target" — the framework claims to reproduce QM's empirical content, so Born-rule reproduction is the right bar; but weak-measurement trajectories (Hacohen-Gourgy & Martin 2020) may be the more discriminating target later.
- S4: "Wu et al. 2013 consistency is a meaningful constraint" — HHG spectra constrain trajectory ensembles in strong fields; whether that constrains the *measurement* relaxation family is unproven — the consistency claim may be weak (S4 is the scaffold most likely to be a decoy).

**Q2. Map–territory hygiene — where might the map be mistaken for the territory?**
- M1: The simulation is a MAP of the mechanism, not the mechanism. A PASS means "this parameterization reproduces Born statistics in silico" — not "nature relaxes this way."
- M2: ε=1e-2 vs "the Born rule" — the Born rule is not a single number; the test targets the standard 2-level measurement statistics. The map is the finite-shot histogram; the territory is the probability law.
- M3: "Radon–Nikodym level" is a formalism map; the territory is whether any physical dynamics corresponds. Reddiger's local random variables may be purely epistemic constructions with no dynamical correspondent.

**Q3. Wobble probe — where is the tension?**
- W1: The framework says relaxation is *continuous and deterministic*, yet Born statistics from deterministic flows require either (a) initial-condition distributions, (b) noise, or (c) effective stochasticity — the wobble is where the "deterministic" claim meets the need for spread.
- W2: The HSH text invokes Hardy (2001) for basins, but Hardy's axioms are about the *minimality* of quantum theory, not attractor dynamics — a possible category mismatch inherited from the seed text.
- W3: GRW/CSL models (Bassi–Ghirardi 2003) already implement relaxation-like dynamics with *stochastic* collapses; a deterministic variant must explain why noise is absent while statistics match — this is the deepest wobble.

## Phase 2 — Stress-Test the Frame

**Q4. Inversion — what if the opposite were true?**
- If CC-1 is FALSE (no admissible equation reproduces Born statistics within ε), then the verdict is published as a disconfirmation — the mechanism gap in RES.016 objection 2 stands, and the hydrodynamic re-grounding loses its one confirmed weakness as a research target (it becomes a refuted target). The project's value would be the *negative result*: a pre-registered, parameter-constrained demonstration that relaxation-into-eigenstates cannot reproduce Born statistics without auxiliary stochasticity — a contribution to the collapse-model literature regardless of PQS.
- If CC-1 is TRUE, the project provides the first explicit relaxation equation for the HSH program — a mechanism where the framework had none, with a reproducibility package.

**Q5. Falsifiability test — a world where CC-1 is false?**
- World: the candidate equation family (e.g., radial-basis attraction with scale parameters) either (a) cannot be expressed in Reddiger's formalism without violating the Kolmogorovian construction, or (b) needs more free parameters than independent constraints (overfitting trap), or (c) reproduces statistics only by smuggling in a de facto projection step. In that world CC-1 is false and the verdict says so, with the specific failure mode identified.

**Q6. Invariant extraction — what stays the same across frames?**
- Change the framework (PQS vs GRW vs Bohmian): the *test protocol* — pre-registered parameters, 2-level Born statistics, ε tolerance — stays identical. The invariant is the falsification harness, not the dynamics family.

## Phase 3 — Multiply Perspectives

**Q7. Radical perspectival shift — how would a different observer see it?**
- A collapse-model theorist (GRW/CSL camp): "This is a deterministic re-implementation of what we do stochastically — show me why the noise-free version doesn't violate the measurement-formalism constraints we already mapped." Their blind spot: they may dismiss the exercise as derivative; their value: the constraint table in Bassi–Ghirardi 2003 is the strongest prior art.

Their insight: initial-condition ensembles may be the natural source of Born statistics without a separate relaxation mechanism — an alternative CC-1 competitor.
- A metrologist: "I don't care about the framework — give me a simulation I can run that tells me whether this mechanism family can reproduce a probability law; that's a calibration problem."

**Q8. Externalized ignorance — who knows something I don't?**
