## 2. Minimum-Viable-Finding

**Structural isomorphism found (per domain):** every domain exhibits the same structure — *a rigorous probabilistic/kinematical framework that outsources its dynamics*:
- Measure theory: Reddiger's RN variables are defined *within* an imported unitary evolution ("the dynamics is borrowed from quantum mechanics").
- Hydrodynamic QM: the Madelung equations ARE dynamical, but their measurement status is unresolved (Wallstrom; no relaxation-to-eigenstates mechanism).
- Collapse models: GRW/CSL supply dynamics but only stochastically; a deterministic relaxation is unconstructed.
- Foundations: the projection postulate is treated as conditional probability (Reddiger Part III) or as an axiom (von Neumann) — never as a derived relaxation.
- Strong field: Wu et al. 2013 demonstrate trajectory-level determinism *within* the unitary dynamics — consistent with either resolution.

**Meta-principle (invariant across all translations):** *the missing piece is a dynamics that produces the Born rule endogenously — no verified framework supplies it, so the project constructs or refutes it.*
