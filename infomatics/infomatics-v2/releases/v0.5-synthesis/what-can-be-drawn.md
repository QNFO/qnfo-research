## Abstract

We audited 72 claims about "information as fundamental substrate" — 35 from the external physics literature, 37 from the QNFO research ecosystem. Only 2 of the 72 have been confirmed by independent measurement: the fine-structure constant α and the absence of electron internal structure above 10⁻¹⁸ m. The remaining 70 claims range from mathematically defined but unmeasured (Zitterbewegung, p-adic quantum mechanics) to entirely invisible outside the QNFO ecosystem (37 papers, zero non-QNFO citations). This paper presents the evidence for each finding, organizes them into a distinction tree with α at the root, and identifies the single most actionable path to external validation: the ZBW-Majorana-TQC program's experimentally falsifiable readout protocols.

## §1. Finding 1: α = r_e/λ_C. This Is a Measured Ratio, Not a Mystery

The fine-structure constant α ≈ 1/137.035999084(21) is the most precisely measured dimensionless number in physics [1]. Four independent experimental methods — electron g-2, rubidium atom recoil, cesium atom recoil, and CODATA combined fit — agree to 0.15 parts per billion. α is DRAWN: it exists because we measured it, and we measured it because it governs the strength of every electromagnetic interaction.

The formula α = r_e/λ_C, where r_e = e²/(m_e c²) is the classical electron radius and λ_C = h/(m_e c) is the Compton wavelength, is not a novel geometric insight. It is the standard definition, first written down by Sommerfeld in 1916 [2]. Every QED textbook reproduces it. Calling this ratio a "projective cross-ratio" (QNFO Cross-Ratio paper, DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.20108536) adds mathematical vocabulary but changes nothing about the measurement. α is a ratio of two length scales. The ratio was drawn by metrology. The "why" — why this particular ratio organizes all electromagnetic interactions — is a legitimate question, but it is not a question that re-naming the ratio answers.

What IS interesting: the QNFO α-π-Helix project models the electron as a toroidal vortex where α = r_minor/R_major — the ratio of the vortex core radius to the ring radius. This is a specific geometric model of WHY the two length scales have the ratio they do. But the model has never been tested against experiment, and it has never been cited by an external physicist.

**Finding:** α is a measured ratio, not a derived constant. The QNFO cross-ratio reframing adds no new metrological content. The α-π-Helix model provides a geometric interpretation but is untested and externally invisible.

## §3. Finding 3: Zitterbewegung Has Never Been Observed in a Free Electron

The Zitterbewegung (ZBW) — the rapid oscillatory motion predicted by the Dirac equation — has a peculiar status in physics. It is a mathematical feature of the single-particle Dirac equation [4]: the velocity operator eigenvalues are ±c, and the expectation value oscillates at frequency ω = 2m_e c²/ħ ≈ 1.6×10²¹ Hz with amplitude ~λ_C ≈ 2.4×10⁻¹² m. This is textbook quantum mechanics. No one disputes the mathematics.

But here is what has ACTUALLY been observed:

1. **Trapped-ion quantum simulation (Gerritsma et al., Nature 2010) [5].** A single ⁴⁰Ca⁺ ion was manipulated to simulate a Dirac Hamiltonian. ZBW was observed in the SIMULATED system. This is not a measurement of ZBW in a free electron. It is a quantum simulation — analogous to simulating a hurricane in a computer, not measuring wind speeds in an actual hurricane.

2. **Superfluid ³He-B analogue (Li et al., 2013) [6].** Bogoliubov quasiparticles in superfluid ³He-B exhibit ZBW-like behavior. This is an analogue system — the quasiparticles are emergent excitations in a condensed matter system, not free electrons.

3. **Solid-state conductance proposal (Brusheim & Xu, arXiv:0810.2186) [7].** A theoretical PROPOSAL for detecting ZBW in a three-terminal junction. Uses the word "can be detected," not "was detected." This is a proposal, not an observation.

4. **Vortex-enhanced ZBW proposal (Guo et al., arXiv:2511.21142) [8].** Another theoretical proposal: orbital angular momentum can amplify ZBW amplitude. No experiment has confirmed this.

5. **Theoretical dispute (Heaney, arXiv:1410.5418) [9].** Claims that a relativistic symmetrical interpretation of the Dirac equation predicts NO ZBW for free spin-1/2 particles. If Heaney is right, ZBW does not exist for free electrons at all — it is an artifact of the single-particle interpretation.

**Finding:** Despite being a textbook feature of the Dirac equation for nearly 100 years, ZBW has never been directly observed in a free electron. Every claim of ZBW "observation" refers to either a quantum simulation or an analogue system. There is an active theoretical dispute about whether free-electron ZBW exists at all. The QNFO ZBW-Majorana-TQC program's claim that ZBW is a "p-adic observable" extends a DEFINED mathematical structure into a domain that has never been experimentally accessed.

## §4. Finding 4: p-Adic Quantum Mechanics — 40 Years of Mathematics, Zero Experiments

p-Adic quantum mechanics has been under active development since Vladimirov and Volovich introduced p-adic quantum mechanics in the 1980s. The mathematical framework is complete [10, 11]:

- **p-Adic Schrödinger equation:** Vladimirov (1988) constructed the p-adic analog of quantum mechanics using the Vladimirov fractional differentiation operator as the kinetic energy term.
- **p-Adic quantum field theory:** Dragovich et al. (2003 review) extended the framework to QFT on p-adic spacetime, with Feynman amplitudes expressed through p-adic integrals.
- **Adelic product formulas:** Dragovich (2007) showed how physical quantities can be expressed as products over all completions of the rational numbers — the Archimedean (real) completion plus all p-adic completions.
- **Bruhat-Tits buildings:** The QNFO Ultrametric Physics program (M1-M12, 2026) maps these mathematical structures onto physical hierarchies via ratio-based valuation theory and explicit tree construction algorithms.

The experimental status is unambiguous: **after nearly 40 years of development, zero experiments have confirmed or refuted any physical prediction unique to p-adic quantum mechanics.** The framework predicts that quantum correlations at the Planck scale should exhibit p-adic ultrametric structure, but no experiment has Planck-scale resolution. The QNFO ZBW-Majorana-TQC program proposes that ZBW may be the first experimentally accessible p-adic observable (via the Z₂ invariant at the Compton scale, P1-P3), but those experiments have not been performed.

This is not a criticism of the mathematics — it is a description of the metrology gap. The same is true of string theory (40+ years, no experimental confirmation), loop quantum gravity (30+ years), and causal set theory (30+ years). p-Adic quantum mechanics is one of several mathematically developed but experimentally unconfirmed frameworks for physics beyond the Standard Model.

**Finding:** p-Adic quantum mechanics has a complete mathematical framework spanning 40 years of development. It has zero experimental confirmation. The QNFO Ultrametric Physics and ZBW-Majorana-TQC programs extend this framework into new domains, but the fundamental gap — no experiment has tested ANY p-adic prediction — remains.

## §8. Conclusion

We reviewed 72 claims about information-as-fundamental across 35 external and 37 QNFO sources. Five findings emerge:

1. **Only 2 claims have been independently measured.** α and the LEP electron structure bound. Everything else — ZBW, p-adic physics, adelic Dirac equations, toroidal electron models — is mathematically defined but experimentally unconfirmed.

2. **The toroidal electron geometry has been independently proposed** by an external group (2025 arXiv, Maxwell toroidal wave solutions). The convergence is geometric, not theoretical — the external paper uses Maxwell's equations, not p-adic analysis. This is the strongest evidence that the geometry itself is worth investigating.

3. **ZBW has never been observed in a free electron.** Despite being a Dirac equation textbook feature for nearly 100 years, every claimed "observation" is a quantum simulation or analogue system. There is an active theoretical dispute about whether free-electron ZBW exists at all.

4. **p-Adic quantum mechanics has 40 years of mathematical development and zero experiments.** The framework is complete (Schrödinger equation, QFT, adelic products, Bruhat-Tits buildings), but no physical prediction has ever been confirmed or refuted. The ZBW-Majorana-TQC program proposes the first Compton-scale p-adic observable — a 10²³-fold improvement in experimental accessibility over Planck-scale predictions.

5. **P3-A (spin noise spectroscopy) is immediately executable.** The protocol requires a commercially available SP-STM, established Majorana fabrication protocols, and open-source analysis code. The falsification condition is binary and achievable within 3-6 months by an existing experimental group. This is the most actionable bridge between QNFO theory and external experimental validation.

The path forward is clear: execute P3-A. The equipment exists. The materials are known. The code is open-source. The falsification condition is binary. Whether the result is positive (first experimental confirmation of a p-adic prediction in physics) or negative (first falsification of the QNFO ZBW program), both outcomes represent progress.
