# The Informational Universe

**Status:** Phase 0 — Project Initialization  
**Last Active:** 2026-07-21  
**Domain:** QWAV Physics / Quantum Foundations  
**Protocol:** research v2.4

## Overview

A research program exploring the hypothesis that information is the fundamental substrate of physical reality. This project investigates whether spacetime, matter, and quantum behavior emerge from underlying information-theoretic principles, potentially reconciling quantum mechanics with general relativity through an information-first ontology.

## Core Claim

The universe is fundamentally an informational structure. Physical law, particles, fields, and geometry are epiphenomena of deeper information-processing constraints — not primitive entities.
