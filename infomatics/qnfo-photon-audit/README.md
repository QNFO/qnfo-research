## Overview

This repository contains the research artifacts, documents, and deliverables for a technical audit of claims that photons constitute a pre-geometric information substrate more fundamental than 3D spacetime, with electrons as "knotted" or self-trapped photonic configurations.

The project began as a visionary essay (Doc A), was subjected to methodological critique (Docs B/D), experimental test design (Doc C), and a comprehensive cross-audit (Doc E). The core claim has been reformulated from a logically invalid non-sequitur into a testable hypothesis.

### Reformulated Core Claim

> The informational properties of quantum fields suggest that geometric spacetime may be emergent in some quantum gravity frameworks — and photons, as the only freely-propagating massless boson with a natural two-state (qubit) polarization structure, are the natural elementary information carriers in such a substrate.
