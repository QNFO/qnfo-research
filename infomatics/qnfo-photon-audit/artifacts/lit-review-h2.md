## 1. Research Question

## 4. Critical Assessment

### 4a. RT Formula and AdS/CFT
The RT formula relates entanglement entropy on the boundary to minimal surface area in the bulk. This is the strongest known example of "geometry from entanglement." However:
- **Requires pre-existing AdS spacetime**: The RT formula derives bulk geometry from boundary entanglement, but it assumes AdS/CFT duality is already valid. It does not show how spacetime emerges from entanglement ab initio.
- **No photonic connection**: AdS/CFT relates gauge theory (boundary) to gravity (bulk). There is no natural role for photons as a pre-geometric substrate in this framework.

### 4b. MERA and Tensor Networks
MERA is the closest known model to "space from entanglement": the tensor network's hierarchical structure geometrically resembles AdS. However:
- **Works in 1+1 dimensions primarily**: Extensions to 3+1 are speculative.
- **Requires critical quantum systems**: MERA applies to ground states of critical Hamiltonians — not to arbitrary photonic configurations.
- **No mechanism for photons specifically**: The "emergent dimension" in MERA is a computational structure, not a physical photon field.

### 4c. Causal Set Theory
Causal set theory derives spacetime from discrete causal relations (partially ordered sets). It is genuinely "pre-geometric." However:
- **No photon-substrate mechanism**: Causal sets are abstract discrete elements, not photons.
- **The "sprinkling" problem**: How discrete elements produce a smooth manifold is unresolved.
- **Photons are emergent, not fundamental**: In causal sets, photons (and all particles) are excitations on an emergent manifold — the opposite of the photonic-substrate claim.

### 4d. Quantum Graphity
Quantum graphity models spacetime as a dynamical graph with links representing adjacency. It is pre-geometric and involves "graph → manifold" emergence. However:
- **No photonic mechanism**: The graph's links are abstract, not photonic.
- **Static-regime limitation**: Most models produce static geometries; dynamical spacetime emergence is an open problem.

## 5. Verdict for Photonic-Substrate Hypothesis

**None of the four approaches (RT, MERA, causal sets, quantum graphity) provides a mechanism for photons specifically to constitute a pre-geometric substrate.**

- RT/MERA: too bound to AdS/CFT; photons are boundary gauge fields, not fundamental substrate elements.
- Causal sets: genuinely pre-geometric but photons are emergent, not fundamental.
- Quantum graphity: pre-geometric graph but no photonic content.

**The photonic-substrate claim is orthogonal to these approaches.** It makes a stronger claim: that the pre-geometric elements are specifically photonic (electromagnetic), not abstract discrete elements, tensor network nodes, or causal set elements. None of the holographic-entanglement literature makes or supports this claim.

### Gap
If the hypothesis means "the TREE structure is like a MERA network and photons are the boundary degrees of freedom," this is a loose analogy, not a mapping. No paper in this literature identifies photons as the fundamental elements of the pre-geometric structure.

### Falsifiability
The hypothesis would be disconfirmed if any model of emergent spacetime (RT, MERA, causal sets, quantum graphity) can be shown to be incompatible with a photonic-substrate mapping — i.e., if it is proven that pre-geometric elements cannot carry electromagnetic charge without presupposing spacetime.
