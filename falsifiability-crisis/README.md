# Falsifiability Crisis in Contemporary Physics

**WBS: QNFO.RES.001 | Branch: res/paper/falsifiability-crisis**

A critique of the de-facto unfalsifiability of contemporary physics frameworks:

1. **ΛCDM shell game** — GR + dark matter + dark energy + inflation creates a protective belt that absorbs anomalies
2. **Standard Model's 19 free parameters** — masses, mixing angles, and couplings are inputs, not predictions
3. **Eddington 1919** — the founding "confirmation" was methodologically compromised
4. **Monopoly on precision** — no competing framework makes predictions at comparable rigor
5. **Independent consilience** — the only epistemologically sound path forward (KIF-60 Bayesian Evidential Weight Gate)
