## Minimum-Viable-Finding

FIVE structural isomorphisms across domains:

1. **ΛCDM ≈ Lakatosian Protective Belt:** Dark matter profiles, dark energy equation of state, and inflationary parameters function exactly as Lakatos's protective belt — absorbing anomalies while preserving the hard core (GR's Einstein field equations). This is not a metaphor; it is a structural identity.

2. **SM's 19 free parameters ≈ Overfitted model:** In statistical learning theory, a model with 19 free parameters measured (not predicted) from data has zero generalization error guarantee. The SM's "predictions" are retrodictions on the data used to fit those 19 parameters.

3. **Eddington 1919 ≈ Confirmation bias case study:** The founding "confirmation" of GR shows the same structural pattern as modern GR tests: subjective data selection (plate rejection), confirmation-biased interpretation, and no pre-registered alternative hypothesis to falsify.

4. **Monopoly on calculability ≈ No test possible:** When no competing framework produces predictions at comparable precision in a regime, the structure of "testing" collapses into "measuring." This is an epistemological finding, not a physics one.

5. **Independent consilience ≈ Δlog-odds > 0:** The ONLY way to break the measurement-exercise trap is independent measurement traditions with distinct systematic error budgets, pre-registered falsification conditions, and a competing alternative hypothesis that makes a DIFFERENT quantitative prediction.

## Synthesis Consilience

**Meta-Principle:** A framework with no serious competitor cannot be falsified — it can only be measured. The structure is identical across GR/ΛCDM, SM, and many other established paradigms: monopoly on calculability → tests become measurements → auxiliary hypotheses absorb anomalies → zero empirical content.

**Frontier Question:** Can we build a pre-registration infrastructure for physics that makes the Δlog-odds gate operational? What institutional incentives would be needed for physicists to pre-register falsification conditions and fund competing frameworks?
