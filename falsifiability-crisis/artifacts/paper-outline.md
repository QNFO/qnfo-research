## Abstract (Target: 200 words)

Contemporary fundamental physics has become de-facto unfalsifiable -- not because individual theories are inherently untestable, but because the structural conditions for falsification are absent. General Relativity embedded in LCDM constitutes a Lakatosian protective belt: dark matter profiles, dark energy equation of state, and inflationary potentials absorb all anomalies while preserving Einstein's field equations. The Standard Model's nineteen free parameters are measured inputs, not theoretical predictions -- making the framework accommodationist rather than genuinely predictive. The founding confirmation of GR, Eddington's 1919 eclipse expedition, was methodologically compromised by subjective plate selection and confirmation-biased interpretation. Subsequent tests of GR are structurally measurement exercises, not falsification attempts, because no competing framework predicts at comparable precision -- creating a monopoly on calculability that converts every "test" into parameter-fitting. The solution is methodological, not physical: independent consilience of evidence across measurement traditions with distinct systematic error budgets, pre-registered falsification conditions, and a competing hypothesis that makes a different quantitative prediction. This is operationalized through the Bayesian deltalog-odds gate (log[P(O|T)/P(O|not-T)]), which distinguishes genuine risky predictions from post-hoc accommodation.

## 1. Introduction: The Falsifiability Gradient (800 words)

- **Opening:** Physics is currently in a methodological crisis. Not a crisis of failed predictions (the predictions are fine), but a crisis of unfalsifiability -- the inability to construct tests that could genuinely disconfirm established frameworks.
- **The falsifiability gradient:** Define the set of observations O such that P(O | framework to be nearly 0). A framework that can accommodate ANY observation through auxiliary hypotheses, free parameters, or reclassification has zero empirical content.
- **Thesis preview:** Five structural patterns that produce this gradient in contemporary physics: (1) the LCDM protective belt, (2) the SM's accommodationist parameter space, (3) the Eddington founding-confirmation pattern, (4) the monopoly-on-calculability trap, (5) the solution: independent consilience + Bayesian deltalog-odds.

## 2. LCDM and the Lakatosian Protective Belt (1,200 words)

- **Lakatos's framework:** Hard core (irrefutable by methodological decision) + protective belt (auxiliary hypotheses that protect the core).
- **LCDM's structure:** Hard core = Einstein field equations. Protective belt = dark matter density profiles, dark energy equation of state w, inflationary potential V(phi), baryonic feedback prescriptions, reionization models.
- **How anomalies are absorbed:** Galaxy rotation curves too fast? Adjust dark matter profile. Universe expansion accelerating? Add dark energy (adjust w). CMB too uniform? Add inflation (adjust potential shape). Dwarf galaxy "core-cusp" problem? Adjust baryonic feedback.
- **Each knob turn preserves the core.** LCDM has NEVER been at genuine risk of rejection because the adjustable components take the hit.
- **Engage with Merritt (2017), Smeenk (2017).** Show that this is not just a conceptual analogy -- it is a documented structural pattern in cosmological model-building.

## 3. The Standard Model's Nineteen Free Parameters (1,200 words)

- **The parameter count:** 19 parameters (9 fermion masses, 4 CKM parameters, 2 Higgs parameters, 3 gauge couplings, 1 QCD theta angle -- modulo the strong CP problem). All are measured, not derived.
- **Prediction vs. accommodation:** The SM DID make genuine pre-registered predictions: the W and Z boson masses, the top quark mass, the Higgs boson. But these are 3-4 of 19. The remaining 15-16 were post-dicted -- fitted to measurement after the fact.
- **The Higgs mass "prediction":** The allowed range was 114 GeV (LEP lower bound) to ~1 TeV (unitarity bound). ANY mass in this range would have been celebrated as "confirmation." This is not prediction -- it is parameter accommodation.
- **The anomaly pattern:** Muon g-2 discrepancy? "Systematics." B-meson flavor anomalies? "Disappeared with more data." SUSY at TeV? "Goalposts moved." WIMPs? "Cross-section limits pushed down." Proton decay? "Lifetime limits extended."
- **Engage with Hossenfelder (C4), PDG (S9).** The accommodationist structure is not a fringe critique -- it is documented in the standard reference works.

## 4. Eddington 1919: The Founding Confirmation as Methodology Failure (1,000 words)

- **The textbook story:** Eddington's eclipse expedition confirmed GR's prediction of 1.75 arcseconds of starlight deflection versus Newton's 0.87 arcseconds.
- **The methodological reality:** Eddington's plates showed deflections ranging from 0.86 to 2.16 arcseconds. The plates closest to Newtonian predictions (0.87 arcseconds) were DISCARDED for "systematic errors." Eddington was a committed Einsteinian who had written a treatise on relativity before the expedition.
- **Earman & Glymour (1980) and Kennefick (2009)** document the subjective judgment in plate selection. The confirmation was methodologically compromised.
- **The significance:** This is not "GR was wrong." GR gives the right answer. The point is epistemological: the founding confirmation of the most tested theory in physics was a subjective data-selection exercise, not a clean falsification of Newtonian gravity. The PATTERN established at the founding continues: subsequent tests are measurement exercises within the GR framework, not competitive tests between GR and a genuine alternative.

## 5. The Monopoly-on-Calculability Trap (1,000 words)

- **The structural problem:** In regimes where only ONE framework produces predictions at high precision, "testing" collapses into "measuring." There is no alternative prediction that could falsify the framework.
- **Pulsar binary orbital decay:** GR predicts dP/dt precisely. No other gravity theory makes a calculation at this precision. If the measurement deviates, is GR wrong? Or is there an undetected third body? The auxiliary hypothesis always wins -- because there is no competing prediction to create a genuine test.
- **Gravitational wave ringdown:** GR's no-hair theorem predicts a specific ringdown waveform. If echoes appear, is GR falsified? Or are there "unknown systematics"? Without a competing framework predicting a specific echo pattern at specific SNR, the interpretation defaults to "more data needed."
- **The monopoly pattern:** String theory in high-energy theory (Smolin 2006). LCDM in cosmology. GR in strong-field gravity. In each case, the dominant framework's precision advantage BECOMES its unfalsifiability -- not despite its precision, but because of it.
- **Engage Smolin (C5), Dawid (C6), Carroll (S11).** Acknowledge that "precision is a virtue" and that the monopoly has a legitimate epistemic basis. Distinguish the descriptive claim (monopoly prevents falsification) from the normative claim (this is bad).

## 6. Independent Consilience: The Only Way Forward (1,200 words)

- **Whewell's consilience:** "The evidence in favour of our induction is of a much higher and more forcible character when it enables us to explain and determine cases of a KIND DIFFERENT from those which were contemplated in the formation of our hypothesis" (Whewell 1840).
- **Why it's the only solution:** Single-stream confirmation with shared systematics carries near-zero evidential weight. Only when MULTIPLE independent measurement traditions, with DISTINCT systematic error budgets, converge on the same conclusion under pre-registered falsification conditions does evidence become genuinely constraining.
- **The Bayesian deltalog-odds gate (KIF-60):** For any claimed correspondence between theory T and observation O: deltalog-odds = log[P(O|T)/P(O|not-T)]. If P(O|not-T) to nearly equal 1 (O was already known; T was built around it), deltalog-odds to nearly equal 0 -- zero evidential weight. If P(O|not-T) << 1 (O is genuinely surprising), deltalog-odds >> 0 -- genuine risky prediction.
- **Three concrete tests:** (1) Pre-registration -- prediction MUST be stated before observational access. (2) Falsifiability condition -- at least one observation that WOULD kill the framework, stated in advance. (3) Surprise accounting -- estimate P(match | random structure) under the null.
- **The tautology trap:** A framework with more free parameters than independent matches explains everything = explains nothing. Overfitting, cherry-picking, absorption -- the three failure modes and how the deltalog-odds gate catches them.
- **Engage Jaynes (B8) for Bayesian foundation.** Acknowledge that deltalog-odds is QUALITATIVE for framework-level claims (P(O|not-T) over an infinite hypothesis space is underconstrained). The gate provides order-of-magnitude distinctions, not precise numbers.
