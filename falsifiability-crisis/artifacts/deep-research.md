## Stage 0: Domain Assessment

### Field: Meta-Physics Methodology Critique

**Active paradigms:**
1. **Popperian falsifiability** — the standard demarcation criterion, widely cited but inconsistently applied in frontier physics.
2. **Lakatosian research programmes** — the "hard core + protective belt" model that maps cleanly onto GR+LCDM.
3. **Non-empirical theory assessment (Dawid)** — the position that theoretical virtues can substitute for empirical test in frontier regimes. THIS PAPER CHALLENGES THIS PARADIGM.
4. **Kuhnian normal science** — the "measurement within paradigm" model that describes the current state of fundamental physics.

**Key questions the paper addresses:**
1. Is GR+LCDM structurally falsifiable, or is it a Lakatosian protective belt?
2. Are the SM's 19 free parameters accommodated or predicted?
3. Does Eddington 1919 hold up under methodological scrutiny?
4. Does monopoly on calculability prevent falsification?
5. Can independent consilience + deltalog-odds solve the problem?

**Positioning:** This paper sits at the intersection of physics, philosophy of science, and epistemology. It is not a physics paper making new predictions — it is a meta-critique of how physics produces (or fails to produce) genuine predictions.

## Stage 3: Red-Team Challenge (5 Adversary Positions)

### Adversary 1: Null-Hypothesis Defender
**Position:** "Physics is working fine. GR has passed every test. The SM made correct predictions (W/Z bosons, top quark, Higgs). You're mistaking a paradigm's maturity for unfalsifiability."

**Response:** Distinguish "making correct predictions" from "being falsifiable." GR+LCDM has NEVER been at genuine risk of rejection in its current form because auxiliary components absorb every anomaly. The SM DID make genuine predictions (W/Z, top, Higgs were all pre-registered mass targets) — but those were 3 of 19 parameters. The other 16 were post-dicted. The paper must be SPECIFIC about which SM predictions were genuine and which were accommodation.

**Score:** The null-hypothesis defender has a valid point about historical SM predictions. The paper must acknowledge these while arguing they are the exception, not the rule.

### Adversary 2: Methodology Skeptic
**Position:** "Your 'falsifiability gradient' is itself unfalsifiable. How do you measure P(O|T) when T is a framework as broad as GR or SM? The deltalog-odds framework assumes precise priors that don't exist."

**Response:** The deltalog-odds framework does not require precise numerical values — it requires ORDER-OF-MAGNITUDE distinctions. "P(O|framework) ≈ 1" vs "P(O|framework) ≈ 0" is sufficient. The framework's own falsifiability condition: if a paper demonstrates that a NOVEL observation (pre-registered, not post-hoc) FALSIFIES a framework where the monopoly pattern held, that falsifies C4. The recursive test works.

**Score:** Valid critique. The paper must emphasize that deltalog-odds is a QUALITATIVE gate, not a precise quantitative tool.

### Adversary 3: Better-Alternative Proposer
**Position:** "Instead of meta-critique, why not propose a genuine alternative to GR? MOND partially fills this role at galaxy scales. TeVeS, f(R) gravity, and others exist. Aren't you ignoring existing alternatives?"

**Response:** The existing alternatives all fail at scales where LCDM succeeds (CMB, large-scale structure). MOND is falsified at cosmological scales. No alternative makes predictions at <1% precision for any strong-field observable. The POINT of the paper is that the monopoly exists BECAUSE alternatives fail — but their failure at one scale shouldn't immunize LCDM at all scales.

**Score:** The paper must engage with existing alternatives (MOND, TeVeS, f(R)) specifically, not dismiss them generically.

### Adversary 4: Scaling Pessimist
**Position:** "Institutional change of the kind you propose (pre-registration infrastructure, funding for competing frameworks) is politically impossible. The physics community's incentives are aligned toward paradigm conservation. This is a 'should' paper, not a 'will' paper."

**Response:** Concede the institutional pessimism. This is a NORMATIVE paper — it argues what SHOULD happen, not what WILL happen. The calibration register's [CHECK: 2030] entries are aspirational, not predictive. Acknowledge the sociological constraint explicitly.

**Score:** Valid. The paper must clearly separate "if adopted" (normative) from "will happen" (predictive). The calibration register entries should be conditioned on institutional change that may not occur.

### Adversary 5: Resource Realist
**Position:** "Funding competing gravity frameworks to <1% precision at strong-field scales would cost billions of dollars (new space-based GW detectors, larger radio telescope arrays, dedicated computing clusters). This is 'fund alternatives to GR' as a mantra, not a budget."

**Response:** Concede the resource constraint. The paper's prescription is for METHODOLOGICAL change (pre-registration, falsification conditions, independent measurement traditions), not necessarily for NEW INSTRUMENTS. Existing data can be re-analyzed under competing frameworks if those frameworks are developed. The paper DOES NOT require billion-dollar new experiments — it requires changing how we evaluate evidence.

**Score:** Partially valid. The paper must clarify that it advocates evaluation practices, not new hardware.

## Stage 7: Strategic Memo

### Executive Summary
Contemporary fundamental physics — GR embedded in LCDM and the Standard Model — has become structurally unfalsifiable. LCDM's auxiliary components (dark matter profiles, dark energy equation of state, inflationary parameters) function as a Lakatosian protective belt that absorbs all anomalies while preserving the hard core (Einstein's field equations). The SM's 19 free parameters are measured inputs, not theoretical predictions — making the framework accommodationist rather than predictive. The founding "confirmation" of GR (Eddington 1919) was methodologically compromised by biased plate selection. Subsequent tests are structurally measurement exercises, not falsification attempts, because no competing framework produces predictions at comparable precision — a monopoly on calculability. The only epistemologically sound solution is independent consilience across measurement traditions with distinct systematic error budgets, pre-registered falsification conditions, and a competing alternative hypothesis — operationalized through the deltalog-odds Bayesian gate (KIF-60).

### Key Findings
1. The protective-belt structure is NOT a criticism of GR — it is a DESCRIPTION of LCDM. The auxiliary components absorb anomalies by design.
2. The SM's 19 parameters are "free" in the specific sense that they are post-dicted from data, not pre-registered predictions. The Higgs mass range was 114 GeV to 1 TeV — any mass in that range would have been "confirmed."
3. Eddington's plate selection was subjective; the Newtonian-plate data was discarded. The founding confirmation of GR was methodologically weaker than the textbook narrative.
4. Monopoly on calculability structurally prevents falsification — not because the framework is wrong, but because there is no way to construct a test that discriminates between "GR is right" and "we have no alternative."

### Risk Assessment
- **Carroll/Dawid rebuttal:** If non-empirical assessment is genuine evidence, the paper's entire positive program collapses. This is the foundational risk.
- **Institutional inertia:** The paper's normative prescriptions are politically infeasible in the current funding landscape. This does not make them wrong.
- **Self-referential falsifiability:** The paper's own deltalog-odds gate must pass its own test. Phase 1 gate execution confirmed all 5 claims pass (deltalog-odds > 0), but the epistemic vs. normative distinction remains vulnerable.

## Stage 8: Cross-Review (Same-Model Consistency Check)

### Consistency Verification
**Claim coherence:** C1-C4 are descriptive claims about the STATE of physics. C5 is a normative prescription. The paper must clearly separate these.
**No contradiction between claims:** C1 (LCDM protective belt) + C4 (monopoly = no-test) are complementary — the protective belt is what MAKES the monopoly structurally unfalsifiable.
**Blind spot: institutional solutions unspecified.** The paper argues that independent consilience is "the only solution" but does not propose institutional mechanisms for achieving it. This is a legitimate scope limitation for a methodology paper — but it leaves the "how" question unanswered.

### Blind-Spot Identification
1. The paper does not engage with the possibility that FALSIFIABILITY ITSELF is a historically contingent value. Kuhn's paradigm shift model suggests that paradigms change for sociological reasons, not because they are falsified. If Kuhn is right, the paper is prescribing a model of science that has never actually operated.
2. The paper assumes that pre-registration infrastructure is politically possible. It provides no path to institutionalization.
3. The paper's Bayesian gate (KIF-60) assumes deltalog-odds can be meaningfully estimated. For framework-level claims (GR vs. all possible alternatives), the denominator P(O|not-T) requires averaging over an infinite hypothesis space. The paper's qualitative use (order-of-magnitude distinctions only) is a simplification, not a solution.
