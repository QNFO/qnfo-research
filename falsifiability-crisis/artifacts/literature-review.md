## KIF-18 Mandatory Symmetry Template (HARD)

### Where External Literature Supports the Paper's Claims

**Claim 1 (LCDM as protective belt):** Lakatos (C1, 1970) defines the precise structure that LCDM instantiates: a hard core (Einstein field equations) surrounded by a protective belt of auxiliary hypotheses (dark matter profiles, dark energy w, inflationary potentials). Merritt (S2, 2017) explicitly argues that cosmological parameters function as convention-absorbers. Smeenk (S3, 2017) documents how LCDM's auxiliary components protect the core from anomaly. Butterfield (S10, 1998) provides the Duhem-Quine philosophical foundation: isolated theory testing is impossible without auxiliary hypotheses.

**Claim 2 (SM accommodationism):** Hossenfelder (C4, 2018) thoroughly documents how the SM's 19 free parameters are measured, not predicted, and how anomalies are absorbed by parameteer adjustment. The PDG (S9, annual) provides the factual basis: 19 parameters, all measured. Weinberg (S5, 1992) acknowledges the parameter problem, though he frames it as a puzzle rather than an epistemic failure.

**Claim 3 (Eddington 1919 implosion):** Earman & Glymour (C2, 1980) is the canonical critique — demonstrating that plates giving Newtonian results were discarded and that Eddington's theoretical commitment determined data interpretation. Kennefick (C3, 2009) corroborates with additional historical detail. Together they establish that the founding "confirmation" of GR was methodologically compromised.

**Claim 4 (Monopoly on calculability):** Smolin (C5, 2006) documents how string theory's monopoly on theoretical physics funding suppressed alternatives — the institutional dimension of the monopoly pattern. Dawid (C6, 2013) provides the most sophisticated DEFENSE of non-empirical theory assessment, making explicit the logic this paper challenges. Perovic (S12, 2011) describes the experimenter's regress mechanism that makes monopoly frameworks immune to falsification.

**Claim 5 (Independent consilience solution):** Whewell (C8, 1840) originates the consilience concept. Jaynes (B8, 2003) provides the Bayesian framework this paper operationalizes. The combination — Whewell's consilience + Jaynes's Bayesian inference — is this paper's unique contribution.

### Where External Literature Constrains or Contradicts the Paper's Claims

**Constraint on Claim 1:** Dawid (C6, 2013) provides a sophisticated defense of non-empirical theory assessment — arguing that theoretical virtues (explanatory power, consistency, elegance) CAN provide evidence in the absence of empirical test. This paper must engage with Dawid's framework, not dismiss it. The rebuttal: theoretical virtues are not Bayesian evidence unless P(O|T) > P(O|¬T) — and Dawid's framework provides no mechanism for computing this ratio. His "non-empirical confirmation" is [UNFALSIFIABLE] by construction.

**Constraint on Claim 2:** The SM's 19 parameters are indeed measured, not predicted — but this is the NORMAL state of science during paradigm consolidation, not a unique failure. Kuhn (B1, 1962) documents that ALL paradigms accumulate auxiliary parameters during normal science. The paper's critique applies equally to ALL mature scientific frameworks — which either means the critique is too broad (attacking science itself) or that the SM is a special case requiring a different argument. This paper must establish the boundary.

**Constraint on Claim 3:** Kennefick (C3, 2009) also documents that Eddington's overall conclusion was CORRECT — GR gives the right answer. The methodological flaws do not mean GR was wrong. The paper's argument is about the EPISTEMIC STATUS of the evidence at the time, not about whether GR is true. This distinction must be made explicit.

**Constraint on Claim 4:** The monopoly argument has a counter: precision IS a virtue. If GR makes predictions at 0.01% accuracy and no alternative does, there is a genuine scientific reason to prefer GR — not just institutional inertia. Carroll (S11, 2018) argues that falsifiability is too narrow a criterion for frontier physics. The paper must distinguish between "monopoly prevents falsification" (epistemic claim) and "monopoly is unjustified" (normative claim).

**Constraint on Claim 5:** Consilience is observational, not experimental. Whewell's original examples (Newton's theory unifying terrestrial and celestial mechanics) were post-hoc consilience — the theory existed FIRST, and consilience was recognized AFTER. The paper's proposal of pre-registered falsification conditions is a DEPARTURE from Whewell's concept, not a direct application. This must be acknowledged: the paper is INNOVATING on Whewell, not simply reviving him.

**Contradiction (Carroll 2018):** Carroll (S11) explicitly rejects falsifiability as the demarcation criterion and argues that "normal science" can proceed without it. If Carroll is correct, this entire paper is wrong. The rebuttal: Carroll's framework (like Dawid's) fails the Bayesian evidential weight gate — P(O|¬T) ≈ 1 for all "confirmations" in his framework → Δlog-odds = 0 → no evidence. The disagreement is not about whether falsifiability matters — it is about what counts as evidence.

**Contradiction (Milgrom/MOND):** The existence of MOND (S7) as a competing framework at galaxy scales partially undermines the monopoly claim — there IS a genuine test between MOND and LCDM at galaxy scales. However, at cosmological scales (CMB, large-scale structure), MOND fails and LCDM is the only precise framework — so the monopoly pattern holds where precision is highest, which is exactly the point.

## Synthesis

The literature supports the paper's five-point thesis while constraining its scope and forcing precision in its claims. The paper must:

1. **Acknowledge Dawid and Carroll** — they are the most sophisticated defenders of the position this paper attacks. Engaging with them strengthens the argument.
2. **Bound the critique** — not all science is unfalsifiable; this is about a specific structural pattern (monopoly on calculability) in a specific domain (fundamental physics).
3. **Distinguish the epistemic from the normative** — "GR is unfalsifiable" (epistemic) ≠ "this is bad" (normative). The paper must separate the descriptive analysis from the prescriptive proposal.
4. **Innovate honestly** — the Bayesian consilience operationalization is a new contribution, not a revival of Whewell. Credit the innovation while honoring the lineage.
