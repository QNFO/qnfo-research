# PROJECT-PLAN: Falsifiability Crisis in Contemporary Physics

## Charter

This paper critiques the de-facto unfalsifiability of contemporary physics frameworks — General Relativity + ΛCDM and the Standard Model — establishing that:

1. **ΛCDM is a protective-belt shell game.** Bare GR is never tested alone; auxiliary components (dark matter profiles, dark energy equation of state, inflationary parameters) absorb every anomaly while preserving GR's core.
2. **The Standard Model accommodates any observation through 19 free parameters.** Masses, mixing angles, and coupling constants are measured inputs, not predictions. Anomalies that appear (muon g-2, B-meson anomalies, SUSY, WIMPs, proton decay) are absorbed by parameter adjustment or "systematics" — goalposts move in one direction only.
3. **Eddington's 1919 "confirmation" was methodologically compromised.** Biased plate selection, subjective curvature assessment, and alternative hypotheses that were never considered.
4. **Subsequent GR tests are measurement exercises, not falsification attempts.** No competing framework produces predictions at comparable precision — creating a monopoly on calculability that makes every "test" structurally a measurement of GR's parameters rather than a genuine attempt at falsification.
5. **Independent consilience is the only epistemologically sound path forward.** Single-stream confirmation with shared systematics, confirmation bias, and no alternative on offer carries near-zero evidential weight per the Bayesian Evidential Weight Gate (KIF-60).
