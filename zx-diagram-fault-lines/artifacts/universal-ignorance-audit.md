# Universal Ignorance Audit — QNFO.RES.015 Core Claim

**Instrument:** The Universal Ignorance Audit (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21901984), 5 phases / 15 questions.
**Target (stated explicitly):** the locked core claim of QNFO.RES.015 — *ZX-calculus constructs
(spiders, Pauli webs, gadgets) are cafeteria imports combined without demonstrated
mutual-compatibility checks; their 2D "tube" diagrams exhibit internal validity but unestablished
bearing on 3+1D physical quantum mechanics; the cafeteria problem generalizes to siloed
disciplines; the remedy is map-aware use with declared seams.*
**Administrator:** parent-agent session (2026-08-18). Answers written, not resolved in Phases 1–4.

## Phase 1 — Surface the Structure

**Q1. Scaffold detection.** *What hidden assumptions hold this claim up?*
- S1: that diagrammatic internal validity (soundness/completeness theorems) licenses physical claim-making at all.
- S2: that 2D topology (planes, tubes, wires) is a faithful *container* for quantum processes that live in 3+1D.
- S3: that each silo's imports are internally consistent, so mixing them is harmless by default.
- S4 (self-scaffold): that the user's originating intuition — "little bearing on quantum mechanics" — is an observation about the territory and not itself a map of a map.
- S5 (self-scaffold): that the QNFO locale/ultrametric lens used to audit the diagrams is not itself an unvetted cafeteria import.

**Q2. Map–territory hygiene.** *Where might the map be mistaken for the territory?*
- The ZX diagram is the map; the quantum process is the territory. Spiders are not particles; wires are not worldlines; the drawing plane is not spacetime.
- The completeness theorems are results about the map's expressiveness, not evidence of physical bearing — reading completeness as physical completeness is the core map–territory error.
- The paper's own maps: "cafeteria" and "seam" are metaphors for the import structure, not the imports themselves.

**Q3. Wobble probe.** *Where is the tension that does not fit?*
- The wobble: a language with "little bearing on quantum mechanics" nonetheless produces spectacularly actionable results — PyZX reduces real circuits, optimizations verified on real hardware. Internal validity demonstrably pays operational dividends while physical bearing stays unestablished. Both halves of the claim are simultaneously true; the wobble IS the seam.

## Phase 2 — Stress-Test the Frame

**Q4. Inversion.** *What if the opposite were true?*
- Opposite: the 2D diagrammatic language is not a mere map but is exactly as real as the Hilbert-space formalism — both are algebraic/combinatorial structures, and "3+1D reality" is itself a map. Then the fault line is not diagram-vs-reality but formalism-vs-formalism (Hilbert QM vs categorical QM), and the cafeteria problem is an import-mismatch between formalisms, not a map–territory error. This alternative reading is live and the paper must hold it open rather than foreclose it.

**Q5. Falsifiability test.** *What world would show this false?*
- A world where: (a) published inter-translation theorems demonstrate mutual compatibility of the silo formulations (2D categorical / 3+1D field-theoretic / 1D thermodynamic imports), and (b) practitioners' use of ZX diagrams does not leak 2D intuitions into 3+1D claims (nobody reads wire-crossings as spacetime braids, nobody imports entropy claims into spiders). The claim is falsified by finding the published compatibility audit that passes — a concrete search target for Phase 1.

**Q6. Invariant extraction.** *What survives a change of frame/base/origin?*
- Survives formalism change (Hilbert → ZX → string diagrams → tensors): the algebraic relations (fusion, complementarity), the computational content (circuit equivalence), the measurement statistics.
- Varies: the topology (2D plane, tubes), the ontological reading. What is real may be exactly the invariants; what is cafeteria is what varies across the silos.

## Phase 3 — Multiply Perspectives

**Q7. Radical perspectival shift.** *How does this look to a radically different observer?*
- Compilation engineer: ZX is a tool that works; the seam question is idle philosophy.
- Condensed-matter physicist: the missing 3+1D structure is not a bug but evidence the diagrams were never meant to carry it.
- Category theorist: a sound, complete calculus needs no physical bearing to be valuable.
- Philosopher of science: a textbook case of representation — and of notation mistaken for ontology.
- A future working qubit: only measurement statistics exist; the diagrams never enter its world. Each observer's verdict is true *from inside their silo* — which is the cafeteria problem restated as an observer problem.

**Q8. Externalized ignorance.** *Who knows something I do not?*
- The ZX community (Coecke, Kissinger, van de Wetering) knows the categorical foundations and the precise scope of the completeness results.
- Stabilizer/QEC theorists know Pauli webs' home turf.
- MBQC researchers know gadgets' origins and the physical assumptions of measurement patterns.
- Historians of diagrammatic notation (Frege/Peirce tradition) would name the blind spot: treating notation as neutral. Their likely verdict on the biggest blind spot: under-weighting the formal equivalence results that make the cafeteria harmless *within the computational domain* — the claim must be scoped so it does not overreach into that domain.

## Phase 4 — Uncover the Hidden Forces

**Q9. Power analysis.** *Who benefits from the current framing; who is silenced?*
- Benefits from diagrammatic adoption: tooling authors, quantum-computing grant narratives ("intuitive formalism"), pedagogy.
- Silenced: the originators' own caveats ("a calculus for computation, not an ontology") — caveats decay faster than the notation spreads.
- The critique's own beneficiary: the QNFO program's locale/ultrametric thesis benefits from finding fault lines in the dominant formalism. Disclosed, not denied: the auditor profits from the audit's conclusion, and that incentive must not select the findings.

**Q10. Protected-ignorance probe.** *The most dangerous question?*
- "Is the QNFO program's own ultrametric/locale framing doing exactly what this paper accuses ZX diagrams of — importing from number theory, tree geometry, and thermodynamics without compatibility checks?" The meta-risk: a cafeteria critique that is itself cafeteria. This question is HELD, not resolved; the paper must answer it in prose (premise-depth disclosure) without pretending the audit lens is privilege-free.

**Q11. Somatic and tacit dimension.** *What does the confusion feel like?*
- The originating note names it: "satisfying to see connections in a graph-like way" — the pleasure of a diagram closing is the pull. The confusion lives in that satisfaction: the wobble feels like the pleasant click of a completed graph, which is exactly the moment the map feels most like territory. The feeling itself knows that the seduction is the risk.

## Phase 5 — Act

**Q12. Willful ignorance.** *What do I already know but pretend not to?*
- The note already states the diagrams are "deliberately an abstracted, simplified model/map." Pretending the rigor of the literature dissolves the risk is willful: rigor of the map is not probative about the territory.

**Q13. Actionable ignorance.** *What can I do now, without resolving it?*
- (a) Build the import-provenance table for spiders/Pauli webs/gadgets; (b) execute the Phase-1 falsifier search: does a published cross-silo compatibility audit exist?; (c) declare the seam in every use of the diagrams; (d) scope the claim to exclude the computational domain where internal validity is the whole story.

**Q14. Relational ignorance.** *What does the unknown want from me?*
