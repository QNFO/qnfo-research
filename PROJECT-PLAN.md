## 1. Charter

QNFO.RES.020 established that the p-adic maximum-entropy distribution of Adelic Shannon
Theory is exactly the Bose–Einstein occupation distribution at inverse temperature
β_p = ln p, that the squarefree restriction is its Fermi–Dirac counterpart, and that an
ideal QND measurement is the equality case of the adelic data-processing inequality —
under a structural thesis that e, π, and R = (e^{iπ})^{2s} form one self-referential
scalar family generated by the act of drawing a distinction.

The six seed notes push one step further, to the state-space geometry behind that
thesis. Their combined claim:

1. **The continuum is an infinite-information object.** A single real coordinate
   specifies infinitely many yes/no distinctions. A finite-entropy world cannot contain
   such coordinates; Planck's h already told us phase space is cellulated (the "UV
   catastrophe again", now at the level of state-space ontology).
2. **The natural geometry of finite distinctions is combinatorial, not Archimedean.**
   Distinguishability is an equivalence relation; distance is graph distance or an
   ultrametric. Two states are either distinct or not at a given resolution; there is no
   arbitrarily small "betweenness". Cartesian axes are labels; only the partition into
   this/that matters.
3. **Quantum mechanics as thermodynamics is a stochastic thermodynamics of finite
   alternatives.** Unitary evolution and superposition are conjectured to be emergent —
   the large-distinction limit of an entropy-Hessian gradient flow — so that the
   Hilbert-space-over-ℂ formulation is a thermodynamic limit (a map), not the physical
   reality (territory).

This project formalizes that triple claim into a research paper under the standard
pipeline (P0–P8), with computational verification deposited as source, and with the
claims graded (identity / dictionary / conjecture) exactly as RES.020 graded its own.

## 2. Core Claim (draft — LOCKED at P6)

> **A finite-entropy world is a finite-distinction world: the Archimedean continuum is
> unphysical as an ontology because it requires infinitely many distinctions per
> coordinate. The state-space geometry of quantum mechanics is therefore combinatorial
> and ultrametric — distance is graph distance between distinctions, and there is no
> arbitrarily small betweenness. Read as thermodynamics, quantum mechanics is a
> stochastic thermodynamics of finite alternatives, in which unitary evolution and
> superposition emerge as the large-distinction limit of the entropy-Hessian gradient
> flow. The Hilbert-space formulation over the complex numbers is that limit — a map —
> not the territory.**

## 5. Verification Plan (COMPUTATIONAL-VERIFICATION-1)

1. **H-CONT** — diagonal/counting argument; no code needed (registers as premise, not
   theorem).
2. **H-ULTRA** — seeded construction: hierarchical clustering of measurement-outcome
   partitions; verify strong triangle inequality on ≥3 levels; log violations (expect 0).
3. **H-UNIT** — seeded Monte Carlo: N-alternative entropy-Hessian flow, N = 2^4..2^14;
   measure entropy production per step and deviation of the effective generator from
   symplecticity; assert scaling → 0 in N (fit exponent).
4. **H-BORN** — seeded Monte Carlo: max-entropy weights over N alternatives vs Born
   frequencies for a fixed test state family; tolerance ±2σ.
5. **H-TIME** — clock subsystem of n distinctions; relational-dynamics convergence
   diagnostics vs n.

All scripts live in `artifacts/verification/`, are stdlib-only deterministic, and are
deposited with the paper. Reproducibility statement (runtime, seed, versions) in the
README. Failing checks are bugs to fix, not results to deposit (VERIFY-FIX-RERUN-1).

## 6. Practitioner Relevance (PRACTITIONER-RELEVANCE-1)

What a practitioner can DO with this, in engineering language:

1. **Resolution-bounded quantum emulation.** A finite-distinction state space gives a
   principled truncation of quantum simulation: the number of distinctions is the
   resource, not the number of amplitudes. Deliverable: a spec-sheet for a
   finite-precision quantum emulator whose accuracy budget is a distinction count.
2. **Energy accounting (JPCUB).** If unitary evolution is the large-distinction limit of
   an entropy-gradient flow, then the joules-per-solution cost of a quantum computation
   is a function of distinctions made per answer — a countable, benchmarkable resource
   aligned with the QNFO.JPC.001 benchmark program.
3. **Ultrametric QEC decoders.** QNFO.UF already classifies QEC codes by p-adic
   valuations; an ultrametric state space turns that classification into a decoding
   metric (nearest-distinction decoding on a tree).
4. **Measurement metrology.** The finite-resolution reading gives a per-measurement
   distinction budget — extend the RES.020 QND entropy-conservation rule to a practical
   audit rule for readout chains.

## 7. Falsification Conditions (paper §7 target)

- **F1 (continuum):** exhibit a physical observable whose value requires an exact real
  number at fixed finite resolution (no finite-description equivalent).
- **F2 (ultrametricity):** a reproducible triple of distinguishable states violating the
  strong triangle inequality.
- **F3 (unitarity emergence):** entropy production of the N-alternative Hessian flow does
  not scale to zero with N.
- **F4 (Born emergence):** the large-distinction limit of max-entropy weights does not
  match Born frequencies within Monte Carlo tolerance.
- **F5 (relational time):** no clock subsystem yields Schrödinger-convergent relational
  dynamics.
