# WBS: QNFO.RES.001

## Charter

**Paper:** Bayesian Guardrails for Cross-Domain Synthesis: A Self-Critical Audit of Adelic Framework Predictions

**Short name:** BSM (Bayesian Synthesis Methodology)

**Research question:** What methodological guardrails prevent cross-domain synthesis frameworks from degenerating into post-hoc rational narratives (tautologies), and how does the Adelic Core Synthesis framework score under Bayesian model comparison against the standard null model (ΛCDM + SM + GR)?

**Core claim:** Bayesian reasoning — specifically the requirement that P(data|theory) ≫ P(data|¬theory) for evidential weight — is the only guardrail that distinguishes genuine cross-domain consilience from retrospective storytelling. Applied to the Adelic Core Synthesis, the framework is currently a research programme with 5 falsifiable predictions, not a fully specified predictive model, and its Bayesian score against the null model is near-zero for post-dictions and pending for forward predictions.

## Success Criteria

1. Paper presents a **reusable Bayesian methodology** for auditing any cross-domain synthesis framework
2. Adelic Core Synthesis receives an **honest Bayesian score** — post-dictions (near-zero weight) vs. forward predictions (pending verification)
3. At least 5 **falsifiable predictions** with specific templates (gravitational wave echoes, matter power spectrum, spectral dimension running, Lorentz violation, CMB parity)
4. Self-critical conclusion: "not a fully specified predictive model, but a research programme"
5. Published on Zenodo with verified DOI, deployed to D1 + papers.qnfo.org
