# Core Claim (LOCKED — P6, Phase 0)

**Project:** Prime Valuation Depth — QNFO.RES.005
**Locked:** 2026-08-13

## Claim 1 [TERRITORY — established]

Multiplication is standardly defined by recursive addition, but recursion hides a structural fact: multiplication generates a new dimension of distinctions. The product is the count of pairs, which requires pairing — a new level of distinction between the two factors. Prime factorization makes the branching explicit. Every positive integer is a finite product of prime powers: each prime is a distinct branch type, and the exponent counts the depth of nesting along that branch. The p-adic valuation is therefore a measure of depth along a prime branch, not a measure of size.

Anchor: Ostrowski's theorem (1916) — the only nontrivial absolute values on Q are the Archimedean one and the p-adic ones; the prime-depth valuations v_p are thereby exhaustive among non-Archimedean valuations.

## Claim 2 [MAP — interpretive bridge, to be defended]

The valuation-as-depth reading is the bridge between the calculus of indications (branching distinctions) and number theory (the tree of prime divisors).

Falsifiability condition: disconfirmed if no nontrivial structure-preserving correspondence exists between nested distinctions and p-adic valuations — i.e., if the correspondence reduces to relabeling with no shared structural laws.

## Claim 3 [MAP — speculative extension, to be developed]

The reading transfers to quantum mechanics. The tensor product multiplies dimensions (it is not a Cartesian product), generating paired distinctions. For n qubits, dim H = 2^n, so v_2(dim H) = n: the 2-adic valuation is the tensor-branch depth. The no-cloning theorem is structural: cloning would require the diagonal map |psi> -> |psi> (x) |psi>, which is nonlinear (quadratic in the amplitudes: cross terms a·b cannot be produced by a linear map). Quantum evolution preserves only linear structure, so multiplicative branching cannot be duplicated linearly. Adjacent readings: no-broadcasting (incompatible branch trees for non-commuting observables), monogamy of entanglement (v_2(dim) = 1 depth unit per qubit).

Falsifiability condition: disconfirmed if the reading yields no explanatory or predictive content beyond the standard formalism (pure relabeling). In that case the claim is capped at [RETRODICTION — not evidence] and the number-theoretic bridge (Claim 2) stands alone.
