# Conference abstract — IS4SI Summit 2027 (and ASC 2027 variant)

**Title (IS4SI):** Ignorance as an Output: an audited AI-accelerated research program and the epistemology of knowing what we do not know

**Abstract (~300 words, IS4SI angle — information science / epistemology):**

This contribution reports a 263-day solo research program (Dec 2025 – Aug 2026) run on a DeepSeek agentic pipeline, published with a complete ledger — $1,032.08, 50.11B tokens, 323,381 requests, 911 Zenodo records — and argues that its most important outputs are epistemic rather than documentary. Three findings: (1) production became cheap and judgment became the entire job; the pipeline's real inventions were the instruments of decision (live citation audits, adversarial review, publication gates); (2) AI error is systematic, not random — fabricated citations, encoding corruption, and phantom identifiers recurred in predictable classes, so the program's defenses became gates rather than intentions; (3) the signature result is a method for auditing ignorance — the Universal Ignorance Audit (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21901984) — which treats "what we do not know" as a first-class research output. The program's ledger is offered as a public artifact with a pre-registered kill criterion: zero external traction by August 2028 renders the efficiency claim hollow. AI involvement is disclosed throughout as a quality signal; the essay's method appendix makes every figure traceable to primary sources.

**ASC 2027 variant (cybernetics as conversation):** reframe around human–machine research as a conversation system — the human as the kill-decision node, the machine as the proposal generator, and the audit loop as the conversation's error-correction channel. Same numbers, same kill criterion.
