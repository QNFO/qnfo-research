# Conference abstract — DICE2026 (Castiglioncello, Oct 5–9 2026)

**Title:** The $1,032 Research Program: an audited ledger of AI-accelerated science

**Abstract (~300 words):**

The cost structure of doing science has changed by two orders of magnitude for the individual researcher, and there is now a way to measure it from primary sources. This contribution reports a 263-day solo research program (December 1, 2025 – August 20, 2026) conducted with a DeepSeek-based agentic pipeline, published with a complete ledger: $1,032.08 in API costs, 323,381 requests, 50.11B tokens (96.4% prompt-cache hits), and an output corpus of 911 versioned Zenodo records (~877 distinct works), 1,646 knowledge-graph paper nodes, 114 repositories, and 20 production Cloudflare Workers. The blended cost was $20.60 per billion tokens; against a fully loaded US postdoctoral researcher for the same period (~$65k–$112k), the measured cost ratio is ~60–110×.

The contribution is not the scale claim — the AI-Scientist literature (Sakana et al., 2024) already reports <$15/paper in laboratory settings. What is new is the *operational audit*: a real program, with receipts, that publishes (i) its method (every figure traces to platform usage exports and public registries), (ii) its failure history (13 hard findings surfaced by the program's own post-publication audits, including fabricated citations caught before distribution), and (iii) its falsifiability condition (zero external traction by August 2028 renders the efficiency claim hollow, registered in advance). Interpreted through DICE's lens of science as information processing, the research program itself is a measurement apparatus — and the ledger is its calibration record. The human remains the unpriced input: judgment, kill decisions, and the audit discipline that gates are what the machine does not supply.
