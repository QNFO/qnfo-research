## Phase 1 — Surface the Structure

**Q1. Scaffold detection — What hidden assumptions and scaffolds hold this situation up?**

- S1: The scaffold "the seed notes accurately represent PQS and the critique" — both notes are AI-generated summaries whose fidelity to the actual source texts is UNVERIFIED. The entire adjudication sits on this.
- S2: The scaffold "there is a verifiable historical record of 1929–1933 that settles the chronology question" — the record exists but is mediated by secondary scholarship and archival availability; the archive is the map, not the territory.
- S3: The scaffold "the QNFO corpus and the external literature are disjoint enough that internal-vs-external corroboration is meaningful" — leakage (QNFO authors citing the same sources, or the corpus absorbing the same claims) could make "external" verification circular.
- S4: The scaffold "a critique's premises failing verification means the framework's claims succeed" — this is the exact asymmetry CC-1 guards against, but the audit itself must not commit it in reverse: grading the critique CONTRADICTED is not grading PQS CONFIRMED.

**Q2. Map–territory hygiene — Where might the map be mistaken for the territory?**

- M1: The five-point summary (note 2) is a MAP of the critique, not the critique itself. Grading the summary's phrasing rather than the underlying arguments would adjudicate a caricature. Mitigation: every grade cites the PQS source text (SSRN/PhilPapers) and the primary literature, not the note's prose.
- M2: "Chronological fallacy" (1932 vs 1933) is a date-map. The territory is the *intellectual development* of measure-theoretic probability (Borel 1909, Lévy, Fréchet 1920s) and of quantum measurement theory — dates are proxies, not the argument.
- M3: "Quantization is measurement-dependent" (PQS spectral-divergence claim) — the map is the claim's phrasing; the territory is what the PQS papers actually assert (which the seed note may compress).
- M4: DOI/SSRN/PhilPapers identifiers in the seed notes are map-pins; a pin that does not resolve is a map error, not evidence about the territory.

**Q3. Wobble probe — Where is the tension, the thing that does not fit?**

- W1: The critique's point 2 demands "no specific equations" from PQS, yet the seed summary (note 1) explicitly names Madelung hydrodynamics and Radon–Nikodým derivatives as the mechanism's formal tools — the critique may have been written against a weaker version of the framework than the summary describes. This wobble suggests possible strawmanning.
- W2: The critique's point 1 says QM "was already empirically established by 1932" — true, yet von Neumann's axiomatization project was *specifically about probability structure*, and Kolmogorov's monograph did not invent measure-theoretic probability but codified it. Both claims can be true; the wobble is in what "the divide" is attributed to.
- W3: Point 5 demands "testable, predictive alternative" while PQS claims to be a *reinterpretation* — the critique may be applying a generation-standard to a reconstruction-standard (category error the adjudication must name precisely).
- W4: The notes were both produced by AI. Their shared provenance may mean the critique inherited the summary's errors (AI-CONVERGENCE-WARNING candidate per KIF-17).

## Phase 2 — Stress-Test the Frame

**Q4. Inversion — What if the opposite were true?**

- If the critique were FULLY correct (all five points CONFIRMED), the adjudication paper would be a short confirmation: PQS's public claims fail on chronology, mechanism, quantization evidence, geometry choice, and overall substantiation. CC-1 would be disconfirmed and the paper would be an honest "the critique stands" — with the consequence that the corpus' PQS-adjacent claims (UMP program) need re-auditing for the same errors.
- If the critique were FULLY wrong, the paper would show a five-fold premise failure — and the interesting finding would be *why* an AI critic produced uniformly unverified premises (shared training priors, KIF-17).

**Q5. Falsifiability test — What would a world look like in which CC-1 is false?**

- World: external verification shows all five critique points' factual premises check out against primary sources (e.g., the historical record really is unambiguously 1932-before-1933 with no 1929–1931 antecedents; the PQS papers contain NO equations for the relaxation mechanism; quantization is demonstrably measurement-independent per the PQS papers' own definitions; the ultrametric replacement has no precedent literature and no gain). In that world CC-1 is false — the critique is valid, and the paper says so with the evidence.
- Cheap escape hatch blocked: CC-1's "≥2 of 5 fail" is graded per-point with a pre-declared evidence class per grade (see D7 spec), so a partial confirmation cannot be dressed as a refutation.

**Q6. Invariant extraction — If I changed the base/frame, what would remain the same?**

- Change the frame from "adjudicate the critique" to "adjudicate the framework": the invariance question is whether PQS's central reconstructive claims (Kolmogorovian grounding; relaxation-not-collapse; quantization as dynamical) survive contact with primary literature *regardless of what the critique says*. The invariant across both frames: **the primary sources are the arbiter; every grade must be source-anchored.**
- Change the base from 2026 back to 1933: the question "was there a live alternative to von Neumann's probability structure in 1932?" remains answerable from the same primary record.

## Phase 3 — Multiply Perspectives

**Q7. Radical perspectival shift — How would this look to a radically different observer?**

- A 1933 mathematician (say, Fréchet): the von Neumann-vs-Kolmogorov ordering is *co-development*, not a race; both men were formalizing structures already alive in the literature (Borel, Lévy, Daniell). From this perspective, the critique's "if Kolmogorov had published first" is a category error about how mathematics develops — and PQS's own "chronological fallacy" framing inherits some of the same artifact. The adjudication must hold BOTH readings.
- A quantum-information practitioner (2026): the measurement problem is irrelevant to their work (they use the axioms operationally). From their seat, both the critique and PQS's reinterpretation are "philosophy" — the adjudication must therefore supply the practitioner-usable output (grading rubric + per-claim register) or it fails PRACTITIONER-RELEVANCE-1.
- A historian of science: the interesting object is not who is right but why the "1932-vs-1933" story circulates — it is a *narrative* with evidentiary and rhetorical load-bearing walls.

**Q8. Externalized ignorance — Who knows something about X that I do not, and what would they say is my biggest blind spot?**

- A historian of probability (e.g., work on von Mises, Borel, or the 1920s frequency debates) would say: my blind spot is the pre-1933 literature — I risk treating 1933 as the origin of classical probability when it is a codification. The adjudication's chronology grade is only as good as the pre-1933 sources I fetch.
- A philosopher of quantum foundations (Bub, Maudlin, Wallace) would say: my blind spot is the decades of collapse-mechanism literature (GRW, CSL, decoherence) that already explored "continuous relaxation" — the adjudication must check whether PQS's mechanism is novel or a variant of existing spontaneous-collapse models, or the critique's "unexplained mechanism" point wins by default of missing prior art.
- A QNFO corpus librarian would say: my blind spot is corpus leakage — the same external sources cited by PQS papers may be the only "external" corroboration available.

## Phase 4 — Uncover the Hidden Forces

**Q9. Power analysis — Who benefits from the current framing? Who is silenced?**

- Framing "critique vs framework" benefits: (a) the critique's author (AI or human) — a failed framework is a satisfying object; (b) the incumbent physics establishment's default dismissal machinery (an "independent researcher" with non-standard math is pre-silenced by framing); (c) QNFO itself — a rebuttal paper is free legitimacy. Silenced: the *third* position — that both sides are partially wrong, which is the least publishable and most likely true outcome. The adjudication must protect that third position (KIF-16: neutral epistemic labels; no "fringe/pseudoscience" vocabulary).
- AI-CONVERGENCE-WARNING (KIF-17) applies: the seed notes are AI-generated; 2+ AI evaluations converging on "ambitious but unsubstantiated" may reflect shared training-data priors about independent researchers with non-standard mathematics — not independent validation.
