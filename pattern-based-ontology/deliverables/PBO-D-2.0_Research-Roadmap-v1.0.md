## 0.0 Framework Foundation

The Pattern-Based Ontology (PBO) posits that all of reality consists of **patterns** generated by the recursive application of a single primitive operation: **drawing a distinction** (Spencer-Brown, 1969). From this operation — the mark `#` and enclosure `[ ]` — and two reduction rules (Calling: `## → #`; Crossing: `[#] → ∅`), the entire universe of structure emerges.

The Bridge Equation survives the reformulation intact:

$$\hbar \omega_C = m_0 c^2 \quad \Rightarrow \quad m = \omega \text{ (natural units)}$$

Under PBO, mass is not a "quantity of substance" but the **iteration rate of a stable pattern** — the frequency at which a pattern's internal distinctions reiterate to maintain its boundary against the surrounding pattern network.

**Certainty:** All claims in this roadmap carry the default label `[my conjecture]` unless labeled otherwise. `[established]` claims reference textbook-consensus physics. The core thesis — that all is pattern — remains a `[PHILOSOPHY]` position, though specific physical predictions (CMB oscillations, spacetime granularity, particle normal forms) are `[speculative, not yet falsifiable]`.

**Map/Territory Distinction:** This roadmap describes a research program — a map. Whether the territory corresponds to the map is an open question. The mathematical structure ($m = \omega$, the ultrametric tree, the STC) is compatible with multiple ontologies.
