### Epistemic Note: Ontological Status

The forty-two theses that follow articulate a *pattern-based reality* through the lens of **Autaxys**—a framework for understanding reality as a dynamic, self-organizing process. Following the ontological calibration documented in the [Autaxys Ontological Status Addendum v1.0](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21369303), this version clarifies the epistemic status of each claim:

- **[my conjecture]** — Author's working hypothesis; not independently verified. Core axioms of the framework.
- **[speculative]** — Derivation from the framework; plausible but lacking direct empirical confirmation.
- **[not yet falsifiable]** — Empirically verifiable in principle, but current experimental capability is insufficient.
- **[PHILOSOPHY]** — Metaphysical position; not subject to empirical falsification in the standard scientific sense.
- **[established]** — Grounded in well-confirmed physical theory, though interpreted through the Autaxys lens.

**Map/Territory Distinction:** This document presents a *model* of reality—a formal pattern-based description—not reality itself. The framework is one of several possible interpretations of the same underlying phenomena [@autaxys-ontological-status-2026]. The Mass-Frequency Identity ($m = \omega$) and Resonant Universe framework admit alternative ontological interpretations, as documented in [The Mass-Frequency Identity v3.2.0 §15](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21360549) and [The Resonant Universe v2.0.1 §12](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21194864) [@mass-frequency-identity-2026; @resonant-universe-2026].

**Preamble: The Explanatory Crisis of Materialism and the Generative Power of Patterns** [PHILOSOPHY]

Physical materialism, the dominant metaphysical framework, has reached an explanatory impasse. While successful in *describing* observed phenomena, it offers no generative account for the universe's most fundamental characteristics. The origin of physical laws, the fine-tuned values of fundamental constants, the non-locality of quantum mechanics, and the irreducible reality of subjective consciousness are treated as axiomatic givens or brute facts. Materialism excels at the 'what' but fails to penetrate the 'why' and 'how' of existence, relying on postulates where a generative source is required.

This failure necessitates a fundamental paradigm shift towards a **process ontology**, where reality is not static substance but a dynamic, self-organizing computational process. The universe is not a collection of objects in a void, but a singular, evolving computation unfolding within a fundamental **dynamic medium**. This process is formally described by the framework of **[Autaxys](https://zenodo.org/communities/autaxys/)**: the intrinsic, irreducible drive towards self-organization and the formation of stable, resonant patterns.

This inherent drive is guided by the principle of **Ontological Closure**—the relentless tendency towards maximal informational coherence and self-consistency. This principle provides the generative explanation that materialism lacks. It reveals the fundamental source from which the universe's structure, laws, and phenomena emerge, not as arbitrary facts, but as necessary outcomes of a universal, self-organizing computation.

The following forty-two theses articulate this pattern-based reality. They serve as a direct challenge to the materialist paradigm, asserting that its phenomena are derivative expressions of a more fundamental, dynamic, and computational reality. While no single document can claim to be the final answer to the ultimate question of life, the universe, and everything, this framework offers a unified and causally coherent account of existence, resolving materialism's core explanatory deficits by revealing the underlying generative mechanism from which all physical structure, laws, and consciousness emerge.

**V. Resolved:** The universe does not merely contain computation; the universe *is* a singular, self-generating computational process, an evolving pattern network driven by Autaxys towards increasing Ontological Closure. [my conjecture]

**VI. Resolved:** The identity of any phenomenon is defined by its underlying informational pattern. This pattern is primary and ontologically precedes any physical manifestation, acting as its persistent computational blueprint. [PHILOSOPHY]

**VII. Resolved:** "Physicality" is the emergent quality of any informational pattern that achieves sufficient resonant stability and Ontological Closure to interact predictably with other stable patterns. [my conjecture]

## Limitations & Open Questions

This framework, while offering a coherent and generative alternative to physical materialism, has known boundaries:

1. **Falsifiability Gap:** Theses labeled [my conjecture] and [PHILOSOPHY] are not directly testable with current experimental capability. The framework makes falsifiable predictions (theses XIX, XX, XXX, XXXII, XXXVIII) but empirical verification may require technological advances beyond the near-term horizon.

2. **Interpretational Pluralism:** The Autaxys framework can be interpreted through multiple ontological lenses—process ontology, information-theoretic, or purely mathematical—as documented in the Ontological Status Addendum. No single interpretation is privileged by the mathematics.

3. **Derivation Chain:** The step from "Autaxys as generative principle" to specific physical predictions (dark energy as computational work, spacetime granularity, etc.) involves a chain of inference that has not been independently validated.

4. **Consciousness Thesis (XXXVIII):** The claim that consciousness emerges from integrated Ontological Closure is the least developed aspect of the framework and would benefit from cross-disciplinary engagement with neuroscience and philosophy of mind.
