## What Is Pattern-Based Ontology?

Pattern-Based Ontology (PBO) is a unified framework for fundamental reality, grounded in Spencer-Brown's *Laws of Form* (1969). The core thesis: **all of reality consists of patterns** generated by the recursive application of a single primitive operation — drawing a distinction.

PBO reformulates and supersedes the Autaxys research program (2025–2026) by replacing the multi-component "generative engine" (5 dynamics + 5 principles = 10 axioms) with **1 primitive operation** (the mark) + **2 reduction rules** (Calling, Crossing).
