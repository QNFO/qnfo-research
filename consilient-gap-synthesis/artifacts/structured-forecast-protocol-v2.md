## 1. Core Claim

The QNFO research ecosystem has identifiable "consilience gaps" — domains, methods, and problem classes where the p-adic/ultrametric toolkit COULD be applied but currently IS NOT. The gap registry (gap-registry.md) catalogs these gaps. The calibration methodology (calibration-methodology-p04.md) provides the framework for prioritizing which gaps to fill first.

## 4. Stage 9: Practical Applications

The primary practical application of the gap synthesis project is itself META: it guides QNFO's research allocation by identifying which gaps to fill first. The "operational signature" is a decision framework:

**Operational Signature:** QNFO research roadmap meetings use the gap registry + prioritization ranking to allocate researcher effort across projects. A gap moves from "identified" → "active project" → "published paper" → "closed."

**Falsifiable Claim:** By 2028-12-31, ≥3 gaps from the registry (including newly identified gaps from this session) will have active QNFO projects with at least one publication each.

## 5. Stage 10: Counterfactual Backcasting

### Tier 1 Fork (~20yr): Systematic Gap Analysis from QNFO's Founding

**Fork:** QNFO's founding documents (2023-2024) included a systematic gap analysis — the gap registry was populated at project inception, not retroactively in 2026.

**Counterfactual:** By 2026, 8-12 gaps would be closed (active projects with publications), not 0. The neuroscience, drug discovery, and climate domains would have years of published results. QNFO would be positioned as a cross-domain consilience institute, not a physics+X research collective.

### Tier 2 Fork (~60yr): Santa Fe Institute Adopts Ultrametric Framework

**Fork:** The Santa Fe Institute (founded 1984, complex systems research) adopts ultrametric/p-adic topology as a core mathematical framework. By 1995, SFI researchers are applying ultrametric methods to economics, biology, and computer science.

**Counterfactual:** By 2026, ultrametric topology is as standard in complex systems science as network theory. The "consilience gap" concept would be recognized as a universal research problem — every interdisciplinary field has gaps, and systematic gap analysis is a standard methodology. QNFO would be entering a mature field, not creating one.
