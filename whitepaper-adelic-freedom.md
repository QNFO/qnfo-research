## 1. The Problem

We have ten fingers, so we count in base-10. We are 60% water, so we defined mass by water's density. We are ~1.7m tall walking apes, so we made the meter body-scaled. Our heart beats at ~1 Hz, our sleep cycle is ~24 hours, and our retinas peak at 555 nm — so the second, the kelvin, and the candela encode these anatomical accidents.

We then projected these features onto the cosmos, forgot we did so, and called the result "natural law."

**This is not a metaphor.** Every SI base unit traces to a specific, identifiable feature of human cognitive architecture:

| Unit | Cognitive Origin | Verdict |
|:-----|:-----------------|:--------|
| meter | Earth meridian / 10^7, body-scaled | Decimal fingers + body-scale |
| kilogram | 1 dm^3 water at 4C | Water = our biochemistry |
| second | 1/86,400 solar day | Circadian + heartbeat |
| kelvin | 1/273.16 water triple point | Liquid water band |
| mole | 12g C-12 bridge | Human lab-scale |
| candela | Photopic V(lambda), 555nm peak | Human retina |
| ampere | Inherited from m*kg*s | Chain of anthropocentricity |

The 2019 SI redefinition fixed fundamental constants but chose their numerical values to preserve the old units. The encryption was relocated from the definitions to the constants. The SI now operates under a **natural constants facade**.
