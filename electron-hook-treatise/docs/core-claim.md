# Core Claim — electron-hook-treatise v0.1

**C1 (central).** The load-bearing assumptions of quantum mechanics, thermodynamics, and computation — the complex Hilbert-space postulate, the spin-statistics theorem's premises, the Archimedean valuation of measured quantities, the particle concept, exact unitarity, ergodicity, and their siblings (fifteen catalogued, §III.7) — are contingent postulates, not theorems; several are already known to fail outside special domains (anyons in $2+1$D, quasiparticle dressing, vacuum-dependent particle content, many-body localization); and every one of the fifteen admits a discriminating observation or theorem (falsifiability map, §IV.3).

**C2 (method).** A uniform six-step audit — state the assumption; identify what is built on it; separate confirmed predictions from postulates; list alternatives; define falsifiability; record open questions — promotes these assumptions from articles of faith to objects of experiment.

**Premise depth (SO-WHAT-GATE-1).** The treatise introduces no new physical postulate. Its unanalyzable primitives, named rather than hidden, are: ordinary set theory, standard measurement practice, and the requirement of empirical adequacy. Its claims are analytical and taxonomic; their depth is exactly the depth of the published physics they cite.

**What is NOT claimed.** Current physics is wrong; any alternative framework is correct; philosophy replaces experiment.

**Falsifiability.** Each assumption's disconfirmation condition is stated inline (catalogue §III.7) and consolidated in the falsifiability map (§IV.3) and `RESEARCH-CONTINUITY-REGISTRY.md`.
