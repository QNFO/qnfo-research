### Q3. Wobble probe — *What is the tension, the thing that does not fit?*

- **Wobble 1:** the treatise derives e and π as "logical scalars" yet must import substantial analytic machinery (trace in compact closed categories, fixed points in DiLL) — the constants are derived within a rich framework, not from the bare mark. The gap between "primitive mark" and "category with traces and differentials" is the treatise's largest unexplained jump.
- **Wobble 2:** §39 (Primal Algorithm) is honestly assessed as [not yet falsifiable] beyond organization — the treatise itself names this as its weakest point. The wobble is *productive*: it means the algorithm is a research program, not a result.
- **Wobble 3:** the Langlands correspondence (§§18–22) is treated as "the deepest instance of loop–tree duality" — but the correspondence is proven only in special cases. The treatise reads Langlands as evidence for the primacy of the mark; the wobble is that the direction of evidence is unidirectional (mark → mathematics), never tested in reverse.

## Phase 2 — Stress-Test the Frame

### Q4. Inversion — *What if the opposite were true?*

- **If the mark is NOT primal:** all downstream structure still exists in mathematics and physics (e, π, adeles, Langlands, QM, statistics are independently established). The treatise would become a *narrative overlay* on existing results — a retrodiction. The P1 audit already flagged this (Δlog-odds ≈ 0 for the modal readings; only the compositional claims carry evidential weight). **The inversion is survivable but degrades the treatise to literary metaphor** — which is why REG-SLB-001 (machine derives e^{iπ}=−1 without imported constants) is the decisive test.
- **If `!` is NOT the loop and `?` is NOT the tree:** the modal pair loses its organizing power; Parts IV–VIII would need a different scaffolding. No published counterexample exists, but no theorem guarantees the reading either.
- **If measurement is NOT trace:** §26 becomes a failed analogy. No experiment distinguishes it (stated honestly).

### Q5. Falsifiability test — *What would a world look like in which this was false?*

| Claim | World in which it is false | Currently testable? |
|:------|:---------------------------|:--------------------|
| e from Df=f via DiLL coKleisli | A DiLL model exists where the differential-operator fixed point is not e | **YES** — REG-SLB-002, formal model construction |
| π from Tr(id_{S^1}) universally | A traced model where the trace of identity on the circle ≠ π | **YES** — survey traced categories (Rel, Cob, FinVec) |
| Euler identity derived without imported constants | The Re-Entrant Machine imports e/π as axioms (G4 fails) | **YES** — Appendix D implementation |

| Mass as re-entry frequency | A particle's rest-frame phase deviates from e^{-imt} | NO — standard QM predicts the same phase; the claim is interpretation-neutral |
| Primal Algorithm generates the domains | A structure it claims to generate cannot be generated by the stated steps | PARTIAL — needs the machine first |

**Audit finding:** the treatise's *formal* claims are falsifiable (and pre-registered); its *interpretative* claims are not (and honestly labeled). This is the correct epistemic posture for the genre. The audit's action item: **execute the three YES-testable conditions before the next version**, starting with REG-SLB-002 (cheapest: a literature/model survey, no machine needed).

### Q6. Invariant extraction — *If I changed the base, origin, center, or frame, what would remain the same?*

- Change the base from Spencer-Brown to a different distinction calculus (Bricken, Kauffman variants) → the constants, adeles, and Langlands structure remain; only the presentation changes. **Invariant: the mathematical content.**
- Change the frame from linear logic to a different resource logic → the loop/tree reading may shift, but Ostrowski, Tate, and Langlands stand independently. **Invariant: number theory and physics.**
- Change the target domain (drop Part VI physics) → Parts I–V and VII–VIII still form a coherent logical-number-theoretic treatise. **Invariant: the logical core.**
- The genuinely invariant core: **e and π as fixed points/traces of the re-entrant structure, and Ostrowski's one-loop-many-trees classification.** These are the treatise's non-negotiable claims; everything else is scaffolding.

## Phase 3 — Multiply Perspectives

### Q7. Radical perspectival shift — *How would this look to a radically different observer?*

- **A working mathematician** (e.g., a DiLL specialist): sees §9.2 as the crux — "show me the theorem that the codereliction fixed point is e"; the rest is evocative but unproven. Would demand the model.
- **A physicist** (e.g., a quantum foundations experimentalist): sees §24/§26 as non-falsifiable re-descriptions; would ask "what experiment distinguishes this from textbook QM?" — answer: none yet; the treatise concedes this.
- **A historian of mathematics:** sees the treatise as the latest instance of the perennial "derive everything from one primitive" program (Leibniz, Boole, Frege, Spencer-Brown, Wolfram). Would assess novelty against that lineage — the treatise's own P1 audit already distinguishes it (starts from the mark; Wolfram from hypergraph rewriting).
- **A logician (Girardian):** would recognize the transcendental-syntax ambition and ask whether the treatise's "grammar" (§37) is a genuine logical system or a metaphor — same crux as Q1.
- **A skeptic of consilience:** sees 40 sections of structural analogy with zero new predictions; the KIF-60 gate exists precisely for this criticism and the treatise passes it only by labeling the analogies [MAP]/[my conjecture].

## Phase 4 — Uncover the Hidden Forces

### Q9. Power analysis — *Who benefits from the current framing? Who is silenced?*

- **Benefits:** an independent-researcher program (QNFO) gains a flagship unification narrative; the framing elevates a self-published treatise to the register of "foundational" work. The AI-assisted pipeline benefits from the framing's scale (40 sections in days).
- **Silenced/excluded:** the mainstream mathematics and physics communities are implicitly positioned as having "missed the connection" — a framing that risks alienating the very experts whose validation the treatise needs. Textbook conventions and established taxonomies are the silent losers of the re-narration.
- **Harmed:** the treatise's epistemic honesty (its [my conjecture] labels) is its defense; but the sheer ambition risks the "overclaim" perception that independent research cannot afford. The power analysis's verdict: **the treatise must court, not bypass, expert validation** — targeted outreach to DiLL and category-theory researchers is not optional.
