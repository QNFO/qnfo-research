## Executive Summary

We forecast a 100-year landscape of quantum computing and fundamental physics across four eras (2026–2126+). The forecast was generated by the Cascading Bayesian Foresight Engine (CFPE) methodology, calibrated through historical back-casting (1976→2026, 1950→2000, 1926→1976), and stress-tested from 8 independent attack vectors.

**The headline finding:** Gate-based quantum computing will continue scaling incrementally for the next decade (82% probability). Room-temperature quantum computing via NV-diamond centers is the most actionable near-term wildcard (15% probability). The CMB-S4 experiment (results expected ~2035) is the single most important empirical resolution across the entire forecast — it will either validate or falsify the hydrodynamic foundations of quantum mechanics. Geometric derivation of fundamental constants faces the strongest headwinds (57% pessimistic).

**Full cascade probability (all 4 eras as paradigm shifts): 6.5% [4–9%].**
