## 1. The problem, stated as a functor-existence question

The derivation requires a monoidal functor

F : (free re-entrant calculus) → C,

where C is a braided compact closed category, F sends the mark to an object M, Calling to idempotence (the projector reading P² = P, per the rigor pass Claim B), and Crossing to the braiding component σ_{M,M}: M⊗M → M⊗M.

The correct syntactic frame: the calculus of indications is a **presented prop** (a product category / symmetric monoidal category generated by a finite signature and relations — [KNOWN] prop theory; Lack 2004). Generators: the mark M (an object), the crossing c: M⊗M → M⊗M (a morphism). Relations: Calling (idempotence of the symmetrizer reading), Position (double crossing = unmarked: c² = id), Transposition (the third LoF axiom — distributive law).

**Universal property:** a monoidal functor out of a presented category exists **iff** the images of the generators in the target satisfy the relations. This is not optional: F is defined exactly by the data (M, σ) ∈ C satisfying the relations.

## 2. Main finding — the existence condition

**F exists iff c² = id in C.** The Crossing relation (Position: double-cross = unmarked state; in the prop, the unmarked state is the unit/identity) maps to σ_{M,M}² = id. A functor must preserve relations, so the relation must hold in the image. Therefore:

1. **Involutivity is a condition on the target of the interpretation — not a theorem derived within it.** The free braided compact closed category on one self-dual object has **non-involutive** braidings: the braidings on M⊗M are indexed by B_n (the TL chain, corpus-verified: σᵢ = A·I + A⁻¹·Uᵢ, σᵢ² = A²I + (1−A⁻⁴)·Uᵢ ≠ I — p-adic-temperley-lieb-parameter 10.5281/zenodo.21208368 §1.1; anyon regime: p-adic-anyon-fusion-braiding 10.5281/zenodo.21208491). Hence the free object does **not** admit F.
2. **F exists exactly in the involutive specializations:**
   - **Symmetric braided categories** — the symmetry axiom gives σ_{M,M}² = id automatically. Physical target: FdHilb/Vec with the flip braiding — the d≥3 regime (π₁(C_N(ℝ^d)) = S_N; configuration-space-topology 10.5281/zenodo.21957291).
   - **TL at A⁴ = 1** (δ = −2): σᵢ² = A²I + (1−A⁻⁴)Uᵢ = I identically; the image is the classical symmetric-group case (Jones representation of ℂS_n at δ = −2).
3. **Corollary (answers the draft's §0 decisive question with a precise mechanism):** involutivity is **not** derived from Calling + Crossing alone. The calculus supplies the *syntax of the involutive quotient* — S_n = B_n/⟨σᵢ²=1⟩ (Artin) is literally the Crossing relation — but the *existence of any nontrivial interpretation* is conditioned on the target's braiding being involutive, which is the d≥3 kinematical input. The mark calculus expresses the involution as its own law; the selection of the involutive quotient remains external. This is consistent with CST's boundary statement and with the rigor pass's honest postulate set.

**Refinement of rigor-pass Claim A.** Claim A ("involutivity is inherited from Crossing, not postulated") is **sound but definitional-in-the-interpretation**: the theorem "F exists ⇒ σ² = id in im(F)" is a tautology of the universal property (F preserves the relation). The substantive content is the *existence* of a nontrivial involutive target — which is the kinematical input, not a free theorem of the calculus. Claim A should be re-stated accordingly in the [DERIVATION] write-up.

## 3. Construction sketch (conditional on the existence condition)

Given a symmetric (or A⁴=1 TL) target C:

1. **Present the calculus as a prop** — finite presentation with generators {M, c: M⊗M→M⊗M} and relations {Calling, Position, Transposition}. (The prop presentation of LoF is itself a deliverable; partial in [KNOWN] literature: Kauffman's knot-logic reading — the Kauffman bracket IS the TL embedding, i.e., the algebraic image of the crossing. In-corpus anchor: 21208368's chain B_n → TL_n → Jones, citing Kauffman 1991.)
2. **Define F by the universal property:** M ↦ chosen object; c ↦ σ_{M,M} (the braiding component; involutive in C by hypothesis).
3. **Calling in the image:** the idempotent projectors P± = (id ± σ)/2 satisfy P±² = P± (rigor pass Claim B) — the two exchange channels.
4. **Transposition ↔ hexagon/naturality:** the third LoF axiom must correspond to the braid/hexagon relations of the braiding; **open check** — the one remaining relation-level verification.
5. **Interpretive question (flag for the rigor pass):** the alternative reading of Calling as an idempotent-object law (M⊗M ≅ M) would itself be a strong abelian-pair-like input — the program's P²=P reading avoids this, but the choice must be argued, not assumed.

## 4. Falsifiability (F2'', the checkable remainder)

F2'' — the derivation program is executable iff all four hold:
(i) the prop presentation of the calculus is finite and explicit (with Transposition);
(ii) F exists iff the target braiding is involutive (established above, conditional on (i));
(iii) in the image, the exchange representation of S_n on N marks is the two-channel character structure, identified with Calling (+1) and Crossing (−1);
(iv) Transposition maps to the hexagon/naturality relations.
The calculus derives the statistics' *values* and their *Calling/Crossing identification* in any involutive target; it does **not** force the target's involutivity (kinematical, d≥3) and does **not** force channel-count (abelian-pair postulate, DHR locality — rigor pass HARD-1, unchanged).
