## 1. Setup

Work in a Z/2-graded (super) symmetric monoidal category C with tensor ⊗, unit I, and braiding

    σ_{A,B}: A⊗B → B⊗A,   σ_{A,B}(a⊗b) = (-1)^{|a||b|} b⊗a,

where |x| ∈ {0,1} is the parity of the homogeneous element x. The braiding is an involution: σ_{B,A}∘σ_{A,B} = id.

**The mark** M is the primitive distinction: an object with a distinguished element m of odd parity, |m| = 1. The treatise's two laws become:
- Calling (idempotence): μ: M⊗M → M with μ(m⊗m) = m — a multiplication that is a retraction of the unit.
- Crossing (involution): the braiding σ_{M,M}(m⊗m) = -m⊗m — exchange acquires the sign (-1) for two odd elements.

Both laws are consequences of the *same* exchange operator on M⊗M (see T2); this notebook fixes the algebraic side.

## 2. The two projections of the exchange operator

On A⊗A define the exchange operator P = σ_{A,A}. Since P² = id, we have two idempotent projectors:

    P_sym = (1 + P)/2,   P_antisym = (1 - P)/2,

with P_sym² = P_sym, P_antisym² = P_antisym, P_sym + P_antisym = id, P_sym∘P_antisym = 0.

**Idempotence of the mark (Calling) is literally the idempotence of these projectors.** The treatise's claim that distinction is idempotent is exactly the statement that the two eigenspaces of exchange are stable under projection.

## 3. Two modal exponentials

Define two comonads on C (candidate exponentials):

    !_S(A) = ⊕_{n≥0} S^n(A) = ⊕_{n≥0} (A^{⊗n})_{S_n}          (symmetric power — trivial rep of S_n)
    !_Λ(A) = ⊕_{n≥0} Λ^n(A) = ⊕_{n≥0} (A^{⊗n})_{sgn}          (exterior power — sign rep of S_n)

**Key structural fact [KNOWN — super-linear algebra]:** in a Z/2-graded symmetric monoidal category, the graded symmetric algebra of an *odd* object equals its exterior algebra: Sym(A) = Λ(A) when |A| = 1 (the sign from the braiding implements antisymmetrization). Hence for the odd mark M:

    !_S(M) = !_Λ(M)   as graded algebras — the two statistics are the two *grading components* of one construction.

Concretely: (M⊗M)_{P_sym} = S²(M) carries the trivial rep (bosonic, symmetric), and (M⊗M)_{P_antisym} = Λ²(M) carries the sign rep (fermionic, antisymmetric). The same exchange operator P splits M⊗M into the two statistics.

**DiLL reading [KNOWN — differential linear logic]:** the exterior algebra Λ(A) with its differential (wedging with the codereliction/derivation) is a canonical model of the exponential in a differential category; the symmetric algebra S(A) is the other standard model. The treatise's Appendix A chooses S(A) — a *model choice*, not a derivation.

## 4. Derivation target (F2, part 1)

**Claim:** the two 1-dimensional representations of S_n (trivial and sign) — hence bosonic vs fermionic statistics — are *forced* by the exchange operator on M^{⊗n} once the mark is odd (|M| = 1) and the category is compact closed with an involutive braiding.

**Sketch:** the representation of S_n on M^{⊗n} factors through the braid group B_n via the permutation quotient; with an involutive braiding (symmetric category), the quotient is well defined and the only 1D characters are the trivial and sign characters, applied to the parity of the permutation. For n = 2 this is the projector split above. For general n, the character χ(π) = (-1)^{|π|} is the only nontrivial 1D character — the sign.
