## 1. The statement

Dimension does not change the invariant structure (superselection sectors + fusion + braiding); it changes *which braided structures are realizable*, hence which values of the spin parameter s are allowed.

## 2. The table

| Spatial dimension d | Motion group of identical particles | Braided structure | Allowed s | Statistics realized |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| d = 2 | Braid group B_n | Ribbon (non-symmetric braiding) | s ∈ R/Z (continuous) | Anyons: R = e^{2πis} arbitrary phase; non-abelian braiding for higher quantum dimension |
| d ≥ 3 | Permutation group S_n | Symmetric (involutive braiding) | s ∈ {0, 1/2} mod 1 (2s ∈ Z) | Bosons (s integer, η = +1), fermions (s half-integer, η = -1) |

**Reading:** the boson/fermion binary is the 3+1D shadow of the law R = e^{2πis}; dimension only quantizes the allowed values of s. In d = 2 the quantization collapses to continuity (anyons); in d ≥ 3 the involutive braiding forces 2s ∈ Z.

## 3. Where the Lorentz input enters (3+1D only)

In 3+1D relativistic QFT, the additional constraint is: the twist θ = e^{2πis} must equal the phase of a 2π rotation in the Lorentz representation of spin s.
