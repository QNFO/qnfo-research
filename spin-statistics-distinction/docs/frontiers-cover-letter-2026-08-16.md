Dear Editors,

I am submitting *The Boson/Fermion Distinction: Spin-Statistics as Structural Invariant* as an Original Research article to the Mathematical and Theoretical Physics section. The manuscript argues that the boson/fermion dichotomy is a derived, dimension-dependent shadow of a single structural relation — the exchange phase of identical particles equals their topological spin, R = e^{2πis} — and reports the outcome of the paper's own pre-registered falsification test of a distinction-based derivation program.

**(a) Falsifiability-first.** The paper's differentiator is that it states, in advance, exactly what would prove it wrong. Condition F2 was pre-registered: if the derivation of the two exchange eigenvalues (±1) from the mark calculus requires importing involutivity (σ² = 1) or the abelian-pair postulate as axioms — rather than deriving them from Calling + Crossing with compact closure as the only categorical structure — the derivation program is disconfirmed. The T2 rigor pass (executed 2026-08-16, artifacts included in the submission) executed this test, and the outcome is reported honestly in §7 and §10: **F2 is disconfirmed in its strong form**, with three independent mechanisms named (abelianity is presupposed, not delivered; the interpretation functor F exists iff the target braiding is already involutive; the calculus's syntactic signature is the Lawvere theory of Boolean algebras, so the binary exchange is added target structure). The surviving pre-registered condition F2′ — a channel-count postulate tied to the locality-based parastatistics exclusion (DHR) — is stated precisely.

**(b) What is derived vs. assumed.** The Parsimony Ledger (§10) counts every primitive, labels each DERIVED or ASSUMED, and states the standing debt. The mark calculus is shown to supply the *syntax* of the involutive quotient S_n = B_n/⟨σᵢ² = 1⟩ (verified quantitatively in the Temperley–Lieb algebra), while the *selection* of the involutive target remains external kinematical input. The minimal ontology that produces exchange statistics is logic + locality + kinematics + Lorentz; the paper says so explicitly rather than relocating the burden. A reviewer's sharpest objection is converted into the paper's own declared test.

**(c) Novelty boundary.** To my knowledge this is the first paper in the distinction/calculus-of-indications tradition to state a derivation *target* for the spin–statistics connection and then publicly report its falsification outcome with a mechanism. The manuscript's §5 boundary is explicitly drawn against the recent parastatistics literature: Mekonnen–Galley–Müller, "Invariance under quantum permutations rules out parastatistics" (arXiv:2502.17576) and the Wang–Hazzard line (2025–26). No conflict: the paper's surviving claim is the invariant formulation (R = e^{2πis}), which is independent of the failed derivation route, and the failed route is reported precisely so that effort is not duplicated.

The manuscript is a single-author preprint (v1.6, published 2026-08-16 under CC-BY-4.0 with full provenance: source repository, evidence files, and the executable verification notebooks accompanying the submission). All external claims are documented in the artifacts. I confirm no conflicts of interest and no external funding.
