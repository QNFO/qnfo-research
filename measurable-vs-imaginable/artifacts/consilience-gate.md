## Core Dynamic

**A system draws an operationally enforceable boundary between what it can verify (finite, constructible, measurable) and what it cannot (infinite, non-constructible, imaginable). The boundary IS the system's identity — not a limitation on the system, but a definition of what the system IS.**

## Domain Translations

### Physics
- **Lexicon:** Measurement, falsifiability, observable
- **Instance:** The Lambda-CDM model uses only computable reals; the cosmological constant is a measured parameter, not a Chaitin constant. No physical theory has ever required a non-computable number to make a distinguishable prediction.
- **Ramification:** Any theory that REQUIRES a non-computable real (e.g., a fundamental constant equal to Ω) is untestable at any finite precision → not physics.

### Computer Science
- **Lexicon:** Decidable languages, halting problem, computational complexity
- **Instance:** The set of decidable languages (recursive sets) is countable; the set of all languages (2^Σ*) is uncountable. Every real program computes a recursive set. The undecidable languages correspond to non-computable reals — they "exist" formally but cannot be effectively recognized.
- **Ramification:** A computational system that "requires" solving the halting problem is not a real computer — it's a mathematical idealization. Physics : measurement :: computation : decidability.

### Cognitive Science
- **Lexicon:** Perception, confabulation, reality testing
- **Instance:** The human visual system fills in the blind spot — it generates "internally consistent content" (a perceptual fiction) for a region it cannot measure. This is structurally identical to non-computable reals in formal systems: internally consistent (by definition of the formal system) but externally unverifiable (no measurement can confirm).
- **Ramification:** "Cognitive fiction" is not a pejorative — it's a structural category. The brain fills perceptual gaps the way mathematics fills proof gaps in non-constructive existence theorems. The measurable-vs-imaginable distinction maps onto perception-vs-confabulation.

### Information Theory
- **Lexicon:** Compressibility, channel capacity, noise floor
- **Instance:** A non-computable real is algorithmically incompressible — it cannot be generated by any finite program shorter than the number itself. A computable real IS compressible (its generating program is the compression). The measurement channel has finite capacity; what cannot fit through the channel is informational fiction from the receiver's perspective.
- **Ramification:** The falsifiability gate IS an information-theoretic channel constraint: measurement bandwidth is finite, so what exceeds it is epistemically inaccessible. This is NOT a technological limitation — it's a definitional one per the channel coding theorem.

### Biology
- **Lexicon:** Selection, viability, fitness landscape
- **Instance:** A genotype that cannot produce a viable phenotype under any environmental condition is "biologically non-existent" — it's a formal possibility in the space of all DNA sequences but zero selective pressure can realize it. Non-computable reals are the same: formal possibilities in the space of all Dedekind cuts but zero physical process can select for them.
- **Ramification:** The natural selection boundary is falsifiability in biological form. An "organism" that cannot interact with any environment is not alive; a "quantity" that cannot interact with any measurement apparatus is not physical.

### Sociology
- **Lexicon:** Norms, enforcement, legitimacy
- **Instance:** A law that cannot be enforced (no measurement of compliance, no sanction for violation) is a "dead letter" — it exists on paper but has zero social reality. The enforceable/unenforceable boundary is isomorphic to the measurable/unmeasurable boundary.
- **Ramification:** "Physics" is to "mathematics/creative fiction" as "enforced law" is to "aspirational declaration." Both pairs share the same structural relation: operationally real vs. formally possible.

## Synthesis Consilience

**Meta-Principle:** The distinction between "what a system can verify" and "what a system can construct" is NOT a bug in the system — it is the system's operational definition. Across all six domains, the boundary between the verifiable and the unverifiable IS the boundary of the domain's ontology. You don't "fix" it; you recognize it as what makes the domain what it is.

**Frontier Question:** If we relax the assumption that measurement must use finite resources (i.e., allow transfinite measurement protocols), does the computable/non-computable boundary dissolve, or does a new boundary appear at a higher cardinality — e.g., between what is and isn't definable in any formal language at all?

## Research Integration

- **Scoping:** The Cognitive Science translation suggests a fruitful line of investigation: "cognitive fiction" is not merely an analogy — it's the same structural phenomenon at the psychological level. The brain's confabulation mechanisms (split-brain studies, choice blindness, false memory) are the cognitive equivalent of non-constructive existence proofs.
- **Deep Dive:** The Information Theory translation suggests formalizing the falsifiability gate as a channel capacity theorem. If measurement bandwidth is finite, there exists a maximum Kolmogorov complexity beyond which quantities become epistemically inaccessible — a "measurement horizon" analogous to the cosmological event horizon.
- **Execution:** The CS translation provides the cleanest bridge to existing formalisms: the physics/math boundary IS the decidable/undecidable boundary for the language of measurement. Define L_meas as the set of quantities distinguishable by finite measurement; prove L_meas = ℝ_comp (computable reals). For a prototype, implement a formal proof that any quantity requiring a non-computable real to express is undecidable in the measurement language.
