## Research idea

Zitterbewegung correlator as a Z₂ invariant distinguishing Majorana from Dirac fermions. Claim: the Majorana Zitterbewegung current correlator vanishes identically, so the ZBW order parameter ℑ_O_{ZBW} acts as a Z₂ topological invariant (0 for Majorana, ≈1 for Dirac) that is intrinsically protected against local perturbations, and the ZBW transition graph on the Bruhat-Tits tree has ~57% fewer edges for Majoranas. Why it matters: this would give a measurable, hardware-level topological marker for Majorana modes and a route to projective readout of topological qubits without active error correction. Test: derive the current correlator for the adelic/Majorana Dirac equation and prove the vanishing and Z₂ quantization rigorously; simulate the ZBW transition graph on the Bruhat-Tits tree to verify the edge-count reduction and invariant stability under perturbations; and analyze feasibility of the proposed spin-noise, EELS/RIXS, and Gromov-δ protocols for detecting the invariant in candidate Majorana systems.
