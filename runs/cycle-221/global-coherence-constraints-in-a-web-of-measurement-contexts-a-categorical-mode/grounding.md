## Research idea

Reality as a Web of Measurement Contexts with Global Coherence Constraints. The note conjectures that the ontic/epistemic distinction collapses: there is no measurement-independent 'territory'; what a thing is equals the totality of its behaviors across all measurement contexts, related by translation rules (renormalization group, product formula, logical consistency) and subject to global coherence conditions. A formal paper could test this by constructing a mathematical model: define a category whose objects are measurement contexts and whose morphisms are translation rules, then prove that (a) coherence conditions analogous to the product formula or RG flow force a unique consistent assignment of 'values' per context, and (b) no context-independent value exists (no terminal object or invariant section). Simulations of RG flows between effective descriptions could check whether cross-scale translation fully determines observables without positing a 'true' underlying state, or reveal whether hidden context-free invariants reappear — which would refute the collapse thesis.
