## Research idea

Archimedean Axiom as Unrecognized Coordinate Choice Generating Physics Epicycles. The note's central conjecture is that the Archimedean axiom is not a necessary foundation of mathematics but an arbitrary coordinate choice whose reification generates specific 'epicycles' in physics and computation: UV divergences, set-theoretic paradoxes, Wheeler-DeWitt timelessness, and the null pointer. It proposes the non-Archimedean Bruhat-Tits tree as the true invariant structure, with the Monna map as the projection from discrete to continuous, and a 'Keplerian criterion' for adjudication: a framework wins if it derives known numbers (e.g., fine-structure constant) from first principles with fewer free parameters. A paper could test this by (1) formalizing the four claimed structural isomorphisms (set paradox ↔ distinction-calculus oscillation, continuum divergences ↔ p-adic tree renormalization, etc.) as precise correspondences; (2) attempting renormalization of a known UV-divergent quantity on the p-adic tree and comparing free-parameter counts against the continuum treatment; (3) analyzing whether the residual background structures (von Neumann algebras, projective geometry) undermine the claim of background independence; and (4) addressing how a static tree yields emergent time under the Wheeler-DeWitt constraint.
