## Research idea

abc Uncertainty Principle Bounds Additive Leakage of Prime Factors. Conjecture: the abc conjecture, recast via valuation vectors v(n)=(v_2,v_3,v_5,...), acts as an uncertainty principle limiting how precisely an additive relation m=a+b constrains the multiplicative structure (prime factorizations) of its terms. Formally, rad(abc)-type inequalities should translate into an information-theoretic ceiling on mutual information between an additive observation and the valuation vector of a secret, giving provable leakage bounds for cryptographic circuits. Test by: (1) deriving an explicit entropy bound I(additive trace; v(n)) from known abc-style inequalities (or weaker effective versions), (2) simulating side-channel leakage on toy RSA-like primitives, measuring empirical correlation between additive traces and valuation vectors, and (3) comparing observed leakage against the derived ceiling to check whether the ultrametric/abc framing yields tighter, provable leakage-resilience guarantees than existing bounds.
