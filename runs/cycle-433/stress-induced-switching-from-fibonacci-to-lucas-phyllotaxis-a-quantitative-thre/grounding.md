## Research idea

Stress-Induced Switching from Fibonacci to Lucas Phyllotaxis. Conjecture: plant phyllotaxis follows Fibonacci sequences only below a critical environmental stress threshold; beyond it, meristem dynamics switch to Lucas sequences (or other phyllotactic solutions). This yields a testable 'stress-dependent optimization threshold' model and a proposed 'phyllotactic resilience index' quantifying pattern stability under resource variation. It matters because ~92% of plants show Fibonacci patterns while the ~8% of non-Fibonacci cases lack a quantitative mechanistic explanation linking pattern choice to environmental conditions. A derivation could extend existing dynamical models of primordia packing (e.g., Douady-Couder type simulations) by adding stress-dependent parameters (nutrient flux, water potential) and showing analytically or numerically that divergence angle solutions bifurcate from the golden angle to Lucas-related angles as stress parameters cross critical values. Controlled growth experiments varying stressors could then measure divergence-angle distributions and test predicted switching thresholds and the resilience index's predictive power.
