## Research idea

Vector Space Model of Interdisciplinary Knowledge and Novelty. Model disciplines as orthogonal basis vectors in an n-dimensional knowledge space, where an individual's knowledge state is a vector of expertise coefficients and a research idea's innovativeness is measured by how many dimensions it meaningfully spans. Claim: the most valuable insights are 'diagonal' vectors integrating multiple fields, and LLM-augmented inquiry shifts the bottleneck from information access to cognitive bandwidth for synthesis. This could be tested by embedding papers/fields in a vector space, defining an interdisciplinarity-novelty metric (number of significant components, angular distance from existing work), and correlating it with citation impact or breakthrough status across corpora. Simulations could also test whether the 'curse of dimensionality' limits synthesis quality as integrated dimensions grow, and whether 'semantic collage' (surface keyword combination) is distinguishable from genuine synthesis using these metrics.
