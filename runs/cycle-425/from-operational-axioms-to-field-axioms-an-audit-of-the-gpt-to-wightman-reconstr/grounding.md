## Research idea

Deriving Wightman QFT axioms from generalized probabilistic theory axioms. The note claims that four operational GPT axioms—causality, no-signaling, local tomography, and continuous reversibility—applied to systems with infinitely many degrees of freedom, necessarily yield the Wightman axioms of relativistic quantum field theory (microcausality, localized observables, unitary Poincaré representation). This would be a genuine reconstruction theorem bridging quantum axiomatics and QFT. A paper could test it by formalizing each GPT axiom in the operator-algebraic setting and attempting a rigorous derivation of each Wightman axiom, identifying precisely where infinite degrees of freedom enter, whether local tomography is actually required (it fails for fermions), and whether the derivation uniquely selects quantum theory over alternatives like quaternionic or real Hilbert-space theories. Constructive counterexamples or gap analyses would clarify if the claim is a theorem or a conjecture.
