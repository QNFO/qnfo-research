## Research idea

CEI Invariance: Architectures as Equivalence Classes of Causal Density and Energy Fidelity. Claim: computational architectures form equivalence classes under a product invariant ρₜ × ηₑ = constant for a given substrate, where causal density (dependent operations per volume-time) trades off against energy fidelity (energy per transition relative to the Landauer limit). This predicts substrates 'prefer' architectures minimizing causal-energy mismatch, yielding a substrate-architecture mapping (silicon→pipelined parallelism, DNA→stochastic search, superconductors→error-corrected quantum). Test: (1) derive the invariant from Landauer's principle plus causal-cone bounds on operation rates; (2) empirically fit ρₜ and ηₑ for measured platforms (CMOS, Loihi, GPUs, DNA computing, photonic) and check whether the product is substrate-constant; (3) simulate architectures on mismatched substrates to verify the mismatch penalty. Confirmation would give a thermodynamic classification of computation; falsification would localize where the tradeoff breaks.
