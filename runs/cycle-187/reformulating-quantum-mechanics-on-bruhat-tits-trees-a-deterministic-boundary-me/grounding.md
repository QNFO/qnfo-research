## Research idea

Reformulating Quantum Mechanics on a Bruhat–Tits Tree. The note conjectures that quantum mechanics can be rebuilt on an ultrametric (p-adic) state space: energy states are nodes of a Bruhat–Tits tree, the wavefunction is a deterministic branching path, measurement is a projection (Monna map) from tree to Archimedean line, and the Born rule emerges geometrically as the fraction of tree-boundary points lying in the measured container. Why it matters: this would dissolve the measurement problem and wave-particle duality as projection artifacts, and derive quantization from tree structure rather than postulate it. Testable via: (1) formal derivation that boundary-measure counting on T_p reproduces |c_i|^2 for standard finite-dimensional systems; (2) simulation of a blackbody spectrum computed with the 2-adic metric to check whether its Archimedean projection matches Planck's law; (3) analysis of whether entanglement correlations correspond to shared deep containers, reproducing Bell-inequality statistics without nonlocality.
