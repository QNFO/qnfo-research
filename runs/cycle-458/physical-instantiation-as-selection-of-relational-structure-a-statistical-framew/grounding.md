## Research idea

Physical instantiation as selection of mathematical relational structures. Conjecture: mathematics is an autonomous relational grammar, and physics works by nature 'selecting' a subset of that grammar whose internal relational skeleton is isomorphic to physical behavior—so the success of abstract structures (e.g., complex Hilbert space in QM) reflects shared invariant symmetries, not Platonic identity. Testable reformulation: for a set of mathematical structures developed without physical motivation, predict which ones later find physical application, and check whether selection correlates with structural invariants (symmetry groups, conservation laws) rather than historical or pragmatic factors. A formal analysis could define an isomorphism measure between algebraic relational skeletons and dynamical systems, then simulate or survey cases (quaternions, fiber bundles, complex numbers) to test whether 'pre-discovery' of physically instantiated structures is statistically non-accidental and predictable from structural features alone.
