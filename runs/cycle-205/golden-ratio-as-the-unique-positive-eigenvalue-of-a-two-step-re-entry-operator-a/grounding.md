## Research idea

Golden ratio as eigenvalue of re-entry operator selecting physical states. The note's strongest original conjecture is that the golden ratio need not be posited in an axiomatic physics framework but can be derived: Φ is the unique positive eigenvalue of the simplest two-step re-entry operator (Spencer-Brown's Laws of Form), since the Fibonacci recurrence is exactly the integer dynamics of x = 1 + 1/x, and Zeckendorf representation gives the canonical form of elements in the Heyting algebra generated by that fixed point. The claim to test: topological resonance selection of stable states on an S¹ substrate with integer winding numbers is equivalent to nucleus fixed-point discreteness on a Heyting algebra, making winding-number completeness a theorem rather than an axiom. A derivation could formalize the re-entry operator in Lean 4 (building on Kauffman's proof that re-entry generates Heyting algebras), prove uniqueness of the positive eigenvalue, and transport the resonance-selector and spectral-dimension-flow claims as theorems from existing nucleus infrastructure. Computational verification would be a machine-checked repository with zero 'sorry', plus exhaustive checks of the Zeckendorf canonical-form correspondence.
