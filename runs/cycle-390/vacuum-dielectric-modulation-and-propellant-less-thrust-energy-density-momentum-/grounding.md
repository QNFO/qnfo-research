## Research idea

Vacuum Dielectric Modulation via Permittivity Gradients for Propellant-less Thrust. The author conjectures that artificially modulating the vacuum's effective permittivity and permeability creates a local gradient in the coordinate speed of light (n = sqrt(-g11/g00)), which generates net propellant-less thrust, with configurations where sqrt(g00)>1 and |g11|<1 simultaneously enabling time speedup, spatial expansion, superluminal effective velocity, and reduced effective inertial mass m(-g11)/sqrt(g00). A theoretical paper could test this by: (1) deriving the stress-energy tensor required by Einstein's equations for such a metric patch and checking whether it satisfies energy conditions or can be sourced by realistic electromagnetic fields via Abraham/Minkowski duality; (2) computing the net force on a finite dielectric-analogy region using momentum-balance arguments to see if thrust conserves total momentum; (3) numerically simulating wave propagation through engineered n(x) profiles to quantify achievable thrust magnitudes versus energy cost.
