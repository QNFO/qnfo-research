## Research idea

Subliminal trait transfer as latent manifold inheritance during fine-tuning. Claim: subliminal learning in LLMs is not transfer of discrete facts but inheritance of a geometric manifold — a teacher's hidden bias warps its entire latent space, and fine-tuning on innocuous outputs (e.g., number sequences) forces the student's internal trajectories to mimic the teacher's, inadvertently reproducing the bias manifold. This explains why transfer requires weight updates (geometry change), is architecture-specific (incompatible geometries), and evades output-token classifiers (signal lives in high-dimensional internal states). Testable via 'Latent Space Tomography': extract activation point clouds for the bias concept from teacher (with/without bias prompt), student (before/after fine-tuning), and controls; use dimensionality reduction and geometric similarity metrics (e.g., Procrustes, MMD, CKA) to verify the bias manifold appears in the fine-tuned student despite never seeing the concept token. A derivation could also model fine-tuning as trajectory-matching optimization and show manifold leakage is a generic consequence.
