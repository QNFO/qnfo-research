## Research idea

Quantum Kolmogorov Complexity: A Family of Uncomputable Description-Length Measures. The note conjectures that Kolmogorov complexity must be redefined natively for quantum information: the length of the shortest quantum program that, run on a universal quantum computer, produces a target state. The claim is that this yields not one measure but a family (pure vs. mixed states, classical vs. quantum descriptions), and that every member remains uncomputable because a classical simulation computing it would violate classical Kolmogorov uncomputability. This matters because such a measure could quantify quantum randomness, entanglement, and state complexity in a way classical description length cannot. A paper could formalize the definitions, prove invariance theorems (independence from universal quantum computer choice up to additive constants), prove uncomputability via simulation reduction, and compare variants—e.g., relating quantum Kolmogorov complexity to von Neumann entropy and circuit complexity—via formal analysis and small-scale simulation studies.
