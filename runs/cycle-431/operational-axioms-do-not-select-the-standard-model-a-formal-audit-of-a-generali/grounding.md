## Research idea

Deriving QFT and the Standard Model from Generalized Probabilistic Theory Axioms. The author conjectures that four operational axioms of generalized probabilistic theories (causality, no-signaling, local tomography, continuous reversibility), when extended to systems with infinitely many degrees of freedom, necessarily yield quantum field theory satisfying the Wightman axioms, and further that combining this with Poincaré symmetry uniquely reconstructs the full Standard Model Lagrangian. This matters because it would ground the SM in information-theoretic principles rather than postulated Hilbert-space structure. A formal analysis could test the conjectured necessity: prove or refute whether the finite-dimensional GPT reconstructions (e.g., Hardy, Wilce) survive the thermodynamic/infinite-degree-of-freedom limit, whether local tomography plus continuous reversibility forces the Wightman axioms or admits exotic GPT field theories, and whether the reconstruction uniquely selects SU(3)xSU(2)xU(1) or permits alternative gauge groups and fermion content.
