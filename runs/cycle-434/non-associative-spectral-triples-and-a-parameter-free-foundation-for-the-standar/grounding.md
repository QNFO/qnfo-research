## Research idea

Non-Associative Spectral Triples: Octonionic NCG as Parameter-Free Standard Model Foundation. The note's core conjecture is that the Standard Model's parameter problem can be resolved by building non-commutative geometry on the octonions: a spectral triple whose algebra is non-associative, with a universal Dirac operator that is a fixed point of a self-consistency (autaxys) condition, yielding particle masses, coupling constants, three generations, and a falsifiable blue-tilted primordial gravitational-wave spectrum contradicting standard inflation. It matters because standard spectral action requires associativity, so the program's decisive first test is mathematical: either generalize spectral triples to non-associative algebras, or find an associative embedding (matrix or Clifford algebra over octonions) where non-associativity appears as symmetry breaking. A concrete first step is a toy-model Dirac operator whose octonionic resonance condition reproduces the Koide formula for charged leptons, followed by cosmological modeling of the ~10^-15 eV dark matter candidate against LSST small-scale structure data.
