## Research idea

Formal Isomorphism Criteria for Automated Cross-Disciplinary Terminology Mapping. The author conjectures that deep connections between concepts across disciplines can be rigorously identified by requiring structural isomorphism, functional equivalence, or logical interdependence—rather than analogy—and that such verified equivalences yield a principled 'terminology crosswalk' mapping terms between domains. The research question: can formal criteria (e.g., category-theoretic isomorphism, shared axiomatic structure, identical functional roles) be operationalized so that a computational pipeline reliably distinguishes genuine structural equivalence from superficial analogy in text corpora? This matters because cross-domain knowledge transfer currently relies on intuition. A paper could formalize the three connection types mathematically, define a decidability or scoring procedure, implement it on curated multi-domain corpora, and evaluate whether extracted crosswalks match expert-validated concept alignments, testing precision/recall against analogy-only baselines.
