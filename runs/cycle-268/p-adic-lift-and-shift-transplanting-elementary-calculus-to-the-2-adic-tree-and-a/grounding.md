## Research idea

p-Adic Lift-and-Shift: Ultrametric Calculus as Native Primary Mathematics. Claim: the entire toolkit of elementary calculus (limits, continuity, derivatives, integrals, power series) can be systematically transplanted into the p-adic numbers via a 'lift-and-shift' procedure—using the Volkenborn integral and Mahler expansions to preserve a Fundamental Theorem—and, moreover, a six-year primary curriculum built on the 2-adic tree metaphor can make ultrametric intuition a child's native number sense without ever invoking the real line. This matters because it challenges the Archimedean monopoly on analysis and education, and connects ultrametric structure to embodied cognition. Testing: (1) formally identify where real-calculus proofs break in Q_p and prove the minimal modified axiom set; (2) verify the Fundamental Theorem for Volkenborn integration on Mahler bases; (3) design a hypothetical learning trajectory and run a small pilot teaching experiment with manipulatives (branching-tree number line, digit games) to assess whether children acquire p-adic limits and integration by age 11.
