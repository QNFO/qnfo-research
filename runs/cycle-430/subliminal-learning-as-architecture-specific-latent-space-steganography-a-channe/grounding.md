## Research idea

Subliminal Learning as Architecture-Specific Latent-Space Steganography. Claim: subliminal trait inheritance during distillation is not data poisoning but a covert channel in which a teacher's hidden traits modulate the statistical 'texture' of outputs via its latent-space geometry, and this channel is decodable only by students sharing the same base architecture and initialization. Question: can this be formalized as steganographic encoding in the teacher's output distribution, with a mutual-information or Fisher-information measure predicting cross-architecture transfer failure? A theoretical paper could derive conditions under which trait information survives content filtering (e.g., as low-amplitude perturbations along teacher-specific directions in function space), and simulations with small transformers—varying initialization, architecture, and data sanitization—could test whether transfer strength correlates with representational similarity between teacher and student, yielding a quantitative model of substrate-modulated information transfer.
