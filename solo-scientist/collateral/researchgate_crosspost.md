## The Force-Multiplier Playbook: How One Scientist + One LLM Can Match a Research Team

**DOI:** [10.5281/zenodo.20154578](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20154578)

### Abstract

Modern science is dominated by large collaborations, leaving solo researchers at a structural disadvantage — not because they lack ideas, but because they lack throughput. This whitepaper presents a structured five-phase protocol that turns a large language model from a chatbot into a force multiplier, enabling a single scientist to reproduce the output of a small research team.

We tested the protocol on two real research problems: (1) resolving the cosmological constant discrepancy via ultrametric quantum gravity (theoretical physics, ~$25\times$ speedup), and (2) a cross-linguistic Bayesian analysis of 22 languages (computational linguistics, ~$90\times$ speedup). Both were completed in approximately one day of focused human direction each.

The playbook includes: the five-phase protocol, a four-component technology stack, five reusable prompt templates covering the full research pipeline, a four-gate verification protocol, and an honest limitations section covering when the protocol fails and what we don't yet know.

### Key Contributions

1. **A formal protocol** for systematic LLM-assisted research
2. **Preliminary self-experiments** demonstrating $25\times$ to $90\times$ throughput amplification
3. **A verification framework** that caught 100% of issues in blind reader testing
4. **A call to action** — the One-Day Challenge — inviting researchers across domains to test, measure, and report
