## 1. Positioning (user directive 2026-08-20, embed in external communications where relevant)

The operator is not a single-domain subject-matter expert: they are an **interdisciplinary / transdisciplinary / cross-cutting research manager** using cutting-edge tools to tackle hard problems that humans alone have been unable to fully resolve. Machines can help if we let them — and let go of the myth of human superior intelligence.
