# Kaizen Proposal — SO-WHAT GATE for all publications (draft, NOT installed)

**Proposed by:** user mandate 2026-08-16 (session EbWqja7WKlk1h_whbR6UE, RES.011 v0.3 cycle)
**Status:** DRAFT — requires user approval before any skill install (kaizen protocol)

## The mandate (verbatim policy)
"SO WHAT? WHY SHOULD A READER CARE?" applies to ALL research and EVERY publication
(including social media posts). Only prioritize and advance research with a purpose
and real-world utility (even if conceptual, not-yet-realized). Research must advance
theory to be credible, but without a practical application it is effectively useless.
A theory/theorem is only as deep as its premises: how deep does it go, and where do
its premises end?

## Proposed gate (Phase 5 addition, research skill)
1. Every publication MUST contain an explicit so-what section or subsection that:
   (a) states the practical/real-world utility (even if conceptual/not-yet-realized);
   (b) names who benefits and what they can do with it;
   (c) applies the premises-depth framing: what is proven, what is assumed, where the
       premises end.
2. Every social media post announcing a publication MUST carry the so-what framing
   (not just title + link). Verified at P7: post text must contain the utility claim.
3. Phase 4 planning: research proposals lacking a purpose/utility statement are
   deprioritized (user: "only prioritize and advance research with a purpose").
