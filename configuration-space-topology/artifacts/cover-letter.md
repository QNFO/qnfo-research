To the Editor, Frontiers in Physics — Quantum Computing and Quantum Information

Dear Editor,

I am submitting for consideration the manuscript *"Configuration-Space Topology and the Distinction Calculus: The Exchange Scalar, Its ±1 Shadow, and a Pre-Registered Derivation Program"* (concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21945449; latest version at submission).

I am an independent researcher (ORCID 0009-0002-4317-5604), unaffiliated and unfunded, working under the QNFO Research Foundation. The manuscript is published as an open-access preprint with complete provenance — full source, references, citation audit, and external-search evidence all deposited alongside it.

**The strongest reason to consider it is its self-constraining structure.** Rather than asserting a derivation, it pre-registers one and states its falsification conditions up front:
- **F1** — exhibit a braided monoidal category generated by the calculus whose one-dimensional braid representations omit the abelian phases, or show the Grothendieck–Teichmüller action is inessential to its structure.
- **F2** — show the mark calculus cannot yield exactly two exchange eigenvalues without re-importing the abelian-pair postulate (the symmetric-algebra choice).
- **F3** — show spatial dimension enters exchange statistics other than through the allowed braided structures.

The central conjecture — the Distinction Calculus lifted into homotopy type theory, with the Grothendieck–Teichmüller group acting on braided monoidal categories, rendering boundary-drawing an arithmetic act — is labeled **[CONJECTURE]**. The spin-statistics connection (which eigenvalue maps to which spin) is conceded **[ACKNOWLEDGED]** to require Lorentz symmetry, microcausality, and positive energy that the calculus cannot supply.

**Two prior-obstacles are addressed directly.** §6.2 carries an explicit differentiation table against the cohesive-HoTT program of Sati and Schreiber (1408.0054, 2206.13563, 2209.08331, 2303.02382, 2408.11896) — novelty is claimed only for the route through the re-entrant mark, never the mathematical objects. §4.1 engages, rather than dismisses, the contested claim of a spin-statistics connection without Lorentz invariance (Li Ge, 10.5281/zenodo.21330410).

**Why a reader should care.** The paper is a boundary-map, not a discovery claim. It makes the abelian-pair postulate explicit as a *named input* — exposing a silent assumption inherited from the Laws-of-Form tradition — and gives the "±1 dichotomy" a theorem-shaped statement with its depth disclosed (R = ±1 ⇔ π₁(C_N(ℝ^d)) = S_N, conditional on that postulate and on the involutive braiding). It pre-registers falsifiable derivation targets T1–T3. It does *not* claim to derive statistics from logic; it locates precisely where such a derivation would have to succeed or fail.

I welcome adversarial review. If the derivation targets or the novelty framing are flawed, I want that surfaced.
