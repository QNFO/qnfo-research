## §1 Charter

Configuration-space topology (CST) is the modern mathematical framework that
derives exchange statistics from the topology of the configuration space of
indistinguishable particles: π₁(C_N(M)) = S_N for d ≥ 3 (bosons/fermions) and
π₁(C_N(M)) = B_N for d = 2 (anyons). It is the bridge between the QNFO
distinction-theoretic program (Laws of Form → Distinction Calculus →
Re-Entrant Calculus) and the physical machine.

This project asks whether CST is the terminus or a map with known limits, and
whether the Distinction Calculus can supply what CST leaves silent: the origin
of the space in which the configurations live. The candidate unification —
surfaced in the source notes — is Distinction Calculus lifted into Homotopy
Type Theory (HoTT), whose intertwining symmetry is the Grothendieck–Teichmüller
(GT) group acting on braided monoidal categories: *drawing a boundary is not
just a logical act; it is an arithmetic act* (each statistical phase selects a
prime's worth of complexity).

## §1.2 Locked Core Claim (P6 — HARD)

**C1 (kinematical, [ESTABLISHED]):** Configuration-space topology —
exchange statistics as representations of π₁(C_N(M)) — is the correct modern
explanation of the boson/fermion/anyon trichotomy, but it is a derived
structure, not a terminus. Its silent scaffolds (point-like indistinguishable
particles, a fixed classical background manifold, externally imposed
dimension, an idealization-hard diagonal) mark its boundary as a *map*.

**C2 (synthesis conjecture, [CONJECTURE]):** The Distinction Calculus
reframes those scaffolds as acts of boundary-drawing. The conjecture: the
exchange phase R = e^(2πis) (RES.010) and the fundamental group π₁(C_N(M))
are two projections of one invariant — the re-entrant mark disciplined by
linearity — and the synthesis is formalized by lifting the Distinction
Calculus into HoTT, where the GT group's action on braided monoidal
categories renders boundary-drawing an arithmetic act.

**C3 (boundary, [ACKNOWLEDGED], per RES.009 §5):** The spin-statistics
connection (which exchange eigenvalue corresponds to which spin) requires
Lorentz symmetry, microcausality, and positive energy — inputs the mark
calculus alone cannot supply. This project does not claim to derive them.

**Falsification conditions:**
- **F1:** Exhibit a braided monoidal category generated by the calculus whose
  one-dimensional braid-group representations omit the abelian phases e^(iθ);
  or show the GT action is inessential to the category's structure.
- **F2:** Show the mark calculus cannot yield exactly two exchange eigenvalues
  (±1) without re-importing the symmetric-algebra choice (the SLB.002 sin) —
  i.e., the abelian-pair postulate is not derivable.
- **F3:** Show spatial dimension enters exchange statistics elsewhere than
  through the allowed braided structures (RES.009 T3 fails) — so "the binary is
  the 3D shadow" is unsupported.
