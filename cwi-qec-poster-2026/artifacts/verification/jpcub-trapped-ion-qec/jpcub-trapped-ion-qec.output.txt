-- Notes (C3 fix):
  * Foliated/treelike distillation: the ultrametric class's practical
    QEC appearance is in T-factory treelike/foliated distillation codes
    (Bravyi-Haah family) — a T-density axis would bring the tree class
    back into the comparison via the distillation layer, not the memory.
  * Repetition code: weight-1 uncorrectable X error (p_X = p2/(1+bias))
    makes it viable only at extreme bias (>=~1e3 at p2=1e-3) — the known
    biased-noise limit; XZZX exists precisely to keep bias advantage
    without the repetition's X weakness.
  * T-factory caveat (C1): both qLDPC and surface need magic states for
    universality; qLDPC non-transversal T overhead is a relative
    weakness. Ranking is workload-dependent — this artifact is the
    Clifford/memory slice.
  * Uncalibrated model choices (flagged): XZZX threshold mapping
    0.010*(1+bias)/2; erasure d_eff=1.5x; Bacon-Shor/Steane rough;
    p_L ansatz C=0.1. Sensitivity block shows ranking robustness.
