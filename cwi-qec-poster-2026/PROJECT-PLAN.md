# cwi-qec-poster-2026 — Project Plan

## Charter

Create a print-ready poster and supporting materials for the CWI Summer School
on Quantum Algorithms and Quantum Error Correction (24–28 August 2026, Amsterdam).
The poster presents open questions that led from fault-tolerant QEC to
ultrametric p-adic quantum metrology, targeting industry researchers attending
the workshop.

## Key Decisions

- **Tone:** Open questions, not answers. Curious, not confrontational.
- **Visual style:** Clean academic, dark header, light body, colored question boxes.
- **Target audience:** QEC researchers, especially industry (IonQ's Nicolas Delfosse).
- **Primary hook:** "What falsifies the roadmap?" + thermodynamic wall + ultrametric noise geometry.

## Success Criteria

1. Poster presented confidently at the summer school
2. At least one genuine conversation > 5 minutes with an industry attendee
3. At least one contact acquired for follow-up
4. Written notes on objections/questions raised
