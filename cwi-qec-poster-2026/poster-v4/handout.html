Things I Don't Understand About Quantum Error Correction

A naive audit of the constructs · CWI Summer School on QA & QEC · 24–28 August 2026 · Poster Session Wed 26 Aug

The Thesis (one paragraph)

Every human construct for understanding quantum computing is internally consistent — and the imports contradict each other. Spiders (categorical algebra), Pauli webs (stabilizer/QEC), and gadgets (MBQC/compilation) are maps drawn by different cartographers: coherent in the computational domain, contradictory where 3+1D particle physics and 1D thermodynamics are mixed into a 2D drawing plane without a compatibility check. The diagrams are not wrong — they are maps. The discipline of using a map is knowing where it ends. (Anchor: "ZX Diagrams at the Seam", 10.5281/zenodo.21992118)

The Method: feigned naivete as probe

I learned the room's language (the Bruhat–Tits tree T₂ as a ZX graph state — fig4) — and that is when the seams became visible. I ask the questions I'm not supposed to need to ask. The naive question is the external structural probe: where imports contradict, understanding breaks — and that break is data, not deficit.

Things I don't understand about QEC

  1. Why the discreteness? Is it physical, or imported from the circuit model? If the abstraction is a choice, fault tolerance is the tax on the choice.
  2. What is a logical qubit, physically? Hardware is bosonic; codes assume qubits. The map is 2D; the machine is continuous.
  3. What does a threshold actually guarantee? Proofs assume stochastic local noise; coherent/correlated errors are unpriced seams.
  4. Where is the quantum in the loop? Syndrome → classical decoder → feedback. QEC is quantum memory + classical control.
  5. Why isn't the decoder part of the code? A classical algorithm decides a quantum code's performance — two silos, never audited together.
  6. Why 2D geometry? Why a surface code and not a tree? What if error topology is ultrametric — the staircase nobody has looked for?
  7. Why count T gates, not joules? The field prices the algebraic resource; nobody prices the ancilla factory, the retries, the decoder.
  8. Why is the cat the unit of protection? Shor states are cat states — exponentially fragile, verified ancillas go stale. Protection from something that fragile?

The statement & the honest caption

The one-line statement: "I cannot grasp quantum computing through the practitioners' constructs because the constructs are maps drawn by different cartographers — internally consistent, mutually contradictory. My confusions are the fault lines."
Honest caption: feigned naivete is a method, not a pose — the naive question finds the seam. I am not claiming the field is wrong; I am claiming the imports were never checked against each other. If the constructs were compatible, the naive questions would answer themselves.
