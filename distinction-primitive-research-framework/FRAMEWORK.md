## 0. What this document is

The DPRF is the working contract for **where any QNFO claim lives and what it takes to move a claim
upward toward physical reality**. It has four parts:

1. **The pre-arithmetic ladder (D0–D8)** — nine levels, each constructed from the one below by a
   single named operation. Every research object in any QNFO program is assigned to a level.
2. **Two hard gates** — G1 (ontic) and G2 (realization) — the only legal ways to cross the
   boundaries the ladder creates. These codify, program-wide, the two cautions stated in the seed:
   do not reify distinction as ontic unless the commitment is explicit; never let a mathematical
   isomorphism pass as a physical realization without a measurement protocol and a falsification
   condition. **(P0.1 R4:** the seed's caution 2 names two items — protocol and falsification; G2 in §2 requires three, adding the null model from the treatise's Ch. 14 discipline and RES.030's matched-density null practice. Deliberate, flagged strengthening.**)**
3. **A claim taxonomy** — a fixed field schema that every pre-registered claim, audit row, and
   publication gate item can carry. It is the reuse surface: any program can attach it without
   changing its own WBS structure.
4. **An integration map** — where the scaffold plugs into the existing QNFO machinery (WBS P0–P9,
   pre-registration, red team, publication gates, corpus sweeps, registry, KG).

The DPRF makes **no empirical claims of its own**. Its claims are about process discipline. Empirical
content belongs to the records it cites (RES.030's D1/D2/D3 results, UMP.014's H1 benchmark, the
7-null ledger).

## 1. The pre-arithmetic ladder

> Distinction is the primitive; pre-arithmetic is structure without number; arithmetic is the
> counting and composition of distinctions; number theory studies the patterns of that composition;
> valuation assigns size; geometry is the resulting relational form; information is distinction made
> operational; measurement is the finite-resolution application of valuation to observation; and
> physics is the empirical filter that decides which of these structures are real.

| Level | Name | Content | Construction (ascent operation) |
|:-----:|:-----|:--------|:--------------------------------|
| **D0** | Distinction | A cut separating inside from outside. The minimal unit of structure. | primitive (given, not derived) |
| **D1** | Pre-arithmetic | Structure without number: order, hierarchy, partition, adjacency, nesting. | relate cuts (structure) |
| **D2** | Arithmetic | Counting and composition of distinctions: concatenation → addition; iteration over independent cut-families → multiplication; primes = irreducible multiplicative distinctions; unique factorization = the composition law. | count distinctions |
| **D3** | Number theory | Patterns of the composition: distribution of irreducibles, factorization statistics, L-functions as generating objects of the composition. | discern pattern |
| **D4** | Valuation | Assigning size to distinctions: norms, absolute values; Ostrowski — one Archimedean place plus one p-adic place per prime; the p-adic valuation is **one realization** of hierarchy distance, not its ground. | assign size |
| **D5** | Geometry | The resulting relational form: metric/ultrametric spaces as the form of valued distinctions; rigidity (the ultrametric triangle). | take form |
| **D6** | Information | Distinction made operational: counting distinctions as bits; entropy; where the distinctions are located (two-point functions vs. one-point thermodynamics — RES.030 D2, disconfirmed as pre-registered). | operationalize |
| **D7** | Measurement | Finite-resolution application of valuation to observation: protocol, instrument, resolution, noise budget; what a finite observer can actually distinguish. | resolve (finite) |
| **D8** | Physics | The empirical filter: falsification decides which of the structures are real. The null-ledger discipline lives here. | filter (falsify) |

### 1.1 Structural rules

- **R1 (declared level).** Every claim, hypothesis, model, or data interpretation declares its
  level or its span of levels. A claim that cannot state its level is not yet a claim. Intent
  markers ("D8-adjacent", "D7-oriented") are charter statements, not levels — a tag that is not
  a level is itself an R1 violation (P1 sweep GAP-2; canonical: RES.021).
- **R2 (default within-level).** Work within a level is the default and needs no justification.
  Cross-level moves need a declared **bridge**.
- **R3 (no skipping).** A move from level X to level Y > X is valid only if every intermediate
  bridge is declared. D2 → D8 claims are invalid unless D2→D3→D4→D5→D6→D7→D8 is each declared.
- **R4 (descent is interpretation).** A downward move (e.g., a physical result "reinterpreting"
  an arithmetic object) is an interpretation, never a derivation. It must say so.
- **R5 (level confusion is a defect class).** Conflating objects that live at different levels, or
  at different positions of the same level, is a registered audit defect. Worked instances already
  adjudicated in the lineage: prime-gap hard-core separations are D3 content about primes, not D3 content about Riemann zeros;
  specific-heat separation is D6/D7 content, not D5; "the universe is made of primes" is a D2→D8
  leak without protocol (the seed treatise's Ch. 14 leakage table — consolidation in progress at RES.031 — generalized here).

### 1.2 What each caution protects

- The **G1 caution** protects against *ontology smuggling*: using the ladder as if D0 were a fact
  about the world when it is only a fact about a language. The ladder itself is neutral; only the
  user of the ladder can reify.
- The **G2 caution** protects against *map–territory slippage*: an exact identity (a map) is
  converted into a statement about a physical system (a territory) without the controlled passage
  that makes the difference checkable.

## 2. The two hard gates

### G1 — ONTIC-GATE

**Statement.** Distinction (D0) — and every level built on it — is a **methodological primitive by
default**. No reification to ontic status is permitted unless the commitment is made **explicitly,
per claim, with justification**, and it must then carry its own disconfirmation burden.

**Fields.** Every claim row carries `ontic_commitment ∈ {methodological, heuristic, ontic}`.

| Commitment | Meaning | Burden |
|:-----------|:--------|:-------|
| `methodological` (default) | The distinction language is used as a construction tool; nothing is asserted about the world's furniture. | none beyond level discipline |
| `heuristic` | The structure is treated as a useful picture with no truth claim. | must state what, if anything, would demote it |
| `ontic` | The claim asserts the distinction structure is real — a fact about the world. | full G2 requirements **and** an explicit statement of what observation would show it false |

**Rule.** If `ontic_commitment = ontic`, the claim is treated as a D8 claim regardless of its
ladder level, and G2 applies in full. An ontic declaration cannot be retrofitted after a null result;
it must be pre-registered with the claim.

### G2 — REALIZATION-GATE (map–territory)

**Statement.** A mathematical isomorphism — however exact — **is a map, not a territory**. It may
not pass as a physical realization without three pre-specified items:

1. **Measurement protocol** — which observable, on which system, with which instrument, at which
   resolution. "What would be measured."
2. **Null model** — what the data would look like if the structure were absent. Matched-density
   nulls (RES.030) are the canonical pattern for spectral claims.
3. **Falsification condition** — the pre-registered observation that would refute the claim.
   Without it the claim is unfalsifiable and is demoted to formal status.

Plus the inherited reporting rules (RES.030 Ch. 9–11 discipline):

- **Effect sizes, not only p-values.** A 34σ hard-core exclusion can be enormous while carrying
  little information about the rest of the spectrum; the reportable object is the full discriminator
  curve.
- **Unfolding sensitivity.** Recompute under ≥ 3 unfoldings; a signature that appears under one
  unfolding only is not a signature.
- **Pre-registered multiple-comparison correction.** Pair correlation, form factor, and number
  variance derive from one two-point function; they are not independent confirmations.
- **Object separation.** Never report a prime-gap result as a zeros result, and conversely.

### 2.1 Leakage table (generalized from the seed treatise)

| Statement | Allowed? | Why |
|:----------|:--------:|:----|
| "This object is a D*X* structure." (level assignment) | Yes | Declarative; level discipline is the framework's business |
| "ζ(β) equals the unrestricted primon partition function." | Yes | Exact D2/D3 identity (map) |
| "Physical spectra may carry arithmetic corrections beyond GUE." | Yes | D7/D8 hypothesis with nulls (UMP.014 H1) |
| "The universe is made of primes." | No | D2→D8 leak; no protocol, no null, no falsifier |
| "Bosons/fermions are defined by zeta functions." | No | Confuses toy-model counting (D2) with exchange symmetry (D8) |
| "The p-adic place is a physical temperature." | No | Separate, additional commitment; requires its own protocol + falsifier (seed treatise Ch. 16) |
| "Distinction is what the world is made of." | Only with G1+G2 | Ontic commitment must be explicit, pre-registered, and falsifiable |

## 3. Claim taxonomy (reusable schema)

Every pre-registered claim row, red-team finding, and publication-gate item may carry this schema.
It is compatible with the RES.030 disconfirmation-matrix pattern: each matrix row is a claim row
with the fields below added.

| Field | Values | Mandatory iff |
|:------|:-------|:--------------|
| `level` | D0–D8 or span `DX–DY` (or `meta` for claims about the scaffold itself; `meta` claims still carry the remaining fields) | always |
| `carrier` | `definitional` \| `formal` \| `computational` \| `empirical` \| `engineered` | always |
| `ontic_commitment` | `methodological` (default) \| `heuristic` \| `ontic` | always (defaults to methodological) |
| `map_territory` | `map` \| `bridge` \| `territory` | always |
| `protocol` | free text: observable / system / instrument / resolution | `map_territory ∈ {bridge, territory}` or `ontic` |
| `null_model` | free text: what absent-structure data look like | `map_territory ∈ {bridge, territory}` or `ontic` |
| `falsifier` | free text: pre-registered disconfirming observation | `map_territory ∈ {bridge, territory}` or `ontic` |
| `reporting_rule` | free text: effect-size / unfolding / multiplicity commitments | recommended for all `computational`+ carriers |

**Consistency checks (mechanical):**

- `map_territory ∈ {bridge, territory}` missing any mandatory item (`protocol`, `null_model`, `falsifier`) → **G2 violation**: demote one rung per missing item (`territory` → `bridge` → `map`, floor `map`).
- `ontic` without G2 triple → **G1 violation**, demote to `heuristic`; dual G1+G2 violations → both demotions apply, the lower status wins (P5 ver-suite refinement of P0.1 R9; the two-items-missing gap was surfaced by artifacts/verification/verify-framework.py exhaustion).
- `level` absent → **R1 violation**, claim unregistered.

## 6. Open problems and decision points

1. **The ontic question for D0.** Under what condition, if ever, should D0 be declared ontic?
   Candidates in the portfolio: the ODR thesis ("Compton count as the only primitive") and
   RES.021's finite-distinction program. The DPRF does not answer this; it only requires that the
   answer, when given, be explicit and pre-registered.
2. **Gentile statistics.** Whether bounded-occupation statistics (D2) form a distinct third class
   or reduce to mixtures of Bose/Fermi — a D2→D4 classification question (RES.028 territory).
3. **The status of D6.** Whether "information = distinction made operational" is a definition or
   an empirical claim. Treated as definitional here; an empirical reading would require G2.
4. **The 2028 decision matrix.** UMP.014's H1 four-outcome matrix is the framework's worked
   example of a pre-registered disconfirmation criterion; it stays with UMP.014/RES.031.
5. **Level-spanning claims.** Some claims legitimately span levels (e.g., an engineered system
   realizing H = ln n is D2/D7/D8 at once). The span form `DX–DY` with a declared bridge list is
   the current answer; whether spans need their own gate is open.
