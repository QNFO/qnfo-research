- **Instrument:** The Universal Ignorance Audit: A Fifteen-Question Method (10.5281/zenodo.21901984; concept 10.5281/zenodo.21878942, v0.4 = 10.5281/zenodo.22158133); synthesis 10.5281/zenodo.21901983
- **Target (X), stated explicitly:** QNFO.RES.032 "Distinction-Primitive Research Framework" v0.1 (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22159888) core claims as locked — F1 (the nine-level ladder covers the published lineage without remainder; every published object assigns to at least one level or declared span), F2 (the two boundary rules adjudicate every defect already adjudicated in the lineage), F3 (the claim record attaches to any program's pre-registration without changing its WBS structure) — together with the two provisional level assignments the published paper names (RES.029 at the measurement level; the RES.021 span).

## Phase 1 — Surface the Structure

**Q1 (Scaffold detection).** Hidden load-bearing premises:
1. *The ladder is exhaustive for the QNFO lineage.* This is F1 itself — the audit target. The scaffold risk is circularity: the ladder's categories are the sieve through which coverage is judged, so "coverage" can be manufactured by generous category boundaries. The countermeasure is the evidence-file discipline: each assignment must cite a concrete published object, not a family resemblance.
2. *Distinction is a neutral primitive.* The word imports the Laws-of-Form lineage (SLB program treats quantity as broken distinction). The framework asserts methodological neutrality; the asserted neutrality is a scaffold, not a demonstration. (UIA RES.031 Q1 flagged the same move at L0.)
3. *Claims normalize to a single level.* The span form (DX–DY) exists precisely because some claims do not; the demotion machinery is defined for single-level states, so spans are the un-modeled region.
4. *There are exactly two boundary-rule failure modes* (ontology smuggling; map-territory slippage). A third, unmodeled failure mode would falsify F2 by construction — F2's disconfirmation matrix pre-registers this, but the matrix cannot name what it cannot imagine.

**Q2 (Map–territory hygiene).** Where the map is mistaken for the territory:
- The ladder is a map of *claims*, not of reality. Assigning a record to a level is a meta-claim about that record; the framework's own record fields carry `level=meta` for scaffold claims — but the provisional assignments (RES.029-D7, RES.021 span) are meta-claims that were published before evidence files existed. This sweep is their adjudication.
- "Information is distinction made operational" (level 7) is stated as a definition; read empirically it becomes a territory claim with no protocol, null, or falsifier. The paper flags the open status of this reading; the audit re-flags it as the single most slippery sentence in the record.
- The cross-walk table (ladder terms ↔ disciplinary terms) is a translation map; a discipline that uses a term differently (e.g., "valuation" in finance) would be misclassified by it.

**Q3 (Wobble probe).** Where the record does not balance:
- The red-team gate that audited this record found exactly the defects the record's own rules police: a misstated ladder correspondence (H1), a citation to a nonexistent gate precedent (H2), a silent 2-vs-3 item strengthening of the seed's caution (H3), and the framework's own locked claims lacking the claim-record fields (H4). All remediated at P0.1 — but the wobble's residue is precisely the two provisional assignments: the record demonstrably drifts at the points where its own discipline is hardest to apply.
- RES.031's five-level ladder (L0–L4) and the DPRF's nine-level ladder are not aligned level-by-level; a dual-ladder regime invites the level-confusion defect class (R5) in every downstream record that cites both.

## Phase 2 — Stress-Test the Frame

**Q4 (Inversion).**
- Opposite of F1: the ladder does not cover the lineage — some published object resists every level and span. Consequence: the framework's core claim is false and the paper's §6 falsification protocol fires. This is a live possibility only the sweep can retire.
- Opposite of F2: the two rules are insufficient — a lineage defect (e.g., the leakage-table rows) resists both. Consequence: a third rule type is needed; the "two boundary rules" architecture collapses to "n boundary rules" and the elegance claim dies.
- Opposite of F3: no other program can attach the record without changing its WBS — the "reusability" claim is local to its own program. Consequence: the framework is a house style, not a research framework.
- Sharpest inversion: the framework's real function is rhetorical discipline (constraining prose), not epistemic guidance (selecting claims). Under that reading, F1–F3 are claims about a style guide. The paper's own SO-WHAT prose leans on the epistemic reading; the inversion is not refuted anywhere in the record.

**Q5 (Falsifiability).**
- A world where F1 is false: a published lineage object with no level or span — checkable now, record by record; this sweep IS that world-check.
- A world where F2 is false: a leakage-table row (or equivalent adjudicated defect) not resolvable by the two rules — checkable against the seed treatise's Ch. 14 table and the P0.1 adjudications.
- A world where F3 is false: a sibling program (UMP, INM, CFE) whose claim-sheet cannot carry the eight fields — checkable by inspection of one pre-registration per program.
- Internal falsifiability (the identities at level 3, the ultrametric triangle at level 2, the demotion totality): the deposited verification suite is the falsification instrument — 11/11 checks, exit 0, re-runnable from the deposited layout.

**Q6 (Invariant extraction).** Re-frame "distinction ladder" → "abstraction hierarchy": the nine levels survive (they are generic structure), the specific operation names are frame-bound. The two boundary rules survive any frame (they regulate claim discipline, not content). The claim record and its demotion machine survive. What vanishes under re-framing: the Laws-of-Form flavor and the "distinction" vocabulary itself — which shows the framework's invariants are its rules, not its ontology.

## Phase 3 — Multiply Perspectives

**Q7 (Radical perspectival shift).**
- *A philosopher of science:* sees the framework as operationalizing Popperian falsifiability plus Korzybski's "map is not the territory" (Science and Sanity, 1933); charge: the ninth level ("the empirical filter") smuggles a verificationist criterion into a construction ladder, and the two boundary rules are demarcation criteria wearing methodological clothing.
- *A working physicist:* asks what the framework buys beyond "state your model, your null, and your test"; the honest answer is a shared pre-registration format and mechanical auditability — bookkeeping with teeth, not new physics. The framework must keep saying this (its §0 does).
- *A metrologist:* reads levels 8–9 as their entire discipline compressed into two rows; charge: resolution, noise budgets, and calibration deserve their own internal structure, and compressing them flattens the distinction the framework elsewhere insists on (finite resolution vs. ideal valuation).

**Q8 (Externalized ignorance).** Who knows more, and what would they call our biggest blind spot?
- *Prior-art holders:* Korzybski (map-territory, 1933); agnotology (Proctor–Schiebinger); ontic structural realism (Ladyman–Ross); levels-of-reality literature (Hartmann; emergentism debates); the primon-gas literature (Julia 1990; Spector 1990; Bakas–Bowick 1991; Bost–Connes 1995). The blind spot they would name: presenting either the map-territory rule or the primon-gas reading as novel when both are established — the DPRF's novelty is the *operationalization* (claim record + mechanical demotion + corpus integration), and the paper must keep the attribution explicit. External verification executed below with evidence files.
- *The lineage's own red team:* their recurring charge has been the L2→L4 slide; the DPRF's own risk is re-importing it at the meta level (ladder-as-reality).

## Phase 4 — Uncover the Hidden Forces

**Q9 (Power analysis).** Who benefits from the current framing: the QNFO program (one discipline across its records), reviewers and auditors (a cheap, mechanical audit surface), and the record's author (a single vocabulary for a decade of work). Silenced or excluded: records whose content does not fit the ladder's grain — engineering or empirical records with no formal-math spine are the most likely to be misfiled or to file themselves apologetically. Harmed: none identified beyond misclassification risk, which the evidence discipline exists to bound.

**Q10 (Protected ignorance probe).** The most dangerous question: *Is the framework itself the failure mode it polices — a map (of claims) that its own program may one day mistake for the territory (an account of how science must work)?* The protected zone is the claim "the framework makes no empirical claims": it is load-bearing for the whole record, and it is sustainable only as long as the ladder is treated as tooling. The dogfood hazard is real — the framework's own red team already caught the record failing its own rules (H4).

**Q11 (Somatic and tacit dimension).** The felt signal: nine levels and two rules have an elegance that invites symmetry-love — the temptation to smooth ragged assignments (the two provisional rows) by re-labeling rather than by evidence. The discomfort: those two rows are the only ragged edges in an otherwise neat record, and a sweep motivated by tidiness rather than by adjudication would corrupt exactly the discipline the record sells.

## Phase 5 — Act

**Q12 (Willful ignorance).** Already known but understated: the two provisional assignments were known-weak at publication time (flagged, honestly, but weak); and the F1 sweep was displaced from its pre-registered position (plan milestone M1) by the publication fast-track. Both facts are now on the record in this audit.

**Q13 (Actionable ignorance).** Without resolving whether the ladder is right: execute the sweep, publish the assignment table with an evidence file per record, adjudicate the two provisional rows on evidence, and keep the open meta-questions (Q4, Q7, Q10) explicitly open. Every downstream record can use the framework today regardless of the open questions.

**Q14 (Relational ignorance).** What the unknown wants: the provisional assignments want evidence files, not argument; the misaligned RES.031 ladder wants a stated correspondence table, not silence; the open questions want names and owners, not footnotes.

**Q15 (Recursive meta-question).** What is not being asked: whether the sweep's outcome, once executed, obligates a substantive v0.2 of the published record (SUBSTANTIVE-UPDATE-MANDATE-1) — the paper's own §6 ties its falsifiability to exactly this sweep, so the sweep is not a registry chore; it is the instrument the paper promised. Answer candidate: yes — the assignment table with evidence files and the adjudications are a substantive update, and the record's next version should carry them.
