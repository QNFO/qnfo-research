## Phase 1-4: The Fifteen Questions

### Q1. Hidden assumptions and scaffolds
Load-bearing, unstated scaffolds: (i) commensurability — that one translation
grammar exists between number-theoretic valuations, logical distinctions, and
physical measurement; (ii) that "consilience" is the natural state and
fragmentation the anomaly (a metaphysical preference, not a finding); (iii) that
the keyword taxonomy itself samples the programs faithfully — it was built for
GitHub search, so it over-weights searchable, tool-adjacent vocabulary and may
under-weight what the programs actually do; (iv) that "measurement" across UMP,
SLB, INM refers to one operation rather than several different operations sharing
a name.

### Q2. Map-territory hygiene
The map is the p-adic tree + product formula; the territory is what measurement
apparatuses do. Two slips risk occurring: (i) treating the encoding schema
(hash-then-valuation) as the event itself — a voltmeter reading must first be
digitized and hashed before it has a p-adic norm, and that hash is a chosen
convention, not physics; (ii) treating "branching" as a homomorphism between
hypergraph rewriting (Ruliad) and restricted products (adeles) when only an
analogy has been demonstrated. The locked claim avoids the strongest form of this
slip by demoting the identification to a bridge (L2, named input), but H1's
benchmarks inherit the encoding-choice problem: the result depends on the hash
and the prime, which must be pre-registered.

### Q3. The wobble
Time. The tree is static; H2 needs a dynamics (an averaging or renormalization
flow) to produce Archimedean physics, and no specific flow is specified. H3 needs
a decoherence timescale; Landauer-type bounds need thermodynamic time. The claim
holds geometry, bounds, and statics but names no equation of motion. The wobble
is the halting condition of the observer loop: nothing in the claim says when a
self-referential measurement process stops, so the framework can absorb any
negative result as "not yet the right p-adic map."

### Q4. Inversion
What if measurement is fundamentally Archimedean and ultrametricity is a lossy
compression artifact of finite observers with bounded memory? Then hierarchical
clustering in data is a shadow of our own bandwidth limits, H1's benchmarks
succeed or fail on engineering grounds alone, and H2/H3 have no physical content.
The inverted world is observationally close to this one — which is precisely why
H1's benchmark design (pre-registered corpora, metrics, and baselines) is the
load-bearing test: it is the only part of the claim that survives the inversion.

### Q5. Falsifiability test (world where this is false)
A world where the strong form is false looks like business-as-usual: Euclidean
retrieval keeps winning; LLM embeddings show no p-adic clustering; biological
coherence decays per Markovian noise; L-functions stay in number theory. The
danger: negative results are absorbable ("wrong prime, wrong hash"), so the
claim's falsifiability rests on the 2028 deadline for the strong form and on
pre-registering the two corpora + metrics + primes for H1 before running them.

### Q6. Invariant extraction
Change the base: replace Q_p with formal Laurent series over finite fields, or
tropical semirings, or plain nested partitions without numbers. What remains:
the ultrametric inequality and the strict hierarchy of nested balls — i.e.,
hierarchical partition logic. The prime-specific arithmetic is accidental; the
hierarchy is essential. The locked claim's invariant core is therefore
"measurement data organizes into nested partitions," and the p-adic dressing is
one realization. H1 should be benchmarked against non-numeric hierarchical
baselines too, not only cosine/HNSW.

### Q7. Perspectival shift
A purely continuous observer (infinite bandwidth, Fourier-native) would see the
framework as an artifact of finite resolution: trees exist because we discretize.
A quorum-sensing bacterial colony has no observers or theories at all. Both
perspectives locate the claim's contingency: it describes the resolution
structure of human, symbol-using, finite-memory observers. The claim should
therefore be stated as a theory of the observer's resolution hierarchy, not of
the substrate — which the locked claim does for H1 but not yet cleanly for H2/H3.

### Q8. Power analysis
Who benefits: niche p-adic/category-theory mathematics gains relevance; the
platform layer (PLT/DEM) gains a theoretical umbrella; the program gains a
unified narrative. Who is excluded: experimentalists who cannot extract a
falsifiable setup from H2; epistemologies that do not run on formal hierarchical
distinctions; and the gradient, fuzzy qualities of actual data that the
ultrametric ball partitions into crisper boxes than the data warrants. The
practitioner section of the plan (Deliverables 1-3) is the mitigation: it hands
the excluded parties concrete instruments.

### Q9. Most dangerous question
Is this a sophisticated exercise in imposing a beautiful, self-consistent, but
ultimately untestable meta-structure onto a research portfolio — where the
rigor of the mathematics masks the absence of a single new coupling constant or
prediction? If the honest answer is yes, the correct move is not to abandon the
work but to re-scope it to exactly what it can carry: H1's engineering benchmark
and the consilience map (Deliverables 1 and 3), with H2/H3 explicitly labeled
exploratory. The audit does not answer this question; it records it as the
standing threat.

### Q10. Somatic/tacit dimension
The confusion presents as the tension between the crispness of the tree and the
flow of time — the body's equivalent of trying to hold a fixed point while
zooming a fractal. What the feeling knows: the value of this program is in the
act of modeling (making distinctions visible, building bridges), not in the
final model. The plan's deliverable structure (tools + benchmark + map) honors
that; the metaphysics does not need to resolve for the deliverables to stand.

### Q11. Willful ignorance
What is already known but unadmitted: the platform/retrieval layer (Deliverable
1) is independently valuable and does not require any of H1-H3 to be true; and
conversely, H2 has no current computational or experimental implementation path
committed anywhere in the plan. Pretending the infrastructure proves the
ontology — or that the ontology justifies the infrastructure — is the failure
mode. The locked claim's separation of L2 (bridge) from L3-L5 (hypotheses) is
the antidote and must survive editing.

### Q12. Relational ignorance
What the unknown wants: to be measured, not mapped. The next concrete act of
listening is RQ1/RQ2 — run the retrieval benchmark on two pinned corpora with
pre-registered primes and metrics, and let the data decide. The unknown does not
care about the philosophy; it cares about the empirical residue. Phase 1 due
diligence should therefore prioritize finding the corpora and the strongest
existing baselines (Murtagh's ultrametric data science; ultrametricity in
spin-glass and clustering literature).

### Q13. Actionable ignorance (what can be done now without resolving it)
(i) Write the p-adic fingerprint index and benchmark it against cosine/HNSW on
two public corpora; (ii) run RQ5's load-bearing keyword analysis over the
taxonomy (count program-overlap per keyword) — this is pure text processing,
runnable today; (iii) state H3's measurement protocol in engineering terms so an
experimentalist can cost it; (iv) keep H2 as a named open derivation target with
the averaging operation explicitly required, not hand-waved. Uncertainty is the
fuel for these; none of them requires the ontology to settle.

### Q14. The gift in the not-knowing
If the exact isomorphism were known, the interdisciplinary conversation would
terminate. The not-knowing keeps the algebraic geometer, the quantum biologist,
the retrieval engineer, and the logician in one room; that conversation is the
consilience the keyword taxonomy was built to serve. The gift would be lost by
a completed theory — the plan should not be allowed to substitute a finished
narrative for the open conversation.

### Q15. Recursive meta-question (seed of the next pass)
Why does the program insist on ONE silent radix — a single unified foundation —
rather than a pluralism of incommensurable measurement grammars with
translation but no reduction? The taxonomy itself contains the counter-evidence:
PLT's stack keywords (IPFS, MCP, HNSW) and UMP's (Berkovich, Lubin-Tate) do not
obviously share one grammar. The next audit pass should take this question as
its target: is the single-radix thesis load-bearing for the deliverables, or is
it a removable aesthetic?

## Phase 5: Resolution deferred

No resolution attempted. The audit's products are: (i) the standing dangerous
question (Q9) to be re-asked at every phase gate; (ii) the pre-registration
requirements for H1 (corpora, metrics, primes, baselines); (iii) the plural-radix
seed (Q15) for the next pass. These are folded into Phase 1 due diligence as
explicit checks, not silently absorbed.
