## Phase 1-4: The Fifteen Questions

### Q1. Hidden assumptions and scaffolds
Load-bearing, unstated scaffolds: (i) commensurability — that one
translation grammar exists between measurement hierarchies, logical
distinctions, and number-theoretic valuations; (ii) that "measurement" is a
single operation across UMP, SLB, INM rather than several operations sharing
a name; (iii) that the hierarchy being "the invariant" has empirical content
beyond "everything can be hierarchically clustered" (a truism of finite
data); (iv) that the program's seven domains actually share the object
rather than merely being organized under one narrative by the same
researcher; (v) that a 2028 decision point is administrable (who adjudicates,
with what evidence base, and does a null decide against the program or
against the specific hypothesis?).

### Q2. Map-territory hygiene
The map is the ultrametric tree + adelic product formula; the territory is
what measurement apparatuses do. Slips at risk: (i) treating the encoding
schema (hash-then-valuation) as the event itself — a voltmeter reading must
be digitized and hashed before it has a p-adic norm, and that hash is a
chosen convention, not physics (RES.022's RQ2 result is the empirical
instance: raw-hash p-adic prefixes do not identify consilience links better
than cosine); (ii) the paper's own title must not conflate "the program's
domains share a structure" (organizational claim) with "measurement data
share a structure" (empirical claim) — H1 is the bridge, and it is a
hypothesis.

### Q3. The wobble
Time and dynamics. H2 needs an explicit averaging or renormalization flow;
H3 needs a decoherence timescale; the corpus is rich in statics (geometry,
bounds) and poor in dynamics. The wobble is also the halting condition of
the observer loop: nothing in the claim says when a self-referential
measurement process stops, so the framework can absorb negative results as
"not yet the right map." The 2028 decision point is the anti-wobble device —
it must survive editing.

### Q4. Inversion
What if measurement is fundamentally Archimedean and ultrametricity is a
lossy compression artifact of finite observers with bounded memory? Then H1
is an engineering claim (possibly true!), H2 has no physical content, and
H3 is vacuous. The inverted world is observationally close to this one —
which is why H1's pre-registered benchmark is the load-bearing test: it is
the only part of the claim that survives the inversion, and the paper must
say so.

### Q5. Falsifiability test (world where this is false)
A world where the strong form is false looks like business-as-usual:
Euclidean retrieval keeps winning; LLM embeddings show no p-adic clustering;
decoherence follows Markovian noise; L-functions stay in number theory. The
danger: negative results are absorbable ("wrong prime, wrong hash"), so
falsifiability rests on (a) the pre-registered H1 protocol (corpora,
metrics, primes, baselines committed before measurement) and (b) the 2028
decision point. Both are already in the locked claim.

### Q6. Invariant extraction
Change the base: replace Q_p with formal Laurent series, tropical
semirings, or plain nested partitions without numbers. What remains: the
ultrametric inequality and the strict hierarchy of nested balls — i.e.,
hierarchical partition logic. The prime-specific arithmetic is accidental;
the hierarchy is essential. This is the program's strongest claim and the
paper must state it as the invariant, with the p-adic dressing as one
realization (the locked claim already does this).

### Q7. Perspectival shift
A purely continuous observer (infinite bandwidth, Fourier-native) would see
the framework as an artifact of finite resolution: trees exist because we
discretize. The claim therefore describes the resolution structure of
human, symbol-using, finite-memory observers — a theory of the observer's
resolution hierarchy, not of the substrate. The P8 red team identified this
as the correct broad statement of the program; the RES.023 paper should
lead with it (this is the "broader theme" the user demanded).

### Q8. Power analysis
Who benefits: niche p-adic/category-theory mathematics gains relevance; the
platform layer gains a theoretical umbrella; the program gains a unified
narrative. Who is excluded: experimentalists who cannot extract a
falsifiable setup from H2; epistemologies that do not run on formal
hierarchical distinctions; the fuzzy, gradient qualities of actual data
that ultrametric balls partition into crisper boxes than the data warrant.
The practitioner deliverables (index, noise protocol, map) are the
mitigation — they hand the excluded parties instruments.

### Q9. Most dangerous question
Is this a sophisticated exercise in imposing a beautiful, self-consistent,
but ultimately untestable meta-structure onto a research portfolio — where
the rigor of the mathematics masks the absence of a single new coupling
constant or prediction? If the honest answer is yes, the correct move is to
re-scope to exactly what it can carry: H1's engineering benchmark and the
consilience map, with H2/H3 explicitly labeled exploratory. The audit does
not answer this question; it records it as the standing threat and notes
that the 2028 decision point is the deadline for a positive result.

### Q10. Somatic/tacit dimension
The tension between the crispness of the tree and the flow of time — the
body's equivalent of holding a fixed point while zooming a fractal. What
the feeling knows: the value of this program is in the act of modeling
(making distinctions visible, building bridges), not in the final model.
The deliverable structure (tools + benchmark + map) honors that; the
metaphysics does not need to resolve for the deliverables to stand.

### Q11. Willful ignorance
What is already known but unadmitted: the platform/retrieval layer
(Deliverable 1) is independently valuable and does not require any of
H1-H3 to be true; and conversely, H2 has no current computational or
experimental implementation path committed anywhere. Pretending the
infrastructure proves the ontology — or that the ontology justifies the
infrastructure — is the failure mode. The locked claim's separation of L2
(bridge) from L3-L5 (hypotheses) is the antidote and must survive editing.

### Q12. Relational ignorance
What the unknown wants: to be measured, not mapped. The next concrete act
of listening is H1's benchmark — run the retrieval test on the
abstract-and-embedding corpus specified by the RES.022 protocol, with
pre-registered primes and metrics, and let the data decide. The unknown
does not care about the philosophy; it cares about the empirical residue.

### Q13. Actionable ignorance (what can be done now without resolving it)
(i) Execute the H1 adjudication corpus (abstract/embedding retrieval on
public corpora — the RES.022 protocol's stated next step); (ii) state H3's
measurement protocol in engineering terms so an experimentalist can cost
it (already a deliverable); (iii) build the CFE bridge explicitly
(learning-curve/forecast keywords as a hierarchy over paradigms — the
weakest documented link from RES.022); (iv) keep H2 as a named open
derivation target with the averaging operation explicitly required.
Uncertainty is the fuel; none of these requires the ontology to settle.

### Q14. The gift in the not-knowing
If the exact isomorphism were known, the interdisciplinary conversation
would terminate. The not-knowing keeps the algebraic geometer, the quantum
biologist, the retrieval engineer, and the logician in one room; that
conversation is the consilience the program was built to serve. The gift
would be lost by a completed theory — the paper should not substitute a
finished narrative for the open conversation.

### Q15. Recursive meta-question (seed of the next pass)
Why does the program insist on ONE silent radix — a single unified
foundation — rather than a pluralism of incommensurable measurement
grammars with translation but no reduction? The taxonomy itself contains
the counter-evidence: PLT's stack keywords and UMP's Berkovich/Lubin-Tate
vocabulary do not obviously share one grammar. The paper's §9 must present
this as the program's own deepest open question (the P8 red team flagged it
as buried in the RES.022 §9 Limitations). The next audit pass should take
this question as its target: is the single-radix thesis load-bearing for
the deliverables, or a removable aesthetic?
