## §1. Charter

### §1.1 Problem Statement

QNFO.RES.009 established that the boson/fermion dichotomy is not the primitive content of the spin-statistics theorem: the relation R = e^{2πis} between exchange phase and topological spin is the invariant [ESTABLISHED], and the dichotomy is its shadow in 3+1 dimensions, where the involutive braiding quantizes s to integers and half-integers. The same paper states a concrete derivation target: a distinction-based foundation of physics — a calculus whose only primitive is the act of drawing a boundary — must, to claim the spin-statistics connection, *derive exchange statistics from the primitive* rather than importing the relation as an axiom.

The re-entrant calculus (21908818) already generates two transcendental constants as *logical scalars* — values the type theory itself computes: e as the unique solution of the fixed-point equation D f = f, f(0) = 1 (§9), and π as the trace of the identity on the circle type, π = Tr(id_{S^1}) = C/d (§10), with the half-turn of the circle as the geometric root of the Euler identity e^{iπ} = −1 (§12). The same paper explicitly marks the bridge as open: "the claim that this parity is the ancestor of physical spin-statistics is [my conjecture], developed in Part VI" — and Part VI never delivers it.

**The gap:** no published QNFO work derives R = e^{2πis} from the re-entrant calculus. This project closes the gap with one observation: the exchange phase is the (2s)-fold half-turn of the re-entrant mark.

### §1.2 Core Claim (LOCKED)

> **"The exchange phase R = e^{2πis} is the (2s)-fold half-turn of the re-entrant mark: R = (e^{iπ})^{2s} = (−1)^{2s}, so the boson/fermion dichotomy is the parity of 2s — and this invariant is generated by the same logical machinery that generates e (the fixed point of D f = f) and π (the trace of the identity on S¹)."**

**Formally:**
$$R = e^{2\pi i s} = (e^{i\pi})^{2s} = (-1)^{2s}$$
- s integer → R = +1 → Bose–Einstein statistics (symmetric exchange).
- s half-integer → R = −1 → Fermi–Dirac statistics (antisymmetric exchange).
- 3+1D involutive braiding quantizes s to {0, 1/2, 1, 3/2, …} → the dichotomy [ESTABLISHED, RES.009].
- 2+1D allows any real s → anyon phases e^{2πis} [ESTABLISHED, Leinaas–Myrheim 1977].

**Scope note on the equality chain (audit correction, 2026-08-14):** the final equality (−1)^{2s} = ±1 holds only in the quantized case 2s ∈ ℤ; for arbitrary real s the general form is R = e^{2πis} = cos 2πs + i sin 2πs (e.g., s = 1/4 gives R = i). The RES.010-specific reading is the *middle* form: R as a power of the treatise's half-turn (e^{iπ})^{2s} — the monodromy of the exchange loop as the (2s)-fold iteration of the half-turn e^{iπ} = −1 (§12.1 of 21908818). RES.009 states the textbook forms η = (−1)^{2s} = e^{2πis} [ESTABLISHED] and identifies the single sign η = −1 with the treatise half-turn (notebook T2), but never writes the composite (e^{iπ})^{2s} nor the e/π/R scalar-family reading — that is RES.010's delta (due-diligence G1, corrected).

**Falsifiability conditions:**
- **F1 (formal):** The claim that the re-entrant calculus *generates* R = e^{2πis} as a logical scalar is falsified if no derivation exists within the traced differential cohesive linear type theory of 21908818 Part VIII (§36) without importing the relation as an axiom. This is a concrete, checkable claim about a formal system. `[my conjecture]`
- **F2 (empirical, inherited from RES.009):** If a stable, local, relativistic excitation in 3+1 dimensions is observed with exchange phase η ≠ e^{2πis} (e.g., a spin-1/2 particle obeying Bose–Einstein statistics), the invariant claim is disconfirmed. No such particle is known in the Standard Model. `[established — this is RES.009's F1, restated]`
- **F3 (scope):** The arithmetic identity R = (e^{iπ})^{2s} = (−1)^{2s} is [established] elementary arithmetic; the identification of the exchange phase with the (2s)-fold half-turn in the geometric model is [MAP — model]; the claim that this identification is a *logical derivation* within a single formal system is [my conjecture], mirroring the status ladder used in 21908818 §12.2.

**Scope of the claim:** This is a *derivation-target claim* — it asserts that the re-entrant calculus contains the exchange-phase relation in its internal structure, not that new physics beyond the Standard Model is predicted. Its validation or refutation is the purpose of this research program.
