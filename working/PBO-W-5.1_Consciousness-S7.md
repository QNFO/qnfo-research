## 1. Research Objective

Formalize consciousness as **self-referential pattern closure** (designated S₇ in the PBO hierarchy). The core thesis: consciousness arises when a pattern system becomes capable of *drawing a distinction that includes itself* — a re-entry in the Spencer-Brown sense that creates a self-modeling loop.

## 2. The Re-Entry Structure

In Spencer-Brown's *Laws of Form*, re-entry is the situation where a form enters its own indicational space:

$$f = \Phi(f)$$

Applied to consciousness:

$$C = \Phi(C)$$

where $C$ is the conscious state and $\Phi$ is the distinction-drawing operation applied to the system's own state. The fixed point of this equation is the self-model.

## 4. Formal Properties of S₇

### 4.1 The Distinction That Includes Itself

For a pattern system $S$ to achieve S₇, it must be capable of drawing a distinction $D$ such that:

$$D(S) \in S$$

That is, the result of applying the distinction operation to the system *is itself part of the system*. This creates a cyclical causal structure that is the formal essence of self-awareness.

### 4.2 Fixed-Point Semantics

The conscious state $C$ is the fixed point of the recursive self-modeling operation:

$$C = \lim_{n\to\infty} \Phi^n(C_0)$$

where $C_0$ is the pre-conscious baseline state. The existence of this fixed point depends on the system's computational capacity and the structure of $\Phi$.

### 4.3 Ultrametric Self-Similarity

Conscious states form an ultrametric tree — thoughts branch hierarchically, and the strong triangle inequality governs their relationships:

$$d(A, C) \leq \max(d(A, B), d(B, C))$$

This ultrametric structure may explain the hierarchical, categorical nature of conscious experience.
