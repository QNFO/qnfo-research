### **2. Graph‑Based Ontologies: Bruhat‑Tits Trees as Geometric Manifestations**

**2.1 The Bruhat‑Tits Tree as an Ultrametric State Space**  
Recent work on p‑adic physics (Quni‑Gudzinas, 2026a) models quantum state spaces as the **Bruhat‑Tits tree** \\(T_p\\), an infinite regular graph where each vertex represents an equivalence class of p‑adic lattices and edges encode hierarchical adjacency. The tree's ultrametric geometry—satisfying the strong triangle inequality \\(|x+y|_p \\le \\max(|x|_p,|y|_p)\\)—naturally implements the **Principle of Intrinsic Coherence**: two points are either close (within the same branch) or far apart (on different branches), with no intermediate distances. This eliminates the possibility of gradual error accumulation, providing **passive geometric fault tolerance**.

**2.2 Mapping Autaxic Dynamics to Tree Dynamics**  
- **Relational Processing** corresponds to the creation and propagation of distinctions along edges of the tree; persistent "things" emerge as stabilised sub‑graphs.  
- **Spontaneous Symmetry Breaking** is realised as the branching of the tree: a symmetric parent vertex breaks into \\(p\\) distinct child vertices, generating specificity.  
- **Critical State Transitions** occur when a walker crosses a large hierarchical distance, jumping from one major branch to another—a discrete leap that mirrors a phase transition.  
- **Feedback Dynamics** (amplification/damping) are modelled by biased random walks that preferentially stabilise certain branches, analogous to the selection of stable meta‑patterns.

**2.3 Applications in Quantum Memory and AI**  
The ultrametric geometry of the Bruhat‑Tits tree has been leveraged in two concrete applications:  
- **Ultrametric Relaxation Dynamics in Topological Quantum Memory** (Quni‑Gudzinas, 2026b) shows that a Hamiltonian engineered to mimic the p‑adic metric causes the system to relax into a protected topological sector without active error correction, directly addressing the "thermodynamic wall." This passive relaxation is an instance of autaxic **Feedback Dynamics** and **Resonance** guiding the system toward a coherent, low‑entropy attractor.  
- **Ballistic Transport on the Bruhat‑Tits Tree** (Quni‑Gudzinas, 2026c) demonstrates that quantum walks on the tree exhibit ballistic transport (\\(O(D)\\) time to traverse depth \\(D\\)), enabling efficient navigation of hierarchical latent spaces. This provides a geometric foundation for **explainable AI**, where the decision path of a model is a transparent walk on the tree, realising the autaxic **Principle of Interactive Complexity Maximization** in a computationally tractable form.
