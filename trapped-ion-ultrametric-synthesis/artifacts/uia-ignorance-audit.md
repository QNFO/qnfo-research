# Universal Ignorance Audit — RES.017 Core Claim

**Target (stated explicitly):** The RES.017 core claim — "trapped-ion quantum simulators are
the first near-term platform on which ultrametric (p-adic/adelic) structure in quantum physics
becomes experimentally testable; passive, dissipation-structured hardware architectures are
the practitioner embodiment of that structure; the methodology records supply the
falsifiability discipline."

**Instrument:** The Universal Ignorance Audit (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21901984), 5 phases / 15
questions. Administration protocol followed: target stated; every question answered
(skipping forbidden, stretching mandatory); answers written down; NO resolution during
Phases 1–4; silence allowed after Q14; Q15 seeds the next pass.
**Case-study frame:** 10.5281/zenodo.21901983. **Date:** 2026-08-19. **Auditor:** parent agent.

## Phase 1 — Surface the Structure

**Q1 Scaffold detection.** What hidden assumptions hold this synthesis up?
- S1: *The three movements are ONE program.* The synthesis claims convergence; the claim
  itself is a scaffold — the sixteen records may be three coincident research lines.
- S2: *Hierarchy = ultrametric.* "Dissipative stabilization hierarchies are Bruhat–Tits
  tree structure" is asserted ("unifying architectural principle", 20411697) but never
  derived — a naming, not a mechanism.
- S3: *Platform continuity.* The 8-week protocols assume continued access to Quantinuum
  H2 / IonQ Forte-class machines (platform risk, unstated).
- S4: *Imported theorems are sound.* The Sufficient Condition Theorem and Silent Parameter
  Principle are imported without independent re-verification.

**Q2 Map–territory hygiene.** Where might the map be mistaken for the territory?
- "Local-Hamiltonian dynamics ARE p-adically ultrametric" (21820137) is a MAP claim
  (modeling choice among Ostrowski completions) phrased as territory.
- "Tensor networks accidentally instantiate the Bruhat–Tits tree" — instantiation
  language leans territory; the tree is one reading of hierarchical locality.
- "The spin-free substrate" risks reifying a design pattern into an entity.

**Q3 Wobble probe.** Where is the tension, the thing that does not fit?
- The corpus's own archive wobbles exactly where the synthesis claims convergence:
  duplicate records (20411697/20411734), near-duplicate records (21336123/21221899),
  chaotic version labels (`vv0.4`, `v0.4-title-fixed`, empty versions), a 2025-era
  concept carrying a 2026 record (21993655), FILE-SLUG-1 violations (`0.12.md`,
  `paper.md`, `_rq1_body.md`). A converged program should not have a disordered archive.
- The earliest record (GKP, 2025-12) predates the ultrametric program's explicit turn;
  retrofitting it into the synthesis is a stretch risk.

## Phase 2 — Stress-Test the Frame

**Q4 Inversion.** What if the opposite were true?
- Opposite 1: trapped-ion platforms are NOT a viable ultrametricity testbed — motional
  heating and noise floors swamp the sub-Compton-scale ZBW signal, so the "testability"
  movement collapses into the very amplification-feasibility uncertainty the vortex
  records admit (21336123/21221899 conclude "needs SNR work", not "feasible").
- Opposite 2: passive architectures are NOT ultrametric — dissipative QEC is good
  engineering in ANY metric; the tree reading is decorative. The break-even result
  (17794233) then stands on thermodynamics, not number theory.

**Q5 Falsifiability test.** What world looks like the claim is false?
- UVR ≈ 32% for BOTH diagonal and nondiagonal clock-rest coupling (no split).
- Measured ZBW frequency ratio ≈ 1 (no 2-adic √2 deviation).
- d_eff(p) ≈ 2, flat in p (ordinary diffusion; no fractal trap).
- Tensor-network advantage persists under long-range/nonlocal perturbations.
- Passive GKP stabilization never reaches break-even in any ion species.
In that world the records are interesting, disconnected engineering notes — a valid
alternative world the paper must price in.

**Q6 Invariant extraction.** What survives frame changes?
- The NUMBERS are frame-invariant: gates-per-simulated-second (6 MS gates, 96.9%),
  measured light-shift fidelities (99.6→93.7% for d=2–5), d_eff Monte-Carlo values,
  UVR 0%/32% predictions.
- The TREE INTERPRETATIONS are frame-dependent glosses on those numbers.
- Invariant core = two metric-independent claims: (i) "structured dissipation is cheaper
  than active correction at the margin"; (ii) "ultrametric structure is computationally
  testable on ion traps" — the paper's load must rest on (i)+(ii), not on the glosses.

## Phase 3 — Multiply Perspectives

**Q7 Radical perspectival shift.** How does it look to a different observer?
- An ion-trap experimentalist (Ringbauer's group): "Why spend trap time on UVR when my
  qudit gates work? Show me the SNR budget." The synthesis has none.
- An error-correction engineer: "Break-even on one mode is not a logical qubit; passive
  stabilization without a fault-tolerance threshold doesn't scale."
- A p-adic mathematician: "Any discrete hierarchical model produces similar signatures;
  the physical predictions over-specify the full adelic apparatus."

**Q8 Externalized ignorance.** Who knows what I don't, and what is my biggest blind spot?
- The tensor-network community (Schollwöck, Eisert et al.): TN success follows from
  entanglement AREA LAWS, not p-adic structure — the strongest external challenge to
  21820137's central reading. The synthesis must engage area-law explanations head-on.
- The ion-trap community: motional-heating rate estimates for the proposed protocols.

## Phase 4 — Uncover the Hidden Forces

**Q9 Power analysis.** Who benefits from the framing? Who is silenced?
- Beneficiary: the synthesis program itself — a unified narrative makes all 16 records
  citably coherent and retrofits the earlier ones (GKP, Architectonics) into a program
  they did not join.
- Silenced: the null hypothesis — "these are sixteen separate engineering notes"; and
  the area-law explanation of tensor-network success.

**Q10 Protected ignorance probe.** The most dangerous question:
- "Is the ultrametric interpretation of the quantum-computing records a reification of
  the author's own formalism — the exact failure mode the Reification record (19605446)
  diagnoses?" If yes, the methodology records indict the physics records. This question
  threatens the program's identity and must be held, not resolved, in the paper.

**Q11 Somatic and tacit dimension.** What does the unease feel like, and what does it know?
- The felt signature: satisfaction at the convergence ("it all fits a tree") coexists
  with unease at the disordered archive (duplicates, chaotic filenames). The body flags
  the archival disorder as the thing that does not belong in a convergence story — the
  disorder is evidence, not noise.
