## How to Read This Guide

This is not a standard quantum computing textbook. It starts with a question most guides avoid: **Why don't we have quantum computers yet?** It teaches the standard curriculum (qubits, entanglement, algorithms, error correction) honestly. It explains why the standard approach faces a thermodynamic wall. It introduces the geometric alternative. And it shows you how to test which paradigm is right.

Every factual claim carries a **confidence tag:** `[EST]` (established), `[PROP]` (proposed), `[GAP]` (acknowledged gap), `[SPEC]` (speculative), or `[OPEN]` (open question).
