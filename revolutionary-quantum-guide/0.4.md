## 0. Why This Document Exists

The beginner's guide blueprint (0.1.md–0.3.md) cites 48 direct source materials: 36 PDFs (the Springer textbook + arXiv papers + BT geometry papers) and 12 QWAV legacy papers. But those 48 sources are the tip of an iceberg. Underneath them lies a research program spanning at least **nine months** (September 2025–May 2026), organized across approximately **200 archived projects**, with at least **five distinct intellectual layers** and **four registered DOIs** (three newly discovered in the archive survey).

This document maps that genealogy. It serves as:

1. **A provenance record** — every claim in the blueprint can be traced backward through this genealogy to its original formulation.
2. **A research history** — showing how the argument evolved from initial critique (Nov 2025) through formal analysis (Dec 2025) to full synthesis (May 2026).
3. **A stratified bibliography** — organizing all prior work into layers that readers can navigate by interest and expertise.
4. **A DOI registry** — cataloguing all registered publications and their relationships.

## 9. How to Use This Genealogy

### For the Blueprint Reader

- **If you want the quick version:** Read Layer 4 (the QWAV papers) as primary references. They incorporate and supersede Layers 1-3.
- **If you want to understand the argument's evolution:** Read Layer 1 (the critiques) first, then Layer 2 (the formal analysis), then Layer 4 (the synthesis). This shows how the argument was built.
- **If you want the philosophical framework:** Read Layer 5 (Convergence Consilience). It explains WHY the ultrametric approach is compelling across multiple domains.
- **If you want to explore alternative physical substrates:** Read Layer 3 (Commercial Pathways). The flowermon/twistronics line is explored in detail.

### For Researchers

The genealogy provides a complete citation trail. Every claim in the blueprint can be traced:

```
Blueprint claim (0.2.md)
  → QWAV paper (Layer 4, DOI)
    → Formal analysis (Layer 2, DOI or archive doc)
      → Initial critique (Layer 1, archive doc)
```

### For Archivists

This document serves as the index to approximately 200 projects. To locate a specific document:

1. Identify the layer and approximate date from the chronological map (Section 8).
2. Navigate to the corresponding archive directory: `Archive/projects/YYYY/MM/ProjectName/`.
3. Consult the project listing in the relevant layer section for the specific filename.
